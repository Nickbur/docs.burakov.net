Finity turns your time into something you can **see and act on**. Track anything with a date as a
live countdown, map your whole life as a grid of weeks, keep a light weekly journal an AI can recap
for you, and watch an RPG-style character grow from what you actually do. It's **offline-first** and
works across phone, web and desktop, with your account carrying your data on every device.

## The idea in one minute

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add what matters">
    Anchor any date — a launch, a birthday, a deadline — and give it a repeat rule if it recurs.
    Finity draws it as a countdown ring.
  </Step>
  <Step title="See the bigger picture">
    Open Life in weeks to see your whole life at once, and pin the events and photos that made a
    week matter.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep a light habit">
    Jot a few lines in the weekly journal. Once a week — and once a year — AI can recap them for you
    on Pro.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch it add up">
    Your character levels up from real activity, and everything syncs across your devices.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Finity is offline-first and private by default — your data lives on your device and syncs only to
  your own account.
</Note>
