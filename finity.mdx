Finity turns your time into something you can **see and act on**. Track anything with a date as a
live countdown, map your whole life as a grid of weeks, keep a light journal that AI summarizes
week by week, and watch an RPG-style character grow from what you actually do. It works on your
phone, on the web and on desktop, and most of it keeps working **offline** — your account carries
your data to every device.

## The idea in one minute

<Steps>
    <Step title="Add what matters">
        Anchor any date — a launch, a birthday, a deadline — and give it a repeat rule if it recurs.
        Finity counts it down and reminds you.
    </Step>
    <Step title="See the bigger picture">
        Answer three questions and see your whole life as a grid of weeks. Mark its chapters and the
        milestones that made a week matter.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Keep a light habit">
        Write a line a day in the journal. At the end of each week, AI summarizes it for you — free on
        every plan.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Watch it add up">
        Your character levels up from the countdowns, weeks, milestones and chapters you add.
    </Step>
</Steps>

## Free and Pro

Finity is free to use, with a few limits on how many things you can create. **Pro** removes the
limits and adds email reminders, the year in review and an AI character read. See
[Pricing](/finity/pricing).
