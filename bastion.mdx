## Two audiences, one product

Bastion is built for two people:

- **Members** use the apps — chat, calls and files — on web, desktop, iOS and Android.
- **Operators** deploy and run the node, and manage the organization from a separate admin panel.

Start with the section that fits you: [Chat](/bastion/chat) and [Calls](/bastion/calls) if you're a member, [Self-hosting](/bastion/self-hosting) and [Administration](/bastion/administration) if you're running it.

## What's included

Every plan includes **encrypted chat and calls** and **high-security chats** for an unlimited number of members. Two optional modules can be added at checkout or later, without re-deploying:

- **Topics** — posts, comments, polls and reactions in public topics your whole organization can search, or private topics only their members can read.
- **AI assistant** — ask questions across the chats and topics you belong to and get answers with links to their sources. It runs inside your own deployment, so your content never goes to an outside AI service unless your admin chooses one.

Chat is always on; your admin can switch calls, high-security chats and either module off for the organization. The apps speak **English, Russian, German, French, Spanish, Italian and Dutch**.
