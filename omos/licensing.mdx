OMOS is proprietary software, but proprietary doesn't mean paid. The compiled system is free to download and use for almost everyone — a royalty is owed only by large commercial users.

## What's free

You can install and use OMOS at no charge, with no per-machine fee and nothing to buy, for:

- **Personal** use.
- **Education and academic** use.
- **Non-profit** use.
- **Commercial use** by any individual or company with **under $1,000,000** in annual revenue.

That covers essentially everyone building, learning, tinkering, or running a smaller business.

## When a royalty applies

If your annual revenue is **$1,000,000 or more** and you're using OMOS commercially, that use needs a paid commercial license: a **5% royalty** — applied only to the portion of the relevant revenue *above* $1,000,000, not the whole amount. Personal, education and non-profit use stays free no matter your revenue.

It is **not** an auto-renewing subscription. For a commercial license at scale, get in touch at [burakov.work@gmail.com](mailto:burakov.work@gmail.com).

## About the source code

These terms cover the **compiled** operating system — the images you download and boot. The OMOS **source code is closed** and isn't licensed for use, copying, or modification. Contributions are by invitation only.
