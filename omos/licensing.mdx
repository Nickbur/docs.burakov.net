OMOS is **proprietary**. The compiled system — the installer, disk and ISO images and binaries — is licensed to you
under the **OMOS End User License Agreement (EULA)**. The source code is closed and isn't licensed at all.

## Free use

You can install and use OMOS at no charge, with no per-device fee, for:

- **Personal** use.
- **Educational and academic** use.
- Use by registered **non-profit** organizations.
- **Commercial use** by any individual or company with **under $1,000,000** in annual revenue (counting affiliates).

The licence is non-exclusive, non-transferable and revocable if the terms are breached.

## Commercial use at scale

At **$1,000,000 or more** in annual revenue, commercial use needs a paid licence:

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Royalty** | 5% of the revenue from products or services built on OMOS |
| **Applies to** | Only the part of that revenue **above $1,000,000** in the calendar year |
| **Payment** | Quarterly, within 30 days of the quarter's end |
| **Reporting** | Revenue reports sufficient to check the amount |
| **Renewal** | Not a subscription — nothing auto-renews |

Personal, educational and non-profit use stays free whatever your revenue. For a commercial licence, contact
[burakov.work@gmail.com](mailto:burakov.work@gmail.com).

## What the EULA doesn't allow

Unless you have written permission, or the law says otherwise, you may not:

- Redistribute, resell, rent or host OMOS for others, or ship it inside another OS distribution.
- Reverse engineer, decompile or disassemble it.
- Modify it or create derivative works.
- Remove copyright, trademark or licence notices.
- Use it to build a competing operating system or product.
- Circumvent licence or security protections.
