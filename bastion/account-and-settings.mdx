## Your account

Open **Account** from the menu:

- **Display name** and **avatar** (JPEG, PNG or WebP).
- **Username** — how people from other organizations can find you. You can change it **once every 30
    days**.
- **Email** — changing it is confirmed with a code sent to the new address.
- **Active sessions** — every device you're signed in on. Rename or sign out any of them, or **Sign out
    all other sessions**. Your Bastion account is shared with the other apps at burakov.net, so this
    signs those other sessions out too.

Exporting or deleting your data is under **Account settings** — see
[Privacy & your data](/bastion/accounts-and-privacy#your-data-rights).

## Settings

| Setting | Options | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Organization** | **Switch organization** | — |
| **Appearance** | Auto, Light, Dark | Auto |
| **Language** | System language, or English, Russian, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch | System language — saved with your account |
| **Notifications on this device** | Allow or block notifications here | — |
| **Lock the app with biometrics** | Off, or lock after 1, 5, 15 or 60 minutes away | Off (5 minutes once turned on) |
| **Block screenshots** *(iPhone and Android)* | On / Off — also hides the app's content in the app switcher | Off |
| **Send crash reports** | On / Off — reports carry an anonymous ID, not your identity | On |

Per-chat notification levels — every message, mentions only, or never — are set in each chat's info
page. See [Chat](/bastion/chat#notifications).

## Support

Open **Support** from the menu to write to us. Pick a topic — **General**, **Bug**, **Billing** or
**Account** — and follow the conversation in the app.

## Updates

When a newer version is out, **About** shows it with an **Update** button that opens your app store. If
your version of the app is too old to keep working, Bastion shows an **Update required** screen until
you update it.
