Bastion is priced **per server, not per seat** — bring your whole organization without watching a counter.

<Note>
  Paying **annually** saves two months compared with month-to-month.
</Note>

## What's included

Every plan includes the base module — **encrypted chat and calls** — for an unlimited number of members. There's no per-seat billing and no free tier: pricing is flat per server so the cost is predictable as your team grows.

## Self-hosted vs. managed

- **Self-hosted** is for teams who want to run and own the whole stack. See [Self-hosting](/bastion/self-hosting).
- **Managed** is the same product with the operations handled for you, if you'd rather not run a server.

Either way, your content stays end-to-end encrypted and, on your own node, on infrastructure you control.
