## What's included

Every plan includes the base module — **encrypted chat and calls, including high-security chats** — for an unlimited number of members. There's no per-seat billing and no free trial: pricing is flat per server so the cost is predictable as your team grows.

## Buying

Plans are bought on [bastion.burakov.net](https://bastion.burakov.net/pricing): sign in with your email (a one-time code, no password), pick the plan, billing period and modules, name your organization and pay. Payment is processed by **Paddle**, our Merchant of Record, which also issues your invoices.

Your organization is created the moment the payment clears, owned by the email you bought with. **[Your organizations](https://bastion.burakov.net/account/)** is where you then:

- get the self-hosted node's **credentials** (organization ID and secret — the secret is shown once) and **rotate** the secret;
- see the **license** and server status — last check-in, version, address — and, for managed, the deployment status;
- **manage the subscription** — payment method, invoices and cancellation, through Paddle's customer portal;
- **change modules** — the price difference is prorated and charged or credited right away;
- **renew** an organization whose subscription ended, keeping its ID, secret and data.

## When a payment fails or a subscription ends

- A failed renewal keeps everything working for **7 days** while the payment is retried — update the payment method from your organization page.
- Cancelling stops the next renewal; the paid period runs to its end.
- After that the server turns **read-only** (members can sign in and read), and 14 days later it stops admitting sign-ins.
- **Nothing is deleted.** Renewing restores full use.

Refunds within 14 days of purchase — see the [Refund Policy](https://bastion.burakov.net/refund/).

## Self-hosted vs. managed

- **Self-hosted** is for teams who want to run and own the whole stack. See [Self-hosting](/bastion/self-hosting).
- **Managed** is the same product with the operations handled for you, if you'd rather not run a server.

Either way, your content stays end-to-end encrypted and, on your own node, on infrastructure you control.
