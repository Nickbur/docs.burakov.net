Every plan includes **encrypted chat and calls, including high-security chats**, for an unlimited number of members. Paying **yearly** costs ten months' price. Prices are in US dollars; sales tax or VAT is added where it applies. There's no free trial.

## Buy

On [bastion-chat.com](https://bastion-chat.com/pricing): sign in with your email (a one-time code), pick the plan, billing period and modules, name your organization and pay. Payment is processed by **Paddle**, our Merchant of Record, which also issues your invoices. Your organization is created the moment the payment clears, and **you are its owner**.

### If you chose Managed

Your dedicated instance is deployed automatically as soon as the payment clears — usually within minutes, always within one business day. You get an email when it's live, and **[Your organizations](https://bastion-chat.com/account/)** shows its status, its address and the link to its admin panel. Then sign in to the Bastion app with the same email and pick your organization, and [invite your team](/bastion/administration) from the admin panel.

### If you chose Self-hosted

Download the node bundle and get your organization's credentials on **Your organizations** (below), then follow [Install a self-hosted node](/bastion/self-hosting).

## Your organizations

**[Your organizations](https://bastion-chat.com/account/)** is where the owner:

- **Downloads the node bundle** for a self-hosted node — the Docker Compose setup, its configuration templates and scripts. See [Install a self-hosted node](/bastion/self-hosting).
- **Gets credentials** for a self-hosted node — the organization ID and secret, with a ready `.env` snippet. The secret is shown **once** and never emailed; store it right away. (Managed organizations don't need credentials.)
- **Rotates the secret** — a new secret, with the old one stopping at once. Credentials can be fetched or rotated up to 10 times an hour.
- Sees the **license** and server status — last check-in, version and address — and, for managed, the deployment status and the link to the admin panel.
- **Manages the subscription** — payment method, invoices and cancellation — in Paddle's customer portal. The portal stays available after a subscription has ended, so past invoices are always at hand.
- **Changes modules** — the price difference is prorated and charged or credited right away; the license reaches the node at its next refresh. (The AI assistant also needs a model configured on the node — see [Run & maintain your node](/bastion/operations#ai-assistant-models).)
- **Renews** an organization whose subscription ended, keeping its ID, secret and data.

## When a payment fails or a subscription ends

| Situation | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| A renewal payment fails | Everything keeps working for **7 days** while the payment is retried. Update the payment method under **Manage subscription**; you get an email once the payment goes through. |
| You cancel | The paid period runs to its end. |
| The paid period (or the 7 days) has ended | The organization turns **read-only**: members can sign in and read, nothing new can be sent. |
| 14 days after that | It stops admitting sign-ins. A managed instance is powered down with all its data kept; renewing brings it back. |
| Any time | **Nothing is deleted.** Renewing restores full use, with the same organization, ID and secret. |

Refunds within 14 days of purchase — see the [Refund Policy](https://burakov.net/refund). A full refund of the current term, or a chargeback, cancels the subscription at once and ends the paid term then: the organization turns read-only for 14 days, so everyone can still read and export, then stops admitting sign-ins — nothing is deleted, and subscribing again restores full use. A partial refund doesn't change the subscription.

## Self-hosted or managed?

- **Self-hosted** is for teams who want to run and own the whole stack — see [Install a self-hosted node](/bastion/self-hosting).
- **Managed** is the same product with the operations handled for you.

Either way, your content stays end-to-end encrypted on your organization's own server.
