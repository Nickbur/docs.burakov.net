For organizations whose IT manages company devices, Bastion fits into your **mobile device management (MDM)** and lets you set **device policies** for everyone — and wipe a device that's lost or no longer trusted.

Device management is part of the **Enterprise controls** module, together with [single sign-on and provisioning](/bastion/sso) — see [Plans & buying](/bastion/pricing).

## Managed app configuration

Bastion's iPhone and Android apps read **managed app configuration** — the standard way an MDM such as Microsoft Intune, Jamf or Workspace ONE configures an app on managed devices (AppConfig on iOS, managed configurations on Android Enterprise). From your MDM you can preset:

- your **organization** and its server, so people don't have to find it;
- the **sign-in domain**, and whether sign-in must go through your [single sign-on](/bastion/sso);
- **this organization only** — no other Bastion accounts on a managed device;
- which **features** are available.

Values set by your MDM override the person's own settings. The admin panel lists the configuration keys.

## Device policies

In the admin panel, **Device policies** apply to every member, on every device running the Bastion apps:

| Policy | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Require app lock** | Members must lock the app with biometrics or the device passcode; you set the longest allowed delay before it locks. |
| **Block screenshots** | Screenshots and screen recordings of Bastion are blocked on phones. |
| **Keep data in the app** | No copying text out of Bastion, no sharing or opening files in other apps, no saving to the photo gallery, no downloading files on desktop or web, no printing. |
| **Offline data limit** | Devices keep at most a set number of days of messages offline. |
| **Wipe after no contact** | A device that hasn't reached your server for a set number of days erases Bastion's data and keys the next time it's opened. Off by default. |
| **Minimum app version** | Older versions must update before they can be used. |
| **No web access** | Members can use only the desktop and mobile apps — the browser version is closed for your organization, because a browser can't enforce these policies. Off by default. |

<Warning>
    **Wiping removes the encryption key too.** After a wipe — remote or after no contact — a person needs their own [key backup](/bastion/security#your-key-your-backup) to bring their history back when they sign in again. Make sure your people keep one.
</Warning>

<Note>
    Policies are enforced by Bastion's official apps. A modified app could ignore them, which is why they matter most on devices your MDM manages.
</Note>

## Devices and remote wipe

In the admin panel, each member's page lists their **devices and sessions**. **Sign out and wipe** ends that device's session and erases Bastion's data and keys on it — at once if the device is online, otherwise the moment it next connects, however long it was away. Either way its high-security credential is revoked on the spot, so the device drops out of every high-security chat even while it's offline.

Members can do this for their own lost phone too, from **Active sessions** in [Account & settings](/bastion/account-and-settings) on any other device — that's part of every plan.
