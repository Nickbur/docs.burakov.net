Every chat in Bastion is **end-to-end encrypted**: messages are sealed on your device and only opened on the recipients'. The server relays ciphertext — the one exception is a chat your organization's [AI assistant](/bastion/ai-assistant) has been added to, which the assistant can read on your organization's own server. See [Security](/bastion/security) for how that works.

## Kinds of chat

<CardGroup cols={2}>
    <Card title="Direct" icon="user">
        A private conversation between exactly two people. Starting one with someone you already have a direct chat with simply reopens it.
    </Card>
    <Card title="Group" icon="users">
        A named chat for several people. Members you add later receive the shared history.
    </Card>
    <Card title="Channel" icon="bullhorn">
        A persistent room. Everyone can post by default; its admins can limit top-level posts to admins (announcements) — members can still reply in threads.
    </Card>
    <Card title="High-security" icon="shield-halved">
        Chats with forward secrecy and per-device certificates, for your most sensitive conversations — see [High-security chats](#high-security-chats).
    </Card>
</CardGroup>

Open **New chat**, pick the kind, choose people from your organization's directory and start typing. A new chat can start with up to 500 people. Someone who has never opened Bastion yet has no encryption key, so they can't be added until they do — you'll see *Not added — they haven't opened Bastion yet*.

Chats you archive move to an **Archived** group at the bottom of the chat list.

## Writing messages

- **Formatting:** messages support Markdown — bold, lists, links, quotes and code blocks with syntax highlighting. Links open in your browser, and images from the web are never loaded automatically, so a link can't be used to track you.
- **Mentions:** type `@` to pick a member (up to 50 in a message). They're highlighted and notified even if they only want notifications for mentions.
- **Drafts** are saved encrypted and follow you to your other devices.
- **Polls** (**Create poll**): a question with 2–10 options — single or multiple choice, optionally with anonymous votes, and an optional deadline at least five minutes ahead. The author or a moderator can close a poll early.
- **Voice messages** of up to five minutes.
- **Files, photos and videos** (**Photo or video**, **Take a photo**, **Attach file**): encrypted on your device before they leave, with a thumbnail and upload progress — up to 100 MB each unless your organization set another limit. Videos are compressed on your device before they're sent: choose **Standard** or **HD**, or **Send as file** to keep the original. Photos and videos are sent without their location and camera details — your device removes them before encryption; only **Send as file** keeps the original file as it is, details included. Uploads run in the background while you keep chatting, continue while the app is in the background, and resume where they stopped after a dropped connection or a restart — nothing restarts from scratch. Images open full screen with zoom, videos play inline.

### Albums

Pick several photos and videos at once and send them as **one message** with a single caption. The message shows as a grid; open it and swipe between the items. Each item is encrypted on your device like any other attachment, uploads in parallel, and retries on its own if the connection drops. Deleting the message removes every item in it.

### Photo and video editor

After you pick or take a photo or video — or from the attachment preview — you can edit it before it leaves your device.

**Photos:**

- **Crop, rotate and straighten.**
- **Solid box**, with a brush or a rectangle — cover text such as names, numbers or addresses on a screenshot before you share it. Always use it for text: blurred or pixelated text can sometimes be reconstructed.
- **Blur and pixelate**, with a brush or a rectangle — for faces and backgrounds.
- **Draw:** pen, highlighter, arrows and shapes, plus text, with undo.

**Videos:**

- **Trim** the start and end — cut out the part you don't want to share, and send a smaller file.
- **Remove the sound** — drop background voices before sending.

Editing happens entirely on your device and works offline. An edited photo is saved as a new, flattened image. If your browser can't process a particular video, it's sent as it is.

### Link previews

When your message contains a link, Bastion shows a card with the page's title, description, site and a small image. **Your device** fetches the page and builds the card, and you can remove it before sending. The card travels inside the encrypted message, so the people reading it load nothing from the web and your organization's server never sees the address. Your organization's admin can switch previews off — see [Organization settings](/bastion/organization-settings#link-previews).

### View once

Mark a photo, video or text message **View once** before sending. Each recipient can open it a single time; after that it is gone on all of that recipient's devices. Choose how long an unopened item stays available — if nobody opens it in time, it expires. You see who has opened it (unless your organization uses [metadata-minimal mode](/bastion/accounts-and-privacy#metadata-minimal-mode)).

Once every recipient has opened it, or it expires, the encrypted item is deleted from your organization's server. The viewer is a dedicated screen: view-once items can't be forwarded, saved, copied, downloaded, shared or quoted, they are not kept in the offline cache or in search, and the [AI assistant](/bastion/ai-assistant) never indexes them. On iPhone and Android the viewer also blocks screenshots and screen recording while it is open.

<Note>
    View once limits copies; it can't prevent them. Screenshot blocking works only in the iPhone and Android apps, and nothing stops someone from photographing the screen. Use it for passwords, contracts and personal data you'd rather not leave lying around — not as a guarantee against a recipient who means to keep a copy.
</Note>

### Share location

Send your current position as a message: coordinates, accuracy and an optional label. It's a one-time snapshot — Bastion doesn't share live location. The message is end-to-end encrypted like any other, and your device asks for location permission the first time.

The bubble has an **Open in maps** button: Apple Maps, Google Maps or OpenStreetMap (on Android, the system chooser). Bastion shows **no embedded map and looks up no address**, because either would send the position to a third party. It only reaches a maps app when you tap.

Messages appear the moment you send them and wait in an outbox while you're offline. If the server permanently refuses one (for example, a file that's too large), it shows why and lets you discard it.

## Threads

A message with replies shows how many it has — for example **3 replies · 2 new**. Tap it to open the thread. Inside a thread, replies work like chat messages: react, save, edit or delete your own, mention people and attach files — changes show up for everyone instantly.

You **follow** a thread when you wrote the message it started from, or once you reply in it. For threads you follow:

- New replies count toward the chat's unread badge and show as **new** on the message until you open the thread.
- You get a notification for each new reply, and tapping it opens the thread directly.

Threads you don't follow stay quiet: you still see their reply count, but they never add to your unread badge or notify you.

## Notifications

For each chat, choose to be notified about **every message**, **only mentions**, or **never** — and whether notifications make a sound. The choice applies on all your devices and is respected by the server, so muted chats really stay silent. Swipe a chat in the list to mute it or mark it as a **favourite** (favourites stay at the top).

## Delivery, read status and presence

As a message travels you see its status — **sent**, then **delivered** when it reaches a recipient's device, then **seen** once read — and a typing indicator shows when someone's replying. Direct chats show whether the other person is online or when they were last seen; groups show how many members are online.

<Note>
    An organization can switch off typing indicators, read and delivery status and online status for maximum privacy — see [Accounts & privacy](/bastion/accounts-and-privacy).
</Note>

## Search and saved messages

Because the server only holds ciphertext, search runs **on your device**: Bastion decrypts your chats locally and keeps building a search index of their full history in the background. Search across everything from the chats list, or inside one chat from its header, and jump straight to a hit — a match in a thread reply opens its thread. **Saved messages** in the side menu collects everything you've saved.

## Managing a chat

Open a chat's **info** page from its header to:

- see members with their roles and online status, and compare **safety numbers** to confirm no one has been impersonated — see [Security](/bastion/security);
- rename the chat and edit its description;
- **add members** from your organization — the chat's key is sealed to them so they can read the shared history;
- create **invite links** — **Expires** in 1 day, 7 days (default), 30 days or never; **Uses** unlimited (default), 1, 10 or 100 — and copy, share or revoke them any time. On iPhone and Android, an invite link opens straight in the Bastion app when it's installed. Someone who joins by link can read the history once a member's app has granted them the key (*Waiting for a member to grant you access…* until then);
- **invite someone from another organization** — find them by their exact username or e-mail (or by name, if their organization is listed network-wide); they get a notification and a single-use invitation that expires after 7 days, and their organization's guest policy still applies;
- choose your notification level, browse the chat's photos, videos and files, and see its recent calls;
- add **bots** — webhook integrations that post into the chat (see [Bots](#bots));
- turn the [AI assistant](/bastion/ai-assistant) on or off for a group or channel, if you're its admin and your organization allows it;
- **archive** the chat (it becomes read-only for everyone), **leave** it, or **delete** it with its history and files.

### Roles

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| Member | Read and post, react, reply, save, forward, delete their own messages, report others' messages, leave |
| Moderator | + pin, delete others' messages, close polls, manage bots |
| Admin | + rename, add members, invite links, channel posting policy, AI assistant, archive, make members moderators, remove members |
| Owner | + any role change, transfer ownership, remove admins, delete the chat |

Both people in a direct chat can pin; a direct chat has no roles and can't be renamed, archived or extended. If the owner leaves, ownership passes to the longest-standing admin; the last person to leave deletes the chat. Your organization's moderators, admins and owner can moderate every chat they're in — see [Administration](/bastion/administration#moderation).

## Bots

Your organization's owner, admins and moderators can add a **bot** to a chat. A bot gets a secret webhook address on your organization's server; anything that sends it a request posts a message into the chat:

```bash
curl -X POST "https://<your-node>/v1/bots/hook/<token>" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"text": "Deploy finished ✅"}'
```

- `text` can be up to 4,000 characters; a bot can post up to 120 messages a minute.
- A bot has no encryption key, so its messages are stored as plain text and marked **🤖 bot · not encrypted**.
- Bots can't post in high-security chats, in archived chats, or while the organization is read-only.

## High-security chats

High-security chats add **forward secrecy and post-compromise security** on top of end-to-end encryption: keys move forward with every change, so a key stolen later can't open earlier messages, and a removed member or device is locked out of what comes next. Every device has its own short-lived certificate (valid for seven days), and Chat info shows each member's devices as verified, not trusted or waiting to be admitted.

To keep that guarantee, high-security chats are text-only (replies and mentions included), don't support threads, edits, invite links, bots or the AI assistant, and only owners and admins add members. Calls work — see [Calls](/bastion/calls). A new device of an existing member is admitted automatically by another member's app.
