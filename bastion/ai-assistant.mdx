The **AI assistant** is an optional module that answers questions from your organization's own
conversations — with numbered links to the messages and posts it used. It runs on your organization's
server, with the AI model your admin configures.

When the module is on and set up, an **Assistant** item appears in the menu.

## Ask a question

Open **Assistant** and type a question. The answer cites its **sources** — tap a number to jump to that
message or post. While it's writing you can **Stop** it; afterwards you can **Ask again**, **Copy** the
answer, or **Clear conversation**.

The assistant only answers from chats and topics where **you are a member and the assistant has been
added** — it never tells you about conversations you can't read yourself. Your conversation with the
assistant is private to you and is cleared when you sign out.

## In a chat

From a chat you can:

- **Ask about this chat** or **Summarize this chat**, and
- **Share to chat** — post the answer into the chat as a normal, encrypted message starting with
    *AI assistant answer:*.

## Which chats it can read

The assistant shows up in a chat's member list with the badge *AI — indexes this chat for org-wide
answers*.

- **Groups and channels** — your admin chooses whether the assistant joins new ones by default (it does,
    unless they changed that). A chat's admins can switch **AI assistant in this chat** off or on, unless
    your organization has locked that setting. Chats created before the assistant was set up need it
    switched on.
- **Direct chats and high-security chats** — **never**.
- **Topics** — each topic has its own switch.

It understands message text and code, and PDF, Word and Excel files. Images and voice messages only if
your organization's setup includes models for them.

## Privacy

<Warning>
    In a chat or topic the assistant has been added to, the assistant **can read the content**: it's
    decrypted on your organization's server to build the assistant's index, and passed to the AI model
    your admin configured to write answers. If that model is an external service, the app tells you so.
    Direct and high-security chats are never included.
</Warning>

Your questions are recorded in the organization's audit log only as a fingerprint and length — not
their text.

## For admins

The module needs an AI model configured on the organization's server, and the assistant's worker set up
once from the admin panel. See [Organization settings](/bastion/organization-settings#ai-assistant) and
[Run & maintain your node](/bastion/operations#ai-assistant-models).
