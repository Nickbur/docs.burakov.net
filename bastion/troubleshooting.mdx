## For members

### I don't see my organization

Ask its admin to invite the exact email you signed in with (or to add your company's email domain), then tap **Check again** on the Organizations screen. Signed in with the wrong email? Use **Use another account**.

### "Not added — they haven't opened Bastion yet"

People get their encryption key the first time they open Bastion. Until then they can't be added to chats or messaged directly. Ask them to sign in once, then try again.

### "Restore your encryption key to read your chats on this device"

This device doesn't have your key. Open **Encryption keys** and restore it from your backup file or the copy you kept on the server. No backup and no other signed-in device? Choose **Start over with a new key** — your conversations come back as other members come online. See [Security & encryption](/bastion/security#lost-your-key-start-over).

### "Waiting for a member to grant you access…"

You joined a chat (for example by invite link) or started over with a new key, and no member's app has handed you the chat's key yet. It happens automatically as soon as another member of that chat is online.

### Can't decrypt a message on this device

The message was sealed for a key this device doesn't have — usually because it arrived before you restored or replaced your key. If another member is online, access usually comes back on its own.

### The organization says "Read-only"

Its subscription is due or has ended: you can still sign in and read, but not send. It's for the organization's owner to renew.

### Calls don't connect

- *Your browser can't encrypt this call* — update the browser, or use the desktop or phone app. Bastion never connects a call without end-to-end encryption.
- On a strict network that blocks UDP or unusual ports, calls fall back to an encrypted relay over TLS. If they still don't get through, ask your admin to check the node's call ports (see [Calls fail on a self-hosted node](#calls-fail-on-a-self-hosted-node)), or try another network.
- In a high-security chat, if the call ends because the chat's key changed, simply call again.

### The desktop or web app didn't ring

Allow notifications for Bastion in your browser: when none of your phones is online, an incoming call then reaches the browser as a system notification, even with no Bastion tab open. The desktop app rings while it's running.

### A file won't upload

Files are limited to 100 MB each unless your organization changed that. Try a smaller file, or ask your admin.

## For admins

### The admin panel says "This account is not an admin of this organization."

Only the owner and admins can open the admin panel. Ask the owner to make you an admin.

### "Not in your license" next to a module

The module isn't part of your plan. Add it on [Your organizations](/bastion/pricing#your-organizations).

### The AI assistant doesn't appear for members

The module must be in your license and switched on, the node needs its AI models configured, and **Provision AI worker** must have been run. Older chats need the assistant switched on per chat. See [Run & maintain your node](/bastion/operations#ai-assistant-models).

### My self-hosted node isn't reachable for members

Check the node's log for an address-verification reason (`not_https`, `private_address`, `unreachable`…) — each is explained in [Address verification](/bastion/operations#address-verification). Members are only routed to a node the control plane could verify from the internet.

### The node is read-only right after setup or a secret rotation

The node can't confirm its license — usually because `BASTION_ORG_ID` or `BASTION_ORG_SECRET` in `.env` is missing or out of date. Fix it and restart the stack. See [Licensing states](/bastion/operations#licensing-states).

### Calls fail on a self-hosted node

Make sure `LIVEKIT_DOMAIN` resolves to the server and that UDP `7882` and `3478` and TCP `7881` and `5349` are open. Behind NAT, set the public IP as `rtc.node_ip` in `infra/livekit.yaml`. See [Calls behind NAT and firewalls](/bastion/operations#calls-behind-nat-and-firewalls).
