**Topics** is an optional module for longer-lived conversations: announcements, knowledge, discussion
threads around a subject. When your organization has it, a **Topics** tab appears in the app.

## Public and private topics

When you create a topic, choose:

| Kind | Who can read it | Encryption |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Public** | *Anyone in your org can read and search it.* | **Not end-to-end encrypted** — its text and files are stored as they are, so they can be searched on the server. |
| **Private** | Only its members (by invitation). | End-to-end encrypted. |

A topic's kind **can't be changed** after it's created.

## Browsing

The Topics tab has four views — **Feed**, **Following**, **Public** and **Mine** — plus:

- **Folders** to group the topics you follow,
- **Bookmarks** for posts and comments you saved,
- **Moments** — short-lived posts — from the topics you follow or from all public topics, and
- **Search** across public topics, and across private posts already on your device.

## Posts and comments

In a topic, **only its creator, admins and moderators publish posts**. Members read, comment, react, vote
and bookmark.

- **Posts** carry text and files, can have **tags**, show how many people viewed them, keep an **edit
    history**, and can be deleted by their author or a moderator.
- A **Moment** is a post that disappears after **48 hours**.
- **Comments** support replies, editing and deleting; moderators can **pin** a comment. Unread comments
    show as **N new**.
- **Polls** in topics have 2 to 20 options, and can optionally show who voted for what.

## Roles in a topic

| Role | Can |
| --- | --- |
| **Creator** | Everything, including deleting the topic. The creator can't leave it. |
| **Admin** | Invite people and accept or decline requests, make members admins or moderators, remove and ban members, publish the topic to the network, turn the AI assistant on or off for it. |
| **Moderator** | Publish posts, pin and delete comments and posts. |
| **Member** | Read, comment, react, vote and bookmark. |
| **Banned** | Removed from the topic by an admin and kept out of it. |

Invited people get **Accept** / **Decline** on the invitation. Your organization's owner, admins and
moderators can moderate every topic they're in.

Removing or banning someone from a **private** topic gives the topic a new key, so they can't read
anything posted after they're out.

## Publish to the network

A topic admin can **Publish to the network** a **public** topic. Any signed-in Bastion user, from any
organization, can then find and **read** it — read-only — and **Report** it to Bastion, which reviews
reports and can take a topic off the network. Only your own organization's members can take part.

## The AI assistant in topics

If your organization uses the [AI assistant](/bastion/ai-assistant), each topic has its own switch for
whether the assistant may index it.

<Note>
    Your organization's **retention** setting deletes old chat messages, but not topics — posts and
    comments stay until they're deleted.
</Note>
