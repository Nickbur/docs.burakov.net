The **admin panel** is where the organization's **owner** and **admins** manage people and policy. It's served by your organization's node: open the admin panel on your node (for Managed, its link is on **[Your organizations](https://bastion-chat.com/account/)**) and sign in with the same passwordless email code as the app. Only the owner and admins can open it. Organization-wide settings — branding, modules, guests, retention and the AI assistant — are covered in [Organization settings](/bastion/organization-settings).

The **dashboard** shows active members, conversations, the number of messages (stored encrypted), and the node's **license**: its state, how long it's valid, how long its offline grace lasts, and which modules are licensed and enabled.

## Members and roles

| Role | Can |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Everything. The person who bought the organization — the owner can't be changed, removed, or leave. |
| **Admin** | Use this panel: manage members, conversations and settings. Admins can't change other admins or the owner. |
| **Moderator** | Moderate every chat and topic of the organization from the app, and work the **Moderation** queue. No access to this panel. |
| **Member** | Use chat, calls and the organization's modules. |

Nobody can change their own role.

### Invite and manage members

- **Invite** by email, as a member, moderator or admin. The person gets an email, signs in to Bastion with that address and accepts on the **Organizations** screen — nobody joins without accepting. Until then the invitation is listed under **Pending invitations**, where you can **withdraw** it.
- The **Members** list has search and shows 50 people per page, each as **active**, **removed** or **left**. A new member appears here as soon as they accept the invitation.
- **Changing a role** is confirmed with Bastion's control plane first; if it can't be reached, nothing changes — try again.
- **Removing** a member cuts their access immediately, including any session that's open right now; **restore** them later to bring them back. Members who **left** on their own need a new invitation.

<Tip>
    Want everyone at your company to find the organization without an invitation? Add your **e-mail domains** in [Organization settings](/bastion/organization-settings#join-without-an-invitation).
</Tip>

## Moderation

Members can report a message with a reason — **Spam**, **Abusive**, **Harassment**, **Illegal** or **Other** — a note and, if they choose, the message text (otherwise moderators see no content). The **Reports** queue lives here and, for owners, admins and moderators, on the **Moderation** screen in the app.

Filter reports by **Open**, **Resolved** or **Dismissed**. From a report you can **Delete message** (for everyone), **Resolve**, **Dismiss**, or **Reopen** it later; in the app you can also jump to the message in chats you're in. Every decision is recorded in the audit log.

Your organization's owner, admins and moderators can also moderate every chat and topic they're in — delete messages, pin, close polls, and in topics remove or ban members — without being given a role in each one.

## Conversations

The **Conversations** page lists every conversation of the organization — groups, channels, direct chats (shown as *(direct)*) and high-security chats — with member and message counts. You see who's in them, never what they say.

- **New channel** — creates a channel with **you** as its owner and only member. Open it once in the Bastion app to create its encryption key (the server never holds it), then add members from the app.
- **Archive** a chat (read-only for everyone) or **unarchive** it.
- **Delete** a chat with its history and files — type its name to confirm.

Direct chats belong to their two participants and can't be archived or deleted here.

## Audit log

Administrative and security-relevant events are recorded in the **audit log**, filterable by **Members**, **Invitations**, **Conversations**, **Moderation**, **Settings**, **AI**, **Retention** and **Sign-ins** (the first time each session reaches your server). Page back through history; names are shown for people and chats. Questions asked to the AI assistant are logged only as a fingerprint and length, never their text.

## More in the panel

- **Single sign-on** and **Device policies** — with the Enterprise controls module. See [Single sign-on & provisioning](/bastion/sso) and [Device management](/bastion/device-management), where each member's devices can also be signed out and wiped.
- **Bot accounts** — create bots that read and answer in chats. See [Bots & integrations](/bastion/bots).
- **Import window** — let members and admins bring history from other tools for a limited time. See [Move to Bastion](/bastion/import).

## If the subscription lapses

When the organization turns read-only, you can still **remove and restore members**, **withdraw invitations** and change who may work with other organizations. Settings and organization changes are blocked until the subscription is renewed — see [Plans & buying](/bastion/pricing#when-a-payment-fails-or-a-subscription-ends).

<Note>
    The admin panel is **content-blind** where it counts: because messages and calls are end-to-end encrypted, administration is about people, roles and policy — not reading conversations.
</Note>
