Every Bastion node comes with a separate **admin panel** for the organization's owner and admins. It runs alongside the node and signs in with the same passwordless email flow.

## Members and roles

Invite people by email and manage who can do what:

- **Owner** — full control of the organization.
- **Admin** — manage members, chats and settings.
- **Member** — use chat and calls.

Inviting, removing and restoring members, and changing roles, all happen here. An invited person gets an email, signs in to Bastion with that address and accepts the invitation on the **Organizations** screen — nobody joins an organization without accepting. Until then the invitation is listed under **Pending invitations**, where you can withdraw it.

Removing a member cuts their access immediately — including any session that's open right now — and the organization disappears from their app; you can restore them later. Members can also leave on their own; they then show as **left** and need a new invitation to come back. Certain actions (like adding members to a chat) are limited to owners and admins.

## Audit log

Administrative and security-relevant events are recorded in an **audit log** you can review — who joined and with which role, role changes, removals and similar actions — so there's an accountable record of what happened in the organization.

## Retention

Set a **retention policy** so old messages are automatically deleted after a chosen period. A background job sweeps expired content on a schedule, keeping the organization to the data it actually needs.

## Privacy controls

Turn on **metadata-minimal mode** to minimize the metadata the node emits: it switches off typing indicators and delivery/read receipts, and forces notifications to their most generic form. It's enforced on the server for everyone in the organization — see [Accounts & privacy](/bastion/accounts-and-privacy).

## Channels

A **channel** is a persistent room. By default any member can post; an owner or admin can switch a channel to **admins-only posting** (announcements) — members can still reply in comment threads. The toggle lives on the channel's info page.

## Modules

Bastion is modular. The base module is **chat + calls**; the admin settings are where an organization's enabled capabilities are managed as more modules become available.

<Note>
    The admin panel is **content-blind** where it counts: because messages and calls are end-to-end encrypted, administration is about people, roles and policy — not reading conversations.
</Note>

## Platform vs. organization admin

- **Organization admin** (this panel) — you, running your own node.
- **Platform admin** — a separate, content-blind view used to manage organizations and licensing at the platform level. It can see that an organization exists and its plan, never its messages.
