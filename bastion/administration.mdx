title: "Administration"
description: "Run your organization from the Bastion admin panel — members, roles, audit log, retention and modules."
---

Every Bastion node comes with a separate **admin panel** for the organization's owner and admins. It runs alongside the node and signs in with the same passwordless email flow.

## Members and roles

Invite people by email and manage who can do what:

- **Owner** — full control of the organization.
- **Admin** — manage members, chats and settings.
- **Member** — use chat and calls.

Adding or removing members, and changing roles, all happen here. Certain actions (like adding members to a chat) are limited to owners and admins.

## Audit log

Administrative and security-relevant events are recorded in an **audit log** you can review — who was invited, role changes, and similar actions — so there's an accountable record of what happened in the organization.

## Retention

Set a **retention policy** so old messages are automatically deleted after a chosen period. A background job sweeps expired content on a schedule, keeping the organization to the data it actually needs.

## Modules

Bastion is modular. The base module is **chat + calls**; the admin settings are where an organization's enabled capabilities are managed as more modules become available.

<Note>
  The admin panel is **content-blind** where it counts: because messages and calls are end-to-end encrypted, administration is about people, roles and policy — not reading conversations.
</Note>

## Platform vs. organization admin

- **Organization admin** (this panel) — you, running your own node.
- **Platform admin** — a separate, content-blind view used to manage organizations and licensing at the platform level. It can see that an organization exists and its plan, never its messages.
