<AccordionGroup>
    <Accordion title="What platforms does Bastion run on?">
        Web (in the browser), desktop for Windows, macOS and Linux, and native apps for iOS and Android. One account works across all of them, and calls ring natively on your phone.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="I'm in more than one organization. Do I need a separate app for each?">
        No — it's one app for every organization. After you sign in, Bastion lists the organizations you belong to; pick one, and switch any time from the side menu or **Settings → Organization**. Each organization's chats stay on its own server, and a notification from another organization offers to switch you there.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Can I talk to people in other organizations?">
        Yes, if both organizations allow it. From a chat's info page, invite someone from another organization by their exact username or email; they get a notification and a single-use invitation valid for 7 days. Guests only ever see the chats they're invited to, and each organization's admin decides whether its members may join, or host, cross-organization chats. See [Chat](/bastion/chat#managing-a-chat).
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Which languages does Bastion speak?">
        The apps, the admin panel and the website are available in English, Russian, German, French, Spanish, Italian and Dutch. Emails and the checkout are in English or Russian.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Is it really end-to-end encrypted?">
        Yes. Messages, files and calls are sealed on the sender's device and opened only on the recipients'. The server — even one you run yourself — only ever handles ciphertext. You can verify a contact with their [safety number](/bastion/security). Two things are deliberately not end-to-end encrypted and are labelled as such: public topics (meant to be searchable by your organization) and messages posted by bots. For the most sensitive conversations, [high-security chats](/bastion/chat#high-security-chats) add forward secrecy.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="What can the server operator see?">
        Not your content. The control plane brokers identity, push and licensing, so it sees sign-in and delivery metadata and which plan an organization is on — never message text or call media. See [Accounts & privacy](/bastion/accounts-and-privacy).
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Can people see who I talk to and when?">
        Encryption hides *what* you say, not that a conversation happens: your node can see the "envelope" — who's talking to whom and when — which is inherent to delivering messages (and on a self-hosted node, it's your own server). Bastion narrows the rest: message sizes are padded, notifications are generic by default, there's no link-preview or contact-sync leak, and an admin can enable **metadata-minimal mode** to switch off typing, receipts and online status too. See [Accounts & privacy](/bastion/accounts-and-privacy).
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Do I have to self-host?">
        No. You can run your own node, or use the managed option — the same stack, operated for you, one organization per instance, ready within one business day. See [Pricing](/bastion/pricing).
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="How is it priced?">
        Flat per server, not per seat — unlimited members. Self-hosted is $99/month, managed $249/month, optional modules +$29/month each; yearly billing gives two months free. There is no free trial.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="How do I buy, and where do I get my organization's credentials?">
        On [bastion.burakov.net](https://bastion.burakov.net/pricing): sign in with your email, pick a plan and pay through Paddle. Your organization is created as soon as the payment clears. A self-hosted node's organization ID and secret are on **[Your organizations](https://bastion.burakov.net/account/)** — the secret is shown once and never emailed; you can rotate it there any time. See [Self-hosting](/bastion/self-hosting#your-organizations-credentials).
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="What happens if a payment fails or I cancel?">
        A failed renewal keeps everything working for 7 days while the payment is retried. A cancelled subscription runs to the end of its paid period. After that the server turns read-only, and 14 days later it stops admitting sign-ins — but nothing is deleted, and renewing from your organization page restores full use. See [Pricing](/bastion/pricing#when-a-payment-fails-or-a-subscription-ends).
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="What's in the base product, and what's coming?">
        The base module is chat and calls, including high-security chats. Optional modules — Topics and the AI assistant — can be added at checkout or later, without re-deploying. On the roadmap: an independent review of the cryptography.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Can I export or delete my data?">
        Yes. In **Account settings** you can export your account data and your data from every organization server you use, erase your data on those servers, or delete your account. See [Accounts & privacy](/bastion/accounts-and-privacy#your-data-rights).
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Can I lose access to my messages?">
        Your key lives on your device and can be backed up with a passphrase. If you lose both the passphrase and every signed-in device, that key can't be recovered — the server never had it. You can still [start over with a new key](/bastion/security#lost-your-key-start-over): teammates' apps hand your shared conversations to the new key, history included. Keep your backup safe.
    </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
