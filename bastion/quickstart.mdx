If your organization already uses Bastion, there's nothing to install or configure — you only need to
sign in. (Setting Bastion up for your team? Start with [Plans & buying](/bastion/pricing).)

<Steps>
    <Step title="Open the app">
        Use the web app at [app.bastion-chat.com](https://app.bastion-chat.com), or install Bastion for
        desktop, iOS or Android — see [Download](https://bastion-chat.com/download). It's one app for every
        organization.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Sign in with your email">
        Enter the email address your organization invited and type the one-time code you receive — no
        password. If you don't have an account yet, this creates one. You can also continue with
        **Google**, and on iPhone with **Sign in with Apple**.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Join your organization">
        The **Organizations** screen shows your **Invitations** — tap **Join** (or **Decline**); it says
        which role you're invited as. Organizations that let everyone with your work email in appear under
        **You can join**. If you belong to just one organization, it opens straight away.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Start talking">
        Open **Chats**, start a **New chat**, pick people from your organization and type. Everything is
        end-to-end encrypted by default — see [Security & encryption](/bastion/security).
    </Step>
    <Step title="Back up your key">
        Your encryption key lives on your device. Open **Encryption keys** in the menu and back it up with a
        passphrase, so you can read your chats on a new device. See
        [Your key, your backup](/bastion/security#your-key-your-backup).
    </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
    Don't see your organization? Ask its admin to invite the email you signed in with (or to add your
    company's email domain), then tap **Check again**.
</Tip>

## Your organizations

- **Several organizations?** Switch any time from the side menu or **Settings → Organization → Switch
    organization**. Switching reconnects to that organization's server and warns you if you have
    unsent messages.
- **Leave an organization** from its **⋯** menu on the Organizations screen. The organization's owner
    can't leave it.
- Each organization shows a status: **Active**, **Suspended**, **Payment due** (everything keeps
    working for 7 days while the payment is retried), **Subscription ended — read-only**, **Awaiting
    payment**, or **Server not connected yet**. In the read-only state you can still sign in and read,
    but not send.

## On a new device

Sign in as usual. If this device doesn't have your encryption key yet, the chat list says *Restore your
encryption key to read your chats on this device* — open **Encryption keys** and restore it from your
backup file or the copy you kept on the server. Lost your key entirely? See
[Start over with a new key](/bastion/security#lost-your-key-start-over).
