---
title: "Quickstart"
description: "Pair two devices in Sidedrop and send your first file."
mode: "wide"
---

You need two devices with a modern browser — your phone and your laptop, or two computers. There's
nothing to install and no account.

<Steps>
    <Step title="Open Sidedrop on both devices">
        Go to [sidedrop.burakov.net](https://sidedrop.burakov.net) on each. Each device immediately shows
        its own room: a QR code, a **share link** and a **code**.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Connect them">
        Pick **one** device's room and join it from the other:

        - **scan** its QR code with the other device's camera, or
        - **open its link** on the other device, or
        - **type its code** into **Have a code?** on the other device and tap **Join**.

        Both devices then show **Connected**.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Drop something">
        On either device, drop files or folders onto the page, or use **Choose files**, **Choose folder** or
        **Send text**. Sending starts right away — the other side doesn't have to accept.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Save it on the other device">
        When an item shows **Ready to save**, tap **Save** (or **Save all** for several files). See
        [Save what you receive](/sidedrop/saving-files).
    </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
    Keep **both tabs open** until the transfer finishes and you've saved what you received. Closing or
    reloading a tab ends the session.
</Warning>
