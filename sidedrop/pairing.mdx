## Every device gets a room

When you open Sidedrop, it creates a private **room** for that device and shows it three ways — a QR
code, a share link and a code. All three point at the same room. To pair, **join one device's room from
the other**:

- **Scan the QR code** with the other device's camera, or
- **open the share link** on the other device, or
- **type the code** into **Have a code?** on the other device and tap **Join**.

Joining with a code leaves the room your own device had made and joins the other one.

## Codes

A code is six characters, shown as `XXX-XXX` — for example `K7Q-2XM`. When typing it:

- capitals or lowercase both work, and spaces or dashes are ignored;
- it never contains the letters **I**, **L**, **O** or **U** — if you see something that looks like an
    O, it's a zero.

## Two devices per room

A room connects **exactly two** devices. A third device trying to join sees *This room is already full —
Sidedrop connects one device to one.* Either side can send once they're paired.

## How long a room lasts

- A room and its code work for **2 hours** after the room was created (the clock restarts when someone
    joins it).
- When both devices have left, the room is deleted about **45 seconds** later.

After that, the code and link show *That room or code was not found. Ask for a fresh one.* — open
Sidedrop again for a new room.

## Sending to another person

Share your room's link or code with them through any messenger. Whoever joins first is paired with you, so
**share it only with the person you mean to send to** — there's no "accept" step on either side.

<Note>
    If the other device leaves, your page goes back to showing your room after a short wait, so the same
    or another device can join again. Unfinished transfers show **Reconnecting…** until the connection is
    back.
</Note>
