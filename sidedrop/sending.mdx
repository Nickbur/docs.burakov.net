Once two devices are paired, **either** of them can send — and both can send at the same time.

## Ways to send

- **Drag and drop** files or whole folders onto the page. Folders are sent with everything inside,
    keeping their structure (empty folders are skipped).
- **Choose files** or **Choose folder** to pick them instead.
- **Send text** opens a box where you type or paste a snippet from your clipboard — a link, a code, a
    paragraph. Press **Ctrl + Enter** (**⌘ +
    Enter** on a Mac) or tap **Send**. Leading and trailing spaces are trimmed. It arrives on the other
    device as an item named `text.txt` with a preview and a **Copy** button.

Sending starts immediately — the other device doesn't have to accept anything.

## The queue

Items you send go **one at a time, in order**. The transfers table shows each one's progress, speed and
time left. On the sending side, an item turns **Saved** once the other device has received every byte of
it.

Either side can **Cancel** an item that's queued, running or paused.

## How big can it be?

Sidedrop sets no limit of its own. In practice:

- In **Chrome, Edge and most desktop browsers**, received files stream to the browser's own storage on
    disk, so multi-gigabyte files work as long as there's free space.
- Browsers that can't do that keep the incoming file **in memory** — Sidedrop shows a warning when that's
    the case — so very large files may not fit. Send big files **to** a desktop Chrome or Edge.
- A **text** snippet has to fit in a single message — around 256 KB of text. Send longer text as a
    file.

<Tip>
    Moving something large between a phone and a computer? Make the phone the **sender** and the computer
    the receiver.
</Tip>
