Sidedrop is one page that changes as you go.

## Top bar

The **Sidedrop** name (clicking it starts a fresh room), a **language** button that switches between
English and Russian, and a **☾ / ☀** button for dark or light theme.

- **Language** starts from your browser's language (Russian if your browser prefers it, otherwise
    English).
- **Theme** starts from your system's light/dark setting, otherwise dark.

Both choices are remembered in this browser.

## Status bar

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Connecting…** | Reaching the signaling server. |
| **Waiting for the other device…** | Your room is ready; nobody has joined yet. |
| **Connected** | Paired — with a **🔒 End-to-end encrypted** badge. |
| **Reconnecting…** | The connection dropped; Sidedrop is rebuilding it. |

If something goes wrong, the bar shows the error and a **New room** button.

## Pairing panel

Shown until a second device joins: *Open Sidedrop on your other device*, a QR code (*Scan to connect*),
the **Share link** and the code (*Or enter this code*), each with **Copy**, and **Have a code?** with a
field and **Join**. See [Pair two devices](/sidedrop/pairing).

## Drop zone

Shown once paired: *Drop files or folders here* — *or click to choose — they start sending immediately* —
with **Choose files**, **Choose folder** and **Send text**. See [Send files, folders and text](/sidedrop/sending).

If your browser can't stream incoming files to disk, a note warns: *This browser buffers incoming files in
memory, so very large files may not fit. Chrome or Edge on desktop have no such limit.*

## Transfers table

Every item sent or received, with its **direction** (↑ sent, ↓ received), **name** (and folder path),
**size**, **progress**, **speed**, **time left** and **status**:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Queued** | Waiting for the items ahead of it. |
| **Transferring** | Moving now. |
| **Reconnecting…** | Paused while the connection is rebuilt; it continues where it stopped. |
| **Ready to save** | Fully received — tap **Save**. |
| **Saved** | On the receiving side: you saved it. On the sending side: the other device has received every byte. |
| **Received** | A text snippet arrived — use **Copy**. |
| **Failed** / **Canceled** | It didn't finish. |

**Cancel** stops a queued, running or paused item. **Clear finished** removes saved, received, canceled and
failed rows. **Save all** appears when two or more files are ready.
