description: "Fixes for common Sidedrop problems — rooms not found or full, pairing that never connects, stuck transfers, phones, and saving."

## "That room or code was not found. Ask for a fresh one."

The room expired (rooms last 2 hours), both devices had left it, or the code was mistyped. Codes never
contain the letters I, L, O or U — an "O" is a zero. Open Sidedrop again on the other device for a fresh
room.

## "This room is already full — Sidedrop connects one device to one."

Two devices are already paired in that room. If you just reloaded one of them, wait a minute and try
again — the old connection takes about 45 seconds to be released — or start a new room.

## "Connection error."

Something else went wrong — for example a link that was cut off when copied. Tap **New room** and pair
again.

## It stays on "Connecting…"

Sidedrop can't reach its signaling server. Check your internet connection, disable anything that blocks
the site, and reload.

## The two devices never connect

You see your room, the other device joins, but the status stays on **Connecting…** or **Reconnecting…** and
never reaches **Connected**. When a network blocks direct connections, Sidedrop switches to a relay on its
own, so this is rare — it usually means a firewall (common on corporate and some public networks) blocks
relay traffic too. Try putting both devices on the same Wi-Fi, or another network.

## A transfer is stuck on "Transferring"

- The **receiving** browser may have run out of storage space — free up disk space and try again.
- On a phone, the page may have been sent to the background — bring Sidedrop back to the front.
- If nothing moves, **Cancel** the item and send it again.

## The other device left

After about 45 seconds, your page goes back to showing your room, and unfinished items show
**Reconnecting…**. Pair again to continue — but only while your tab is still open.

## My received files disappeared

Starting a **new room** or **joining another code** in that tab discards files that weren't saved yet, and
reloading the tab ends the session. Save files as soon as they're **Ready to save**.

## A large download on a phone fails

Some mobile browsers keep incoming files in memory and can't hold very large ones. Make the phone the
**sender** and a desktop browser the receiver.

## Save all downloads a zip

That's how browsers without a folder picker save several files. In Chrome or Edge on desktop, **Save all**
lets you pick a folder instead.
