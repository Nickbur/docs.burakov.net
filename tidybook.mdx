Tidybook builds **one clean address book** out of every place your contacts live — your phone, your Google
account, a CardDAV server, vCard and CSV files. It finds duplicates for you to review, tidies numbers and formatting,
keeps the book identical on all your devices with **end-to-end encryption**, and pushes the result back to your
sources when you say so.

## How it works

<Steps>
    <Step title="Bring everything in">
        Import from your phone or files, or connect Google and CardDAV accounts. Everything lands in one detailed
        address book, and nothing a source provides is thrown away.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Tidy up">
        Review the duplicates Tidybook finds and merge them — or don't. Phone numbers are stored in one international
        format, so the same number always matches.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Keep it everywhere">
        With **Sync**, your book stays the same on your phone, tablet and the web, encrypted on your device before it
        is uploaded.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Send it back">
        Push the tidy book back to your phone, Google or CardDAV — after a preview of exactly what will change.
    </Step>
</Steps>
