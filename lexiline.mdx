Lexiline is a **topic watcher**. You follow a subject you care about — even one with no news
yet — and Lexiline keeps watch across the web for you, bringing what matches into one clean,
chronological **timeline** the day it appears. No feeds to manage, no algorithm, no noise.

## The idea in one minute

<Steps>
  <Step title="Follow a topic">
    Describe what you care about in your own words — a possible sequel, a person, a product, a
    decision. It's fine if there's nothing to read about it yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Lexiline keeps watch">
    Instead of you checking back, Lexiline watches the web for your topic and reads for meaning,
    not just keywords.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Matches arrive in your timeline">
    When something genuinely matches, it lands in one clean, newest-first timeline — the day it
    appears — with nothing reshuffled or hidden.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You tune it">
    A thumbs-up or thumbs-down on any match teaches Lexiline what you meant, and the timeline
    gets sharper over time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Lexiline is calm on purpose: a timeline that ends, a cadence you choose, and no engagement
  bait. It keeps watch so your attention doesn't have to.
</Note>
