Lexiline is a **topic watcher**. You follow a subject you care about — even one with no news yet —
and Lexiline keeps searching the news and the web for it. An AI reads each result against what you
asked for, and what matches lands in one **feed**, newest first. It runs on **iPhone, Android and the
web**.

## The idea in one minute

<Steps>
    <Step title="Follow a topic">
        Describe what you care about in your own words — a possible sequel, a person, a product, a
        decision. It's fine if there's nothing to read about it yet.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Lexiline keeps watch">
        Its servers search for your topic again and again — whether or not your phone is on — and an
        AI scores every result for how well it matches what you meant.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Matches arrive in your feed">
        What scores high enough lands in your feed, newest first, with a short reason why it matched.
    </Step>
    <Step title="You tune it">
        Swipe a match to say "more like this", "not relevant" or "mute this site", and the topic gets
        sharper.
    </Step>
</Steps>
