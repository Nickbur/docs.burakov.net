Proofday is a **daily-accountability journal**. Each evening a reminder invites you to **close the
day** — a line or two about what you got done. Every day ends up **won**, a **rest** day, or **missed**,
and on **Pro** an AI turns your entries into proof of progress: weekly and monthly recaps, trends, and a
career brag-doc. It runs on **iPhone, Android and the web**.

## The idea in one minute

<Steps>
    <Step title="A reminder in the evening">
        Proofday reminds you to close the day at a time you choose — once, or more insistently if you
        want.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Close the day">
        Write a line or two about what you got done and optionally pick a mood. That's the whole entry,
        and the day is won. It takes about a minute.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Build a forgiving streak">
        Won days build your streak and momentum. Need a day off? Take a rest day, today or planned ahead —
        it doesn't break the streak. Forgot yesterday? Fill it in from the calendar.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Turn it into proof">
        On Pro, the AI turns your entries into recaps, trends and a career brag-doc — on demand.
    </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
    Proofday never judges the quality of your day — showing up and logging honestly is the whole win.
    The daily habit is free; the AI proof and your full history are Pro.
</Note>
