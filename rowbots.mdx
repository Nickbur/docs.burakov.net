Rowbots AI is an **autonomous software team for a fleet of products**. You connect your projects and
hand it whatever you have — tasks, half-formed ideas, user feedback, error reports — and it works the
way a whole staff would: it plans before it codes, writes the change, has an **independent judge**
prove the result against executed reality, and hands you a **pull request to merge**. With nothing
queued, it keeps the fleet healthy on its own, and it genuinely stops when nothing is worth the cost.

<Note>
    Rowbots AI is in **early access**. The engine — planning, execution, the judge and pull requests —
    runs today; the dashboard at [app.rowbots.dev](https://app.rowbots.dev) and the external channel
    connectors are opening to early users step by step. These pages describe how the product works.
</Note>

## The idea in one minute

<Steps>
    <Step title="Connect your products">
        Point Rowbots at the git repositories of your projects — on GitHub, GitLab, Gitea or your own
        server — and connect your model keys and compute.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Agree on the rules once">
        A short onboarding interview turns your product vision, taste and priorities into a
        **constitution**. Rowbots acts only on what you confirmed there.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Hand it work — or let it maintain">
        Drop in tasks, ideas and feedback. Rowbots triages them against your priorities, plans each one,
        builds it in a sandbox and verifies it. With no tasks queued it fixes, updates and hardens what
        is already there — only while the work is worth its cost.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Merge the result">
        Every change arrives as an ordinary pull request in your own git host, with the evidence of what
        was verified and an honest note of what could not be. Merging stays your decision.
    </Step>
</Steps>

## Why it's different

- **Reality over opinion.** A change is accepted because it compiles, its tests pass, the scanners are
  clean and the app actually renders — never because another model said it looks fine.
- **Results per dollar, not activity.** You set a hard monthly budget; Rowbots plans the month against
  it and tells you up front what fits.
- **You own the tool, not a lab.** Bring your own keys and your own compute, or run open-weight models
  on your own endpoint. Pricing is a flat fee per active project — never per token.
