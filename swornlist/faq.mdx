---
title: "FAQ"
description: "Common questions from guests and organizers."
mode: "wide"
---

<AccordionGroup>
    <Accordion title="Do I have to verify for every event?">
        No. One check is reused at every Swornlist event until your document expires or two years pass. An identity
        check also covers age checks.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Does the organizer see my passport?">
        No. Organizers never see your document or birth date. Door staff see your verified name (identity checks), the
        age checks you meet and your photo — only around the event.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="What does a verification cost?">
        Nothing for you if the organizer pays for checks. Otherwise a Verified ID costs $1.99 once and works at every
        event.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Can I pay for a ticket in Swornlist?">
        No — ticket money goes straight to the organizer, the way they tell you on the event page. Swornlist only
        records whether you paid.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="My phone died at the door.">
        Staff can find you by name and see the same card as a scan.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="What happens if the door has no signal?">
        The door keeps working from its encrypted offline list and syncs when the signal is back.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="We hit the monthly verified-guest limit — will guests be turned away?">
        Never at the door. Only new registrations that need a check wait until you upgrade or the month turns.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Can a banned person just make a new account?">
        A ban also matches the verified document, so a new account with the same document is caught when it verifies.
    </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
