## My check failed

- **The document photo was unclear or the selfie didn't match** — try again in good light; a Verified ID covers up to
  three attempts.
- **"This ID is already linked to another Swornlist account"** — one document backs one account. Sign in with that
  account, or delete the check there first.
- **It says the check is under review** — the provider is looking at it; the ticket activates by itself when it
  passes.

## My ticket has no QR

The QR appears once the ticket is active. Open it to see what is missing: a payment, the organizer's approval, or your
check (and its deadline).

## The door says "Different time slot", "Already checked in" or "Collect payment"

- **Different time slot** — the ticket is for another time slot of the event.
- **Already checked in** — the ticket was scanned in already (perhaps on another device).
- **Collect payment** — take the payment and mark it paid when admitting.
- **Must verify first** — the guest hasn't finished their check; with late check at the door on, they can do it now.

## "Purchases are made in the app"

Plans, credits and Verified ID are bought in the Swornlist app for iOS and Android.

## A door device was lost

Revoke it in **Settings → Door devices** — its offline list is wiped the next time it connects, and it expires on
its own 24 hours after the event anyway.
