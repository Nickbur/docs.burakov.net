<Steps>
    <Step title="Create your organization">
        Sign in, open **Organize** and give your organization a name. You are its owner; your plan covers every
        organization you own. Before your first event with checks goes live, verify your own identity (free — Swornlist
        pays for it); it keeps guests' data in trusted hands.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Create an event">
        **New event**: title, description, time and time zone, venue (you can show the exact address only to confirmed
        guests) or an online link, visibility (anyone with the link, or private behind an access code) and capacity.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Add ticket types">
        In **Tickets**: free or paid (your price and how you take payment), how many, and what each type asks for —
        **no check**, an **age check** with a minimum age, or an **identity check**. See
        [Events & tickets](/swornlist/events).
    </Step>
    <Step title="Publish and share">
        **Publish** makes the page live. **Copy link** and share it anywhere.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Run the door">
        On the day, open **Door** on any phone of your team. It downloads the guest list for offline use 24 hours before
        the start. Scan, look at the photo, admit. See [The door](/swornlist/door).
    </Step>
</Steps>

## Who pays for a guest's check

Each event chooses **who pays for new verifications** — guests verified before cost nothing either way:

- **We do (credits)** — one credit per guest new to Swornlist. Every plan comes with credits; see
  [Plans & credits](/swornlist/plans-and-credits).
- **Guests do** — a guest new to Swornlist buys their own **Verified ID** once, and it works at every event.

If your credits run out, new guests are offered a Verified ID automatically, and you get an email.
