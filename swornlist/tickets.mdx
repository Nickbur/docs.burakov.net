## The QR

Each ticket has a **signed QR** made for you. It works offline and cannot be forged; at events that ask for a check,
staff match your face to your verified photo. The QR appears once the ticket is active — after payment, approval or
your check, whatever the ticket needs.

## Wallet and calendar

On a ticket, **Take it with you** offers:

- **Add to Apple Wallet** (iPhone) or **Save to Google Wallet** (Android) — the same QR, updated when the event moves
  or the ticket changes;
- **Add to calendar** — a calendar entry that updates itself, or a prefilled **Google Calendar** / **Outlook** event;
- **Calendar sync** (Tickets → Calendar sync) — connect Google Calendar once and Swornlist keeps a calendar of its own
  with all your tickets. It only touches the calendar it creates.

## Bring someone

If the event allows **companions**, your ticket has links to share — each companion claims their own ticket (and
verifies themselves if needed).

## Can't go?

- **Transfer** — if allowed, send the ticket to someone by email; they claim it and your QR stops working.
- **Cancel** — before the organizer's cut-off, give the place back.

## Sold out

**Join the waitlist**. If a place frees up you get an offer by email and push, and have 24 hours to take it.

## Member cards and pick-up passes

Club members find their **member cards**, and adults authorised to collect children their **pick-up passes**, under
**Tickets**.
