## The monthly verified-guest limit

Each guest counts once per event in a calendar month, across all your organizations, once their ticket with a check
becomes active. Reaching the limit stops only **new** registrations that need a check — guests who already have a
ticket always get in. You get an email when it happens; the limit resets on the 1st, or upgrade.

## Credits

A credit pays for checking a guest who is **new to Swornlist** — guests verified before cost nothing. A credit is
used only when a check succeeds.

- Plan credits arrive each month and lapse at the month's end.
- Credit packs: **25 for $15**, **100 for $49**, **500 for $199** — bought credits do not expire.

**Organize → Settings → Plan & credits** shows your plan, this month's verified guests, your credits and every
movement. Only the owner buys.

## Verified ID (for guests)

When the organizer does not pay for checks — or has run out of credits — a guest new to Swornlist buys a
**Verified ID**: **$1.99** once, up to **3 attempts**, then valid at every Swornlist event until the document expires
or two years pass.

## Where to buy

Plans, credit packs and Verified ID are bought in the **Swornlist app for iOS and Android**. Store purchases are
refunded under the store's rules.
