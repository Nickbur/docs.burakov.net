Open **Door** and pick the event — door staff see only the events of their organization, from 24 hours before the
start until 24 hours after the end.

## Scanning

Point the camera at the guest's QR (in the app, the web page or a Wallet pass). The result card shows:

- **Admit** (green) or what to look at (amber / red): collect payment, must verify first, different time slot,
  already checked in, a canceled, transferred or expired ticket, a pass for another event;
- for tickets that ask for a check: the **verified name** (identity checks), the **age checks met** and the
  **photo** — match the face, then decide.

**Admit** records the check-in; **Deny** asks for a reason — the face doesn't match, the identity couldn't be confirmed, behaviour, or other.

## Works without signal

When the door opens, the device downloads an **encrypted offline list** — every ticket and, where the event asks for
it, the verified names and photos. With no signal, scans are checked against it and decisions are queued; they sync
as soon as the connection is back. If two devices admitted the same guest offline, the second is flagged as a
duplicate for you to review. The offline list deletes itself 24 hours after the event, and a revoked device is wiped
remotely.

## Find a guest by name

**Find a guest by name** searches the list — useful when a phone is dead. You see the same card as a scan.

## Check-out and re-entry

A checked-in guest can be checked out and back in. At kids events, children are checked in and out one by one — see
[Kids events](/swornlist/kids).

## Member cards and pick-up passes

The door also reads **member cards** (club members) and **pick-up passes** (adults authorised to collect children).

## Door devices

Every phone that opens the door registers as a **door device** of your organization (**Settings → Door devices**).
Your plan sets how many you can have; revoke a lost phone there.
