title: "Kids events"
description: "Children declared by a verified guardian, authorised pick-up adults and check-out at the door — on the Venue plan."
mode: "wide"
---

Turn on **Kids mode** for an event and mark a ticket type **Kids**. A guardian with a verified identity registers and
then, on the ticket, adds:

- **children** — first name, age and an optional note (an allergy, for example), which door staff see. No document or
  photo of a child is ever collected;
- **who may collect them** — other adults by email. Each one verifies their identity and gets a **pick-up pass**.

## At the door

- Scanning the guardian's ticket shows the children; staff tick who comes in, and later who goes home.
- Scanning a **pick-up pass** shows the authorised adult's verified photo and the children they may collect.
- Anyone else is refused — a pending or revoked authorisation does not work.
- **Children still inside** lists every child who has not been collected yet.

Children's data is deleted 30 days after the event.
