## Who sees what

| Who | Sees |
| --- | --- |
| **The organizer's team** | Your display name, your ticket and payment status, your answers, and whether you met the requirement. Never your document, birth date or contact details. |
| **Door staff** | Around the event only: what the ticket asks for — the verified name (identity checks), the age checks met and your photo. |
| **Other guests** | Nothing. There are no public profiles, no chat and no guest lists. |

## How long data is kept

| Data | Kept |
| --- | --- |
| Your verification record | Until the document expires or 24 months — or until you delete it |
| The verification provider's session (document images, selfie) | 30 days |
| Answers, payment receipts, children's data | 30 days after the event |
| Offline guest lists on door devices | Until 24 hours after the event |
| An organizer's guest history (name, flags, attendance) | 12 months |
| Your tickets and check-ins | With your account; erased when you delete it |

The full Privacy Policy is on the [project page](https://burakov.net/projects/swornlist).

## Delete your data

- **Verification → Delete my check** removes your verification and photo now.
- Deleting your account (on burakov.net) erases everything else.

## Report an event

Something wrong with an event — a scam, something unsafe or illegal? Open its page and tap **Report this event**. The
organizer is not told who reported it. Swornlist may take the event down.
