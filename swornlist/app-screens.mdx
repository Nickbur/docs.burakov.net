## For guests

| Screen | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Event page** | What, when, where, the ticket types and what each one asks for; register, apply or join the waitlist; report the event. |
| **Tickets** | Upcoming and past tickets, with links to **Member cards**, **Pick-up passes** and **Calendar sync**. |
| **Ticket** | The QR, the place, Wallet and calendar buttons, companions, transfer, cancel — and, at kids events, your children. |
| **Verification** | Your check: level, validity, delete; the consent screen before a new check, with the Verified ID offer when needed. |

## For organizers

| Screen | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Organize** | Your organization, its events, **New event**, **Door**, **Analytics**, **Members** and **Settings**. |
| **Event** | Overview (status, link, counters, publish, door, edit, repeat, message guests, insights), **Tickets** (types and time slots), **Guests** (filters, approve, mark paid, import, export, guest cards), **Registration** (codes and questions). |
| **Door** | Scanner with the result card, offline list status, search by name, children still inside. |
| **Settings** | Name and branding, team and invitations, ban list, door devices, **Plan & credits**. |
| **Plan & credits** | Your plan and limits, this month's verified guests, credits and their history. |
