## An event

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Title, description** | The description takes simple formatting: paragraphs, **bold**, *italic*, links and lists. |
| **When** | Start and end on the venue's own clock, with its time zone. |
| **Where** | Venue name and address, a map link, or an online link. **Show the address only to confirmed guests** hides it until a guest's ticket is active. |
| **Who can see it** | **Anyone with the link**, or **Private** — the page asks for an access code. |
| **Capacity** | Optional, for the whole event; ticket types have their own quantities too. |
| **Registration closes** | Optional deadline. |
| **Verify by** | How many hours before the start a guest must finish their check (default 2). **Let late guests verify at the door** lets them do it on arrival instead. |
| **Who pays for new verifications** | Your credits, or the guest's own Verified ID — see [Plans & credits](/swornlist/plans-and-credits). |

A draft is visible only to your team. **Publish** makes it live; **Cancel event** cancels every ticket and tells the
guests. Changes to the time or place of a published event are announced to its guests.

## Ticket types

| Setting | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Free / Paid** | A paid type has your price and currency, a payment link and instructions. |
| **How payment is confirmed** | **I mark it paid** · **Guest uploads a receipt** · **Paid at the door**. See [Payments](/swornlist/payments). |
| **Verification** | No check · age check with a minimum age · identity check. |
| **Quantity** | Empty means no limit of its own. |
| **Hidden** | Only shows to people who enter an access code. |
| **Transferable**, **Allow companions** | See [Registration features](/swornlist/registration). |
| **Members only** | Only for members of your club — see [Clubs](/swornlist/clubs). |
| **Kids** | In kids mode — see [Kids events](/swornlist/kids). |
| **Time slots** | Limit the type to some of the event's slots. |

## Holds and deadlines

- An unpaid ticket waiting for a payment or a receipt is held for **48 hours**; then the place goes back (and to the
  waitlist).
- A guest who has not finished their check by the **verify-by** time loses the ticket unless late check at the door is
  on.

## Cancelling

A guest can cancel their own ticket until your cancellation cut-off. You can cancel any guest's ticket from the guest
list. Refunds of ticket money are yours to make.
