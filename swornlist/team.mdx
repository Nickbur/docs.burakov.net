## Organizations

An organization is the organizer behind events — a club, a venue, a promoter. It has no public profile or followers;
guests only ever see its name (and, on Pro, its logo and colour on event pages). You can own several organizations;
your plan covers all of them.

## Roles

| Role | Can |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Everything, including the plan, billing and deleting the organization. |
| **Manager** | Create and run events, manage guests, codes, questions, members and bans. |
| **Door** | Open the door of the organization's events and see their guest lists around event time — nothing else. |

Invite people by email in **Settings → Team**; they accept with the link. Your plan sets the team size (the owner
counts).

## Ban list (Pro)

From a guest's card, **Ban** adds them to your organization's ban list with a private note. A ban works by account
**and** by the verified document, so a new account with the same document is caught too. Banned people cannot
register, claim or join the waitlist for your events; they only see a neutral message. You can cancel their upcoming
tickets at the same time, and lift a ban any time in **Settings → Ban list**. Bans must be lawful.
