## Approve every registration

Turn on **Approve every registration** and guests **apply** instead of registering. Applications wait in the
**Applications** filter of the guest list; approve or decline them one by one or in bulk.

## Waitlist

When a ticket type is sold out, guests can **join the waitlist**. A freed place is offered to the next person, who
has **24 hours** to take it before it moves on.

## Codes

In **Registration → Codes**:

- **Access codes** open hidden ticket types or a private event.
- **Promo codes** take a percentage or an amount off your displayed price, optionally for some types, a number of
  uses or until a date.

## Questions

Ask guests anything at registration: free text, one choice, several choices or a checkbox, optional or required.
Answers show in each guest's card and in the CSV export, and are deleted 30 days after the event.

## Companions

Allow up to a number of **companions** per guest. Each companion gets their own link and claims their own ticket —
and verifies themselves if the type asks for it.

## Time slots

Add **slots** (rounds, class times) with their own capacity. Guests pick one at registration; the door warns if a
ticket is for another slot.

## Transfers

If the type is transferable, a guest can pass their ticket on by email. The new holder claims it and verifies
themselves; the old QR stops working.

## Importing guests

**Import guests** takes a CSV of emails and names (and the ticket type). Each person gets a link to claim their
ticket. You need their consent to email them.

## Exporting (Pro)

**Export CSV** downloads the guest list — names, ticket types, slots, statuses, payment, check and the answers. No
photos, documents or contact details.
