## Two levels

| Level | What the guest does | What the door sees |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Age check** | A selfie video; the provider estimates age, and asks for a document when the estimate is too close to the limit. | Age checks met (16+, 18+, 21+) and the photo. No name, no birth date. |
| **Identity check** | A photo of an identity document and a selfie video matched to it. | The verified name, age checks and the photo. |

An identity check also covers any age check. A ticket type can ask for an age check with a minimum age (for example
18+) or for an identity check.

## How a check runs

<Steps>
    <Step title="Consent first">
        Before the check, the app shows what is checked, what is kept, who sees the photo and who does the
        biometric processing — and the guest agrees.
    </Step>
    <Step title="The hosted check">
        A specialist identity-verification provider runs the check in the browser: the document, a live selfie and a
        face match. It usually takes about a minute.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Back in the app">
        The result arrives on its own; waiting tickets become active. A check sent for review is decided by the provider
        — the app shows it as pending until then.
    </Step>
</Steps>

## What is kept

- the verified level, and for an identity check the name and date of birth;
- the document's country and expiry date;
- a one-way fingerprint of the document, so one document cannot back two accounts;
- a portrait, kept encrypted.

The document images and the selfie video stay with the provider, which deletes them within 30 days. Organizers never
see a document or a birth date.

## How long it lasts

A check stays valid until the document expires or for **24 months**, whichever comes first. Then the next event that
asks for one asks again.

## Deleting your check

**Verification → Delete my check** removes the record and the portrait at once. Tickets that need a check go
back to waiting until you verify again.

## Who sees the photo

Only door staff of an event you hold a ticket for, from 24 hours before it starts until 24 hours after it ends — and
only for ticket types that ask for a check.
