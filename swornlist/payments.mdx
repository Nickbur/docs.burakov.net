title: "Payments"
description: "Ticket money goes from guests to you directly — Swornlist only keeps the status."

Swornlist never touches ticket money. For a paid ticket type you set the displayed price and tell guests how to pay —
a payment link (PayPal, Revolut, a bank invoice, your own checkout…), bank details, or cash at the door. Swornlist
records only whether the ticket is paid.

## How payment is confirmed

| Mode | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **I mark it paid** | The ticket waits as **Payment due**. You mark guests paid in the guest list — one by one or in bulk. |
| **Guest uploads a receipt** | The guest uploads an image or PDF of the receipt; you open it in their card and approve or reject it. |
| **Paid at the door** | The ticket is active with a **Pay at the door** flag; door staff collect the money and mark it paid when admitting. |

Unpaid tickets (the first two modes) are held for **48 hours**, then the place goes back. Receipts are deleted 30
days after the event.

## Refunds

Refunds are between you and the guest. Mark a ticket **Refunded** in the guest list to keep the record straight.

<Note>
    Swornlist's own prices — plans, credits and Verified ID — are separate. See
    [Plans & credits](/swornlist/plans-and-credits).
</Note>
