## Message guests (Pro)

**Message guests** on an event sends an email and a push to:

- **Everyone registered**,
- **Guests still expected** (active tickets not yet in), or
- **Guests who came in**.

Guests' addresses stay private. Up to **5 messages per event a day** (and 20 per organization); every message is
logged on the event.

## How an event is going

The event overview shows its funnel: registrations, applications (approved, declined, waiting), checks finished,
guests who came in, no-shows, cancellations, the waitlist (offers taken), returning guests and the credits the event
used.

## Analytics

**Organize → Analytics** sums up the last 30, 90 or 365 days across your events: events held, registrations, check-ins,
no-shows, how many finished their check, returning guests, waitlist conversion, credits used — and this month's
verified guests against your plan. Counts only; no names.
