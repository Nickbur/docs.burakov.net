---
title: "Quickstart for guests"
description: "Register for an event, verify your age or identity once, and get in with your QR."
mode: "wide"
---

You don't need the app to start: every event has a web page. The app adds offline tickets, push reminders and the
Wallet buttons.

<Steps>
    <Step title="Open the event link">
        The organizer shares a link like `app.swornlist.com/e/…`. The page shows when and where, the ticket types and
        what each one asks for — **No check**, **Age check 18+** or **ID verification**.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Sign in with your email">
        Enter your email and the 6-digit code you receive — no password. One account works for every event.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Pick a ticket and register">
        Choose a ticket type (and a time slot, if the event has them), answer the organizer's questions, and tap
        **Register** — or **Apply** when the organizer approves every guest.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Verify if the ticket asks for it">
        Tap **Verify now**, read what is checked and kept, agree, and follow the hosted check: a photo of your
        document and a short selfie video. When it passes, the ticket becomes active by itself. See
        [Verification](/swornlist/verification).
    </Step>
    <Step title="Show your QR at the door">
        Open **Tickets**, tap the ticket and show the QR. It works without signal. You can also add it to
        **Apple Wallet** or **Google Wallet** and to your calendar.
    </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
    If the ticket is paid, the page tells you how the organizer takes payment — a link, bank details, or cash at the
    door. Swornlist never handles that money; refunds are the organizer's.
</Note>
