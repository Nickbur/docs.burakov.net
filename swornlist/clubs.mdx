## Series

**Repeat…** on an event turns it into the first occurrence of a series:

- **Weekly** on chosen days (every week or every 2–4 weeks), **monthly** on a day of the month or on the first /
  second / third / fourth / last weekday, or a list of **dates**;
- an optional last date, how far ahead occurrences are created (4–26 weeks), and whether they are published
  straight away.

Every occurrence is a regular event you can edit, publish or cancel on its own. After you change one, Swornlist offers
to **apply the changes to the following occurrences**. Changing the rule removes future dates that nobody registered
for; **End series** stops new ones.

## Memberships

In **Organize → Members**:

- **Kinds of membership** — name, how long it runs, and whether the member card asks for an age check or an
  identity check.
- **Add member** by email (they get a link to claim it), **import** a list, or let guests **ask to join** from a
  members-only ticket — then approve or decline.
- **Renew** or **revoke** at any time. Members get a reminder before a membership expires, and so does your team.

A member sees their **member card** under **Tickets → Member cards**: a signed QR the door scans to show the
member's verified photo and how long the membership runs. Ticket types marked **Members only** admit only members.

Membership fees, if any, are yours to collect.
