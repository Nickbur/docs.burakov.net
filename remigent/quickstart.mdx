Remigent runs in your browser at [app.remigent.com](https://app.remigent.com). Sign in with your email and a one-time
code — no password.

## If you're setting up Remigent

<Steps>
    <Step title="Create your workspace">
        Sign in at [app.remigent.com](https://app.remigent.com). Your workspace is created with you as its **Owner**.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Connect the essentials">
        Connect your git host, choose where code should run — a managed sandbox provider or your own runner — and add
        at least one model provider key: in the dashboard for managed mode, or in your runner's configuration, where it
        never leaves your machine. Your **14-day Studio trial** starts when you connect your first git account.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Add a project and take the interview">
        Add your first project from its repositories. Remigent studies the code, then interviews you — one question at a
        time, each with a recommended answer — until your constitution and the project's recipe are confirmed.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Set the budget">
        Set the monthly spending cap. Autonomy switches on.
    </Step>
</Steps>

The full walkthrough, with every option, is in [Set up a workspace](/remigent/setup).

## If you were invited

<Steps>
    <Step title="Accept the invitation">
        Open the invitation email and sign in with that email address.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Find your way around">
        Your role decides what you see and can do — see [Team & permissions](/remigent/team-and-permissions) and
        [Dashboard tour](/remigent/dashboard).
    </Step>
</Steps>

## Your first piece of work

<Steps>
    <Step title="Add a task">
        In the **Inbox**, describe what you want in your own words and language — "add CSV export to the reports
        page". Attach screenshots or files if they help. See [Feeding it work](/remigent/feeding-work).
    </Step>
    <Step title="Watch it move">
        The task moves through triage, planning, building and judging. Its card shows the current stage, the plan and
        the cost forecast. If Remigent needs a decision only you can make, it asks — see
        [Review, merge & escalations](/remigent/review-and-merge).
    </Step>
    <Step title="Merge the pull request">
        The result arrives as a pull request in your own git host, with the evidence of what was verified. Read it and
        merge — your CI/CD takes it from there.
    </Step>
</Steps>
