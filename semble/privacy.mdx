Semble is private by default: the AI is off until the host turns it on, nothing is recorded unless the host
starts a recording, and everything a meeting produces can be deleted.

## Hosts and guests

- **Hosts** sign in with an email code. Their name in meetings is their account **username**.
- **Guests** join from a link with a display name only — no account, no email. The name is remembered in
    their browser for next time.

## Your media

Audio and video travel through Semble's own media servers. Recordings are stored on Semble's own storage.
The in-room chat goes straight to the people in the meeting and is never stored.

## When the AI is on

While Kai is on — and when the host asks for a summary — meeting audio and text go to these processors:

| Processor | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Deepgram | Speech-to-text — transcribes what people say |
| OpenAI | Text-to-speech — gives Kai its voice |
| OpenRouter → Anthropic (Claude) | Writes Kai's answers and the summary |
| OpenRouter → OpenAI | Indexes the shared documents and each question, so Kai finds the right passages |
| OpenRouter → Google (Gemini) | The private tone mirror — only for people who switch it on |

Requests through OpenRouter are sent with data collection turned off, and Deepgram is instructed not to use
meeting audio to improve its models. We never sell your data, and never use meeting content to train models. Payments for credits go through **Paddle**.

## What's kept, and who can see it

Kept with the meeting until the **host deletes the meeting** (or their account):

- the **participant list** — names and when they joined and left;
- **invitee emails** for scheduled meetings;
- the **transcript**, **Kai's questions and answers**, the **documents** and the **summary**;
- **recordings**, until deleted.

<Warning>
    **Everyone in the room can read the full transcript** — of the whole meeting, including earlier sessions —
    and the names of the shared documents. Meeting links don't expire, so this includes anyone who opens the
    link later. Lock or delete the meeting when it's over if that matters.
</Warning>

## Export or delete your data

Both are in your account on burakov.net — **Account → Data & deletion**:

- **Export your data → Download my data** gives you one file with your meetings, transcripts, documents,
    Kai's answers and recording details (not the audio itself — download recordings from each meeting
    page).
- **Delete my account** locks it at once; it's erased after **30 days**. In that time, your meetings can't
    be joined. Remaining credits are forfeited.

**Delete meeting** on a meeting's page removes it permanently — transcript, summary, documents and
recordings included — and closes the room.
