Kai is the AI participant in every Semble room. It's **off** until the host turns it on — until then
nothing is transcribed and nothing is sent to an AI provider.

## Turn Kai on

The host opens the **Kai** tab and taps **Enable** (*Costs 3 credits per 15 min of AI time.*). Kai's tile
changes to *Kai is listening* for everyone. The host taps **Turn off** to stop it. See [Credits](/semble/credits).

<Warning>
    **Leaving the call doesn't turn Kai off.** As the host, tap **Turn off** before you leave, or Kai keeps
    using credits.
</Warning>

## Ask by voice

Start what you say with Kai's name — and keep going in the same breath:

> "Kai, what does the brief say about the budget?"
> "Hey Kai, which option has the lowest risk?"
> "Кай, кто отвечает за ревью безопасности?"

- The name must come **first** — "Kai" or "Кай", optionally after *hey*, *hi*, *ok* (or *эй*, *ок*).
- A bare "Kai?" on its own is ignored — ask your question right after the name.
- Kai answers **out loud** for everyone, and the answer appears in text in everyone's Kai tab, with who
    asked.
- Kai stops speaking as soon as someone starts talking.
- Say "Kai, recap so far" to hear a recap of the recent conversation — out loud and in text.

## Ask in text

- Type a question in the **Kai** tab (up to 4,000 characters; Enter sends) and tap **Ask Kai**.
- Tap **Recap so far** for a short recap.
- In **Chat**, start a message with **@Kai**.

Typed questions, @Kai messages and the **Recap so far** button are answered **in text only** — not out loud.

## What Kai knows

Kai answers from two things:

- the **shared documents** — the passages most relevant to the question (see
    [Share documents with Kai](/semble/documents)), and
- the **last few minutes** of the conversation.

It has no outside knowledge and doesn't read the chat. When the answer isn't in those, it says so rather
than inventing one. It answers in the language of the question. Because it only remembers the last few
minutes, **Recap so far** covers the recent conversation — for the whole meeting, use the
[summary](/semble/transcript-and-summary) afterwards.

## Citations

When an answer draws on the documents, it shows numbered chips like `[1] Budget & Timeline.md`, matching
the numbers in the answer, so everyone can see where a claim came from. The chips list every passage Kai
looked at, so the same document can appear more than once.

## Good to know

- Everyone sees every answer. People who join later see the last 30.
- To keep things fair, each person can ask up to 6 questions and 3 recaps a minute — more shows *A little
    too fast — try again in a moment.*
- Speech is transcribed for up to 6 people talking at the same moment.
- Fair-use limits, such as a daily limit on questions to Kai, may apply.

<Warning>
    Kai's answers are generated automatically and can be incomplete or wrong. They aren't legal, financial or
    medical advice — check anything important yourself.
</Warning>
