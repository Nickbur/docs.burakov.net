Video meetings in Semble are **free** — up to 4 people and 40 minutes per meeting. You pay only for two
optional extras, and only the **host** pays:

| What | Cost |
| --- | --- |
| **Kai** — voice answers, live transcript | **3 credits per 15 minutes** Kai is on |
| **Audio recording** | **5 credits** per recording |
| Documents, typed questions, summaries, the tone mirror | Free |

Turning Kai on also lifts the free limits for that meeting (up to 50 people, no time limit). There's no
subscription and no per-seat pricing — Kai's time costs the same for 3 people or 30.

## Your wallet

Your balance — and roughly how many minutes of Kai it covers — is on the home screen, with your last few
activity entries. Every account gets **12 free credits** (an hour of Kai) the first time it uses Semble.
Credits don't expire.

## Buying credits

| Pack | Credits | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Starter** | 60 | $16 |
| **Plus** | 200 | $40 |
| **Pro** | 600 | $96 |

Prices are in US dollars. In other regions Paddle charges the equivalent local price, which can differ slightly
because of currency conversion and local taxes.

Checkout is handled by **Paddle**, our merchant of record; Semble never sees your card details. Credits
appear in your wallet once the payment clears (*Payment received — credits will appear shortly.* — tap ↻ to
refresh).

## How Kai's time is charged

- **Turning Kai on** charges the first 15-minute block straight away.
- While Kai stays on, the **next block** is charged as each one runs out.
- **Turning Kai off and on again** starts — and charges — a new block.
- Turning Kai off early doesn't refund the current block.
- If your wallet **can't cover the next block**, Kai turns off on its own. You're never charged more than
    your balance.

<Warning>
    **Leaving the call doesn't turn Kai off.** Tap **Turn off** in the Kai tab before you leave.
</Warning>

## Automatic refunds

If Kai can't be started, or a recording fails without producing a file, the credits for it are returned to
your wallet automatically (*Credits returned* in your activity).

## Refunded payments

If the payment for a credit pack is refunded or charged back, the credits it bought are taken back — all of
them for a full refund, the same share for a partial one — even if they were already spent, so your balance
can drop below zero. While it's negative, Kai and recordings can't be started; your next purchase covers the
gap first. If a chargeback is later reversed, the credits come back.

Credits can't be transferred or exchanged for money. Refunds of credit-pack payments follow the Refund Policy
on burakov.net.

<Note>
    Deleting your account forfeits any credits left in your wallet.
</Note>
