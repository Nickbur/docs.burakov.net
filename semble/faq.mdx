<AccordionGroup>
    <Accordion title="Do guests need an account or an app?">
        No. Guests open the link in a browser, type a name, and join. Only the host signs in — with an email
        code, no password.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Is Semble free?">
        Meetings are free for up to 4 people and 40 minutes. Kai costs **3 credits per 15 minutes** it's on —
        and turning it on also lifts the free limits for that meeting. An audio recording costs **5 credits**.
        Only the host pays, from prepaid credits; every account gets 12 free credits. See
        [Credits](/semble/credits).
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Can guests use Kai too?">
        Yes — once the host has turned Kai on, everyone can ask it by voice, in the Kai tab, or with `@Kai` in
        the chat, add documents, and read the live transcript. Answers are shared with the whole room.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Someone joined who shouldn't be there. What can I do?">
        As the host, remove them from their tile and keep **Also lock the meeting** ticked. See
        [Keep the room private](/semble/hosting).
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Does Kai listen all the time?">
        Only while the host has it turned on. When Kai is off, nothing is transcribed and nothing goes to an AI
        provider — except a summary the host asks for afterwards. While it's on, it listens to keep the
        transcript but answers only when someone starts with its name.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Kai didn't answer me. Why?">
        Start with its name and ask in the same breath — "Kai, …" or "Hey Kai, …". Check Kai's tile says *Kai is
        listening*. If the host's credits ran out, Kai turns off on its own. See
        [Troubleshooting](/semble/troubleshooting).
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Can Kai recap the whole meeting?">
        **Recap so far** — or saying "Kai, recap so far" — covers the last few minutes. For the whole meeting,
        generate the summary on the meeting page afterwards.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Which languages does it understand?">
        The app is in English and Russian. Kai's speech recognition handles many languages, and Kai answers in
        the language of the question; its name has to be said as "Kai" or "Кай". Summaries are written in the
        meeting's main language.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Which devices are supported?">
        A modern browser on desktop or mobile — nothing to install. Screen sharing needs a desktop browser.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Can I record video?">
        No — recordings are audio-only: every participant, Kai included, mixed into one `.ogg` file.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Is something wrong with the service?">
        The **Status** link at the bottom of the app shows whether the web app, the API, real-time meetings and
        the AI assistant are working, checked live from your browser.
    </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
