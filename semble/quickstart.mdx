Semble runs in the browser on desktop and mobile — there's nothing to install.

## Host a meeting

<Steps>
    <Step title="Sign in">
        Open [app.semble.app](https://app.semble.app), enter your email and type the 6-digit code you
        receive — no password. Your name in meetings is your account's **username** (you can change it in
        your profile on burakov.net).
    </Step>
    <Step title="Start a meeting">
        Tap **Start an instant meeting** to open a room now, or **Schedule for later** to send invitations —
        see [Start, schedule & manage meetings](/semble/meetings).
    </Step>
    <Step title="Invite people">
        In the room, **Invite** copies the meeting link (or opens your device's share sheet). Anyone with the
        link can join until you lock the meeting.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Bring in Kai">
        Open the **Kai** tab and tap **Enable**. Kai costs 3 credits per 15 minutes — new accounts start with
        12 credits. See [Talk to Kai](/semble/talking-to-kai).
    </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
    To come back to your own meeting, open it from your **home screen** (Upcoming or Recent meetings). Opening
    the meeting's invite link — even your own — joins you as a guest, without the host controls.
</Tip>

## Join as a guest

Open the link you were sent, type the name others should see (up to 60 characters) and tap **Join**.
You join with your **microphone on and camera off** — your browser asks for microphone access as you
join, and for the camera when you first turn it on.

Guests are full participants: they can ask Kai, add documents, read the live transcript and chat.

<Tip>
    If you hear nothing after joining, look for **Tap to enable sound** — some browsers block audio until you
    interact with the page once.
</Tip>
