You join with your **microphone on and camera off**.

## The control bar

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Speaker** | Mutes everyone else — for you only. |
| **Mic** | Turns your microphone on or off. |
| **Camera** | Turns your camera on or off (your browser asks for permission the first time). |
| **Switch camera** | Switches between cameras, when you have more than one. |
| **More** | **Raise hand**, **Reactions**, **Blur background**, **Share screen** and **Audio & video**. |
| **Leave** | Leaves the call. The room stays open for the others. |

- **Reactions** — 👍 👎 😂 ❤️ 🎉, shown on your tile for three seconds.
- **Blur background** — available while your camera is on.
- **Share screen** — on desktop browsers. In Chrome and Edge you can share a tab's audio too. Phones
    don't support screen sharing.
- **Audio & video** — pick your camera, microphone and, where the browser allows (Chrome, Edge),
    speaker.

## Tiles

Click a tile to pin it large; click again to return to the grid. You can mirror your own video and switch
a tile between **Fill** and **Fit**. Video quality adapts automatically to each person's connection; a badge
shows each person's connection quality, and a short message tells you when someone joins or leaves.

## Chat

The **Chat** tab is a text chat with everyone in the room (up to 1,000 characters per message) — handy for
links and names. Messages go straight to the people in the call and are **never stored**, so someone who
joins later doesn't see earlier messages. Start a message with **@Kai** to ask Kai (see
[Talk to Kai](/semble/talking-to-kai)).

## The private tone mirror

In the **Kai** tab, anyone can switch on **Show my tone**. While Kai is on, Semble checks the tone of what
you say (sentences of at least 15 characters), and if it sounds sarcastic, surprised or sharp, a small badge
— *🌀 Sarcasm*, *❗ Surprise* or *⚠️ Sharp* — appears on **your own tile, for you only**, for a few seconds.
Nobody else sees it, nothing is stored, and it costs no credits. It's off by default.
