A host can record a meeting's **audio** — every participant, Kai included, mixed into one file. Recording
is off until the host starts it, and it's never silent: from the moment it starts, **everyone sees** *This
meeting is being recorded (audio only).*

## Record

<Steps>
    <Step title="Tap Record">
        In the room header, the host taps **Record**. *Record this meeting?* explains what happens and what it
        costs.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Confirm">
        **Start recording** begins capturing audio, and the notice appears for everyone.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Stop">
        Tap **Stop recording** — or it stops on its own when the room closes.
    </Step>
</Steps>

You can make **several recordings** in one meeting. Recording doesn't lift the free-meeting limits — only
turning Kai on does.

## Download or delete

The meeting page lists each recording with its start time, length and size, and a status: **Recording…**,
**Finishing…**, **Ready**, or *Failed — credits refunded*.

- **Download** saves an `.ogg` audio file named `semble-YYYY-MM-DD-HHMM.ogg` (in UTC). The download link
    works for 15 minutes — tap again for a new one.
- **Delete** removes a finished recording permanently.

Recordings are kept until you delete them or the meeting.

## Cost

Each recording costs **5 credits**, charged when it starts. If a recording fails without producing a file,
the credits go back to your wallet automatically.

<Warning>
    Recording laws differ by place. Semble always tells everyone in the room when recording is on, but you're
    responsible for any additional consent your local law requires.
</Warning>
