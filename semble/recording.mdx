---
title: "Recording"
description: "Optional audio recording — everyone is told, nothing is silent."
---

A host can record a meeting's **audio** — every participant, Kai included, mixed into one file.
Recording is always off until the host starts it, and it's never silent: the moment it starts,
**every participant sees a "This meeting is being recorded" banner**, guests included.

## Record a meeting

<Steps>
    <Step title="Press Record">
        In the room header, the host presses **Record**. Semble shows what will happen and what it
        costs before anything starts.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Confirm">
        **Start recording** begins capturing audio, and the recording banner appears for everyone.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Stop">
        Press **Stop recording** — or just end the meeting; the recording finishes on its own once
        everyone has left and the room closes.
    </Step>
</Steps>

## Download or delete

The meeting's page lists its recordings with their length and size. **Download** saves the
audio as an `.ogg` file; **Delete** removes it permanently from storage.

## Cost and refunds

Each recording costs a flat **5 credits**, charged when it starts. If a recording fails and no
usable file is produced, those credits go back to your wallet automatically.

<Warning>
    Recording laws differ by place. Semble always tells everyone in the room when recording is on,
    but you're responsible for any additional consent your local law requires.
</Warning>
