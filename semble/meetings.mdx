## Start an instant meeting

On the home screen, tap **Start an instant meeting**. You're taken straight into a new room as its
**host**, with the summary style set to **Team meeting**.

## Schedule for later

Tap **Schedule for later** and fill in:

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Title** | Up to 200 characters. |
| **When** | A date and time in your local time zone, in the future. |
| **Summary style** | How the summary will be written — General, Team, Learning or Sales. |
| **Invite by email (optional)** | Up to 50 addresses. If one is malformed, nothing is sent — fix it and try again. |

Tap **Schedule & send invites**. Each invitee gets an email (*You're invited: …*) with the join link and a
calendar file, and a reminder (*Starting soon: …*) about 30 minutes before the start. The emails show the
time in the invitee's own time zone and language if they have an account, otherwise in yours.

<Note>
    You, as the host, don't get an email. A scheduled meeting can't be edited — to change it, delete it and
    schedule a new one. Invitees aren't told when a meeting is deleted.
</Note>

## Your meetings

The home screen lists **Upcoming** and **Recent meetings** (up to 100), each **Not started**,
**Scheduled**, **Live now** or **Ended**. Open one to see its **meeting page**: **Open room** to go back in,
the summary, transcript and recordings, and **Delete meeting**.

## How links work

- A meeting's link **doesn't expire**. Anyone with it can open the room at any time — before you arrive,
  or days after the call — unless you've **locked** the meeting, deleted it, or a free meeting has used up
  its time.
- There's no waiting room: people with the link join directly. Use **Lock** once everyone's in — see
  [Keep the room private](/semble/hosting).
- **Leaving** the call doesn't close the room for others. **Delete meeting** is the way to close it for
  everyone and remove everything it holds.

## Free meetings

A meeting is free for **up to 4 people** (Kai and the recorder don't count) and **40 minutes**, counted from
when the room first started — the clock doesn't reset if people leave and come back. You can run **one**
free meeting at a time: schedule as many as you like, but while one is live, Semble offers to take you back
to it instead of opening another. A free meeting that has used up its time can't be reopened.

Turning Kai on in a meeting — even once — lifts both limits for that meeting: up to **50 people**, with no
time limit. See [Credits](/semble/credits).
