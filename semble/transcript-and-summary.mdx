## Live transcript

While Kai is on, what people say is transcribed and **attributed to the person who said it**. The
**Transcript** tab shows it live to **everyone in the room** — the whole meeting so far, including earlier
sessions of the same meeting — so a late joiner can catch up. Kai's own answers and the chat aren't
included.

The transcript is captured **only while Kai is on**.

## After the call

The host finds everything on the **meeting page** (open the meeting from the home screen):

- **Transcript** — tap to expand the full transcript.
- **Summary** — see below.

There's no download button; to keep the transcript, select and copy it.

## The summary

Pick the meeting's **summary style** and tap **Generate summary**. Semble writes one summary for the meeting
from its transcript, in the language the meeting was mostly held in:

| Style | Sections |
| --- | --- |
| **General meeting** | Key points, Decisions, Points of agreement, Open questions, Action items |
| **Team meeting** | Key points, Decisions, Action items (owner — task), Open questions |
| **Learning session** | Topics covered, Key takeaways, Questions raised, Next steps / homework |
| **Sales call** | Customer needs, Objections raised, Commitments, Next steps |

Empty sections are left out. The summary covers the meeting as a whole, and shows which AI model wrote it.
**Regenerate** replaces it with a new one. A copy is **emailed to you** each time, and you can also send it to
other email addresses — recipients with an account get it in their own language, others in yours. Generating a
summary costs no credits.

<Note>
    A summary needs a transcript, so Kai must have been on at least once during the meeting. Otherwise you'll
    see *No transcript to summarize yet.*
</Note>
