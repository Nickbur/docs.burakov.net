title: "Share documents with Kai"
description: "Add documents to a Semble meeting so Kai can ground its answers in them — formats, limits, who sees what, and removing them."
mode: "wide"
---

Every meeting has **one shared pool of documents**. Anyone in the room — host or guest — can add to it,
and Kai looks things up in it when answering.

## Add a document

In the meeting panel, open **Docs** and either:

- **choose a file** (or drop it on the Docs tab) — plain text (`.txt`) or Markdown (`.md`), or
- **paste text** and give it a name (up to 300 characters).

Then tap **Add to shared docs**.

| Limit | |
| --- | --- |
| File size | Up to 400 KB |
| Length | About 200,000 characters per document |
| Documents per meeting | 50 |
| Uploads | 10 per 10 minutes per person |

<Tip>
    Short, focused documents work best: an agenda, a brief, a proposal, the numbers you'll discuss. Give each
    a clear name — it's what shows in the citation chips. Only `.txt` and `.md` files are read properly; save
    other documents as plain text first.
</Tip>

## Who sees what

Everyone sees the **names** of the documents in the pool — not their contents. Your own are marked *yours*.
A document shows *waits for Kai* while Kai is off and *preparing…* while it's being made ready.

## How Kai uses them

When someone asks a question, Kai finds the passages most relevant to it and combines them with the last
few minutes of conversation. Answers cite the passages with numbered chips. See
[Talk to Kai](/semble/talking-to-kai).

## Removing documents

Only the **host** can remove a document — with the trash icon in the Docs list. Guests can't remove
documents, including their own.

## Where they live

Documents belong to that meeting and stay with it across sessions — they're never used for another meeting
or to train models. They're deleted when the host deletes the meeting.
