The host is whoever created the meeting. Besides turning [Kai](/semble/talking-to-kai) and
[recording](/semble/recording) on and off, the host keeps the room in order.

## Lock the meeting

The **lock** button in the room header stops anyone new from joining with the link; people already in the
meeting stay. Tap it again to unlock.

The lock **stays on the meeting** — it still applies the next time the room opens. While it's locked,
someone who drops out or reloads the page can't get back in until you unlock it.

## Remove a guest

Hover a guest's tile and tap **✕** (on touch screens it's always shown). They're disconnected right away.
The confirmation offers **Also lock the meeting**, ticked by default — leave it ticked to keep them out, since
anyone with the link could otherwise rejoin (for example under another name).

The host can't be removed.

## Remove a document

In the **Docs** tab, the host can remove any document with the trash icon.

## Free-meeting limits

| | Free meeting | After Kai has been turned on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| People (Kai and the recorder don't count) | Up to 4 | Up to 50 |
| Length | 40 minutes from when the room first started | No limit |
| Free meetings live at once | 1 per host | — |

In the last five minutes of a free meeting, everyone sees a countdown; the host's banner has a **Turn on
Kai** button that opens the Kai panel. Turning Kai on — even briefly — lifts the limits for that meeting for
good. When a free meeting reaches 40 minutes it ends, and its link stops working.
