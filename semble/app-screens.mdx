## Screens

| Screen | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** (signed out) | What Semble is, and signing in with an email code. |
| **Home** (signed in) | **Start an instant meeting**, **Schedule for later**, your **Upcoming** and **Recent meetings**, and your **AI credits** card. |
| **Join** | A guest types a name and taps **Join**. |
| **Call** | The meeting itself — see below. |
| **Meeting page** | One meeting's status, **Open room**, its summary, transcript and recordings, and **Delete meeting**. Host only. |
| **Status** | *System status* — the live state of the web app, the API, real-time meetings and the AI assistant, with **Refresh**. |

The header has a language switcher and **Sign out**; the footer links to these docs, the Privacy Policy,
Terms and Status.

## The call screen

- **Header** — the call timer, the participant count, **Invite**, the **Kai** button (with a badge for
    unread answers) and, for the host, **Lock** and **Record**.
- **Tiles** — everyone's video; click one to pin it large, click again to go back to the grid.
- **Controls** — Speaker, Mic, Camera, Switch camera (when you have several), **More** and **Leave** —
    see [Use the call controls](/semble/in-the-call).
- **Banners** — the recording notice, the free-meeting countdown, and connection messages
    (*Connecting…*, *Reconnecting…*).

## The meeting panel

The **Kai** button opens a side panel with four tabs:

| Tab | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Kai** | Turn Kai on or off (host), ask a question, **Recap so far**, the answers, and **Show my tone**. |
| **Chat** | A text chat with everyone in the room. |
| **Docs** | The meeting's shared documents. |
| **Transcript** | The live transcript while Kai is on. |

## Settings you can change

| Setting | Options | Remembered |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Language** | English, Русский — starts from your browser's language | In the browser, and on your account (for emails) |
| **Summary style** | General meeting, Team meeting, Learning session, Sales call | Per meeting |
| **Show my tone** | Off / On | In the browser |
| **Devices, background blur, mirror my video, fit or fill** | — | For this call |
| **Lock**, **Kai**, **Record** | Host only | Lock stays on the meeting until unlocked |
