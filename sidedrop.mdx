Sidedrop is a **peer-to-peer file transfer** tool that runs in your browser. Open it on two devices, pair
them with a link, a short code or a QR code, and drop files, folders or text — the data streams
**directly between the two browsers**, encrypted, and is never uploaded to a server.

## What the server does — and doesn't

A small **signaling server** introduces the two browsers: it creates rooms, matches codes to rooms, passes
along the messages the browsers need to connect, and — when a network blocks a direct connection — gives
them temporary access to a relay. It never receives your files, their names or
their sizes — those travel only over the direct, encrypted connection between the two browsers. There's
no account and no analytics.

<Note>
    Sidedrop isn't cloud storage: **both tabs need to stay open** while a transfer runs. There's no "send
    now, pick up later".
</Note>
