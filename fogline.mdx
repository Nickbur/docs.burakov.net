Fogline turns the roads you travel into a **personal "fog of war" map of the real world**. Tap **Start** once,
then just live your life — drive or walk, and the phone **paints in every road you cover** in the background,
while everywhere you haven't been stays fogged. Watch your coverage climb as the blank streets close up. It's
free, on **iPhone, iPad and Android**.

<Note>
    **Where it paints:** Fogline has road data for **Kyiv**, the **Kyiv region** and the other regions of
    Ukraine. It picks your region from where you are — see [Regions & offline maps](/fogline/offline-maps).
</Note>

## The idea in one minute

<Steps>
    <Step title="Tap Start">
        Open Fogline and tap **Start** on the map — once. From then on it follows where you go, even with the
        screen off or after you reopen the app, until you tap **Pause**.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Drive or walk">
        Every road you travel along is matched to the map and painted in, a stretch at a time.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Watch it fill in">
        Painted roads glow on the map, the rest stays fogged, and your **% coverage** grows.
    </Step>
    <Step title="It follows you">
        Your painted roads back up to your account and appear on your other devices.
    </Step>
</Steps>
