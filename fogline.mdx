Fogline turns the roads you travel into a **personal "fog of war" map of the real world**. You just
live your life — drive and walk — and the phone **paints in every road you cover**, while everywhere
you haven't been yet stays fogged. There's nothing to start and nothing to log: it works quietly in
the background, and you watch your city fill in.

<Note>
  Fogline is a personal project — **free, no subscriptions**. It covers Kyiv and the surrounding
  oblast today, with other regions available as downloadable map packs.
</Note>

## The idea in one minute

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install it and go about your day">
    Install Fogline, sign in, and download your region. Then just live your life — there's nothing to
    press before a trip and nothing to log after one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let it paint in the background">
    As you drive or walk, the phone quietly records where you travel — screen off, app closed. Every
    road you cover gets snapped to the map and painted in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch your city fill in">
    Open the app to see your live map: painted roads where you've been, fog where you haven't, and
    your **% coverage** climbing as the blank spaces close up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="It stays yours">
    Your raw movements never leave your phone — only the set of painted roads backs up to your
    account, so your map is safe and follows you to a new device.
  </Step>
</Steps>
