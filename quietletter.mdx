Quietletter is a **digital dead man's switch**. You set up what should happen if something
happens to you — a farewell message, an encrypted file for your family, account credentials,
or a private note — and keep a simple periodic **check-in** to prove you're still around.
Miss it, and only after repeated warnings and a grace period of at least 48 hours, your switch
fires exactly as you set it.

It runs on **iPhone, Android and the web**, as a hosted service — or on your own server with the
open-source core.

## The idea in one minute

<Steps>
    <Step title="Create a switch">
        Give it a title, choose how often you'll check in and how long the grace period is, add the
        people who should receive it, and compose what they'll get.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Check in">
        Open the app and confirm you're here with your PIN. Each check-in resets the deadline — weekly,
        monthly, or every few months, as you choose.
    </Step>
    <Step title="If you ever stop">
        After the deadline passes, the switch enters its grace period and warns you by push and email
        (and SMS on Pro). Only if you still don't check in by the end of the grace period does it fire.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Your people receive it">
        Each recipient gets an email. Readable messages are right in it; encrypted items open on a
        private web page with the passphrase you shared with them ahead of time.
    </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
    Quietletter is designed to **fire when it should and never when it shouldn't**: a grace period of
    at least 48 hours, warnings on more than one channel, and a fire decision made on the server, so a
    phone that's offline or lost doesn't matter either way.
</Note>
