The dashboard at [app.rowbots.dev](https://app.rowbots.dev) is **mission control for what git doesn't have**. Pull
requests themselves live — and are merged — in your own git host; the dashboard is where you hand over work, make
decisions and keep an eye on the fleet.

It's built for the desktop, works on a phone, and can be **installed as an app** from the browser, with push
notifications — handy for approving a batch or answering an escalation in seconds. The interface is in English and
Russian.

## Sections

| Section | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Inbox** | Hand over tasks, ideas and feedback — text, screenshots and files — and follow every work item and its stage (backlog, scheduled, planning, building, judging, pull request, merged, measuring) on its **Work** tab. See [Feeding it work](/rowbots/feeding-work). |
| **Merge queue** | Everything waiting for your merge, ranked by risk and confidence: approve safe changes as a batch, open high-stakes ones individually. See [Review, merge & escalations](/rowbots/review-and-merge). |
| **Questions** | The decisions only you can make, each with options and Rowbots' recommendation. |
| **Projects** | All your projects side by side: activity, health and where the budget goes. |
| **Budget** | Spend against the monthly cap, the month's forecast and its odds. See [Budget & forecasts](/rowbots/budget). |
| **Constitution** | Your confirmed rules — product invariants, taste, priorities — with their history. Changes are confirmed through the interview. |
| **Track record** | Rowbots' measured results for your workspace: merge rate, how much merged work survives, escaped defects, forecast accuracy, cost per merged result, the attention it asks of you. |
| **Audit** | Every privileged action and every decision, with who made it. |
| **Connections** | Repositories, model providers, compute, channels and allowed MCP tools. See [Set up a workspace](/rowbots/setup). |
| **Members** | Invite people and set their roles. See [Team & permissions](/rowbots/team-and-permissions). |
| **Plan** | Your plan and invoices. See [Pricing](/rowbots/pricing). |
| **Settings** | Workspace settings — the language of plans and pull requests, commit authorship, attention budget, notification defaults, data export — and your **Account**, including **Delete Account**. |
| **Notifications** (the bell) | Everything Rowbots told you, and the channels it reaches you on. |

What you can open depends on your [role](/rowbots/team-and-permissions).

## Track record

The track record is computed from reality — your git history, your git host and the judge's results — never from
opinion:

| Measure | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| **Merge rate** | How much of Rowbots' work you merge without editing it |
| **Survival** | How much merged work is still there after 30 and 90 days |
| **Escape rate** | How many merged changes later turned out defective |
| **Forecast calibration** | How often Rowbots' probabilities came true |
| **Cost per merged result** | Model and compute spend per merged pull request, as a trend |
| **Attention cost** | Your minutes per merged pull request, and escalations per week |
