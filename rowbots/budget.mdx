## A hard monthly cap

You set a **monthly spending cap** for your workspace. It covers the model usage Rowbots meters on your
own keys, and it is an absolute wall: Rowbots never goes over it and never silently drops work to stay
under it.

- **It plans the month against the cap** and tells you up front whether the work you want fits.
- **When the plan stops fitting**, it re-prioritizes and warns you early, with a choice: "I'll finish A
  and B; C won't fit — drop C or raise the cap?"
- **There's no hidden emergency reserve.** If urgent work hits an exhausted cap, you raise the cap or
  re-prioritize.
- **It saves on models before it cuts scope** — moving routine work to cheaper models first.
  Verification is never weakened to save money.

Compute is tracked alongside: if you use a managed sandbox provider that reports usage, Rowbots forecasts
it too, and you can cap it as well. A [self-hosted runner](/rowbots/self-hosted-runner) runs on your own
machine and isn't metered.

## Honest forecasts

Estimates are **probabilities, not promises** — for example, "70% the budget suffices; delivery in about
two months". Each forecast covers the whole pipeline, from planning to judging and fixes, and is grounded
in what similar work actually cost before.

Rowbots **publishes its own track record**: how often its past forecasts came true. When it has little
history with your projects, it says plainly that its confidence is low.

## Plans

Pricing is a **flat monthly fee by the number of active projects** — never per token, per pull request or
per task. Rowbots makes money from running your team well, not from spending more.

| Plan | Monthly | Annual | Active projects | Mode |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Free** | $0 | — | 1 | task-triggered only |
| **Solo** | $49 | $490 | 1 | always-on |
| **Studio** | $199 | $1,990 | 5 | always-on |
| **Fleet** | $499 | $4,990 | 15 | always-on |
| **Agency** | $1,299 | $12,990 | 50, then $25 per extra project a month | always-on, priority |

- **Every plan** is bring-your-own-keys and bring-your-own-compute — your model and sandbox usage is
  billed to you directly by those providers, at their prices.
- **Paid plans** add always-on maintenance, shared memory across your fleet, unlimited members and email
  support. Work runs in parallel up to a fair-use ceiling of 32 items per workspace.
- **Free** runs one item at a time, for one member, with community support.
- **Agency** also gets priority throughput and priority support.

<Tip>
    New workspaces get a **14-day Studio trial with no card**, starting when you connect your first git
    account. One trial per git account; afterwards the workspace moves to Free unless you subscribe.
</Tip>

Subscriptions are handled by **Paddle**, our Merchant of Record, which also issues invoices; prices are
in USD and annual billing costs ten months. You can change the payment method, see invoices and cancel
from Paddle's customer portal. Current prices are always listed on the
[pricing page](https://burakov.net/projects/rowbots/pricing).

## Downgrades and failed payments

- **Downgrading** below your number of active projects pauses the extra ones — they become read-only,
  nothing is deleted — and you choose which stay active.
- **A failed renewal** keeps everything working for a 7-day grace period while the payment is retried;
  after that, work pauses until the subscription is active again.
