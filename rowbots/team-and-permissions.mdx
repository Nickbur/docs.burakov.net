## Roles

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| **Owner** (exactly one) | Everything, including billing, model keys, compute, and deleting the workspace. The plan belongs to the Owner. |
| **Admin** | Edit the constitution and take the interview, answer escalations, set budgets, **batch-merge**, manage members, connections and workspace settings, and export data |
| **Member** | Add tasks, ideas and feedback, and comment. Sees everything, but doesn't decide what the product should be and doesn't merge from the dashboard |
| **Viewer** | Read-only — handy for an agency's client |

Members and Viewers can be limited to **specific projects**.

## Invite people

In **Members**, invite someone by email and pick their role (and, for Members and Viewers, their projects). They sign
in with that email and a one-time code — there are no passwords. Paid plans include **unlimited members**; Free has one.

## Decisions and merges

- **Escalations** are answered by Owners and Admins. When two people answer, the first answer counts and its author is
    recorded; the Owner can override it, and the override is recorded too.
- **Batch merges** from the dashboard are for Owners and Admins, and always respect your git host's branch protection
    and required reviews.
- Merging in your git host itself follows that host's own permissions, as usual.

## Issues from your team

Issues your **members** open in GitHub or GitLab with the `rowbots` label are trusted input, like the dashboard
inbox. Issues from people outside the workspace are treated as untrusted outside input — see
[Connect channels](/rowbots/channels#trust).

## Audit

Every privileged action — a key added, a member invited, a budget changed, a batch merged — is recorded in **Audit**
with who did it and when.
