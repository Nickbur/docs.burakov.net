Pricing is a **flat monthly fee by the number of active projects** — never per token, per pull request or per task.
Rowbots makes money from running your team well, not from spending more.

## Plans

| Plan | Monthly | Annual | Active projects | Mode | Parallel work | Members | Support |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Free** | $0 | — | 1 | task-triggered only | 1 item | 1 | community (GitHub Discussions) |
| **Solo** | $49 | $490 | 1 | always-on | up to 32 items | unlimited | email |
| **Studio** | $199 | $1,990 | 5 | always-on | up to 32 items | unlimited | email |
| **Fleet** | $499 | $4,990 | 15 | always-on | up to 32 items | unlimited | email |
| **Agency** | $1,299 | $12,990 | 50, then $25 per extra project a month | always-on | up to 32 items, priority throughput | unlimited | priority |

- **Every plan** is bring-your-own-keys and bring-your-own-compute: model and sandbox usage is billed to you directly
    by those providers, at their prices, and kept in check by your [monthly cap](/rowbots/budget).
- **Paid plans** add always-on [maintenance](/rowbots/feeding-work#when-nothing-is-queued), work in parallel up to a
    fair-use ceiling of 32 items per workspace, opt-in global memory (anonymous patterns pooled across teams),
    unlimited members and email support.
- **Free** works only on the tasks you give it, one item at a time, for one member.
- **Annual billing** costs ten months.

## Free trial

New workspaces get **14 days of Studio, with no card**, starting when you connect your first git account. There's
one trial per git account. Afterwards the workspace moves to Free unless you subscribe.

## Billing

Subscriptions are handled by **Paddle**, our Merchant of Record, which charges in **USD** and issues invoices. The
**Owner** manages the plan in the dashboard under **Plan** — upgrade or downgrade (prorated), change the payment
method, see invoices or cancel through Paddle's customer portal. Current prices are always listed on the
[project page](https://burakov.net/projects/rowbots).

## Downgrades and failed payments

- **Downgrading** below your number of active projects pauses the extra ones — they become read-only, nothing is
    deleted — and you choose which stay active.
- **A failed renewal** keeps everything working for a **7-day grace period** while the payment is retried; after that,
    work pauses until the subscription is active again.
