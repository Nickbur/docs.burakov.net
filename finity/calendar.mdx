The **Calendar** ties your countdowns, milestones and photos together — a single screen for "what is
this week about?".

## The month view

Each month shows a **dot** under every day that has something on it:

- a **countdown** occurrence — from about two months back to over a year ahead, including every
    repeat,
- a **milestone** from your [life in weeks](/finity/life-in-weeks), or
- a **photo** from your [journal](/finity/journal) that's dated to that day.

**Today** jumps back to the current month.

## A day's page

Tap a day to open it. At the top, Finity shows where that day sits in your life — your stage of life,
which **week** of your life it is, and whether it's behind you, this week or still ahead. Below are
three tabs: **Countdowns**, **Events** (milestones) and **Photos**.

You can add a milestone to that day right there, or delete one.

<Note>
    The life context and adding milestones to a specific day need your **date of birth** in
    [Life](/finity/life-in-weeks). With only your age, Finity works in whole weeks instead of days.
</Note>
