## Journal

The journal asks for very little — **a few lines a day** and a photo if you have one. A ritual you
can finish in thirty seconds is one you'll actually keep.

- **Weekly recap** — once a week, AI can summarize what you wrote: the shape of the week, not just
  its contents.
- **Year in review** — at year's end, those weeks are stitched into a single story. *(Pro.)*

## Character

Your **Character** is an RPG-style profile of your life that only ever goes up. Instead of a streak
you're afraid to break, it grows from what you actually do — the countdowns you keep, the weeks you
annotate, the entries you make — becoming **levels, attributes and achievements**.

- **Character reading** — on Pro, AI offers a short, honest read of your profile: what you've been
  leaning into, and what you've been neglecting.

<Note>
  The AI features — weekly recaps, the year-in-review, and character readings — are part of **Pro**.
</Note>
