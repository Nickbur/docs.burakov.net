## One account, every device

You sign in once with a **passwordless email code** — no password to forget. The same account works
across every app in the burakov line, and it carries your Finity data across **phone, web and
desktop**.

## Offline-first

Everything you see lives in a **local database on your device** and works with **no connection**.
Sync happens quietly in the background: when you're online, your data reconciles to your account and
appears on your other devices; when you're offline, you keep working and it catches up later.

## What stays private

Your data starts on your device and syncs only to **your own account**. Signing out clears the local
data on that device. Notifications are handled locally by default; email reminders (Pro) are sent
from the server only for the countdowns you've asked to be reminded about.

## Lock the app

On iOS and Android you can turn on **Settings → Privacy & security → Lock with Face ID /
fingerprint**. Finity then asks for your face or fingerprint when it opens and after 5 minutes in
the background (on iPhone your device passcode works too). The check is done by your phone itself —
nothing about your face or fingerprint ever reaches the app or our servers.
