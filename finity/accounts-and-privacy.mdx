## Sign in

You sign in with your **email and a one-time code** — no password. If you don't have an account yet,
signing in creates one. You can also use **Sign in with Google**, and on iPhone
**Sign in with Apple**. You can ask for a new code after 90 seconds; after five wrong codes in a row, Finity makes you wait five minutes.

It's one account across the apps at [burakov.net](https://burakov.net), and it carries your Finity
data to every device you sign in on.

## What works offline

Finity keeps your data in a **local database on your device**, so you can browse and edit your
countdowns, chapters, milestones and journal with **no connection**. When you're back online — or
when you open the app — it syncs in the background. If the same thing was changed on two devices, the
most recent change wins.

Some things need a connection: signing in, saving your Life answers, uploading photos, generating an
AI summary yourself, character reads, opening your Character, and purchases. Automatic weekly
summaries are written on our server and arrive with the next sync.

## Signing out and switching accounts

**Log out** (in the menu) removes Finity's data from that device straight away, without asking —
including changes that haven't synced yet. Go online and let Finity sync before you log out.

If someone signs in with a different account on the same device, the previous account's data is
removed from it first.

## What leaves your device

| Data | Goes to | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Countdowns, chapters, milestones, journal entries, your Life answers | Your Finity account on our server | When it syncs |
| A smaller copy of each journal photo | Your Finity account on our server | When you add it — the original never leaves your device |
| The text of a week you wrote in | An AI provider | When the week ends, or when you tap **Generate summary** |
| The entries of a year | An AI provider | Only when you generate your year in review (Pro) |
| Your character's numbers (never your journal text) | An AI provider | Only when you reveal your character read |
| Your rough location | A location lookup service, to find your country | Only when you tap **Detect** |
| Purchase details | The App Store, Google Play or the web payment provider | When you subscribe |
| Error details with an account identifier (not your name or email), your time zone, and occasionally a recording of the app screen with all text masked and images hidden | A crash-reporting service | Only if **Send crash reports** is on (it is by default) — turn it off in Settings |
| An account identifier and this device's push address | A push notification service | After you sign in (iPhone, Android and the web) |
| Your sign-in request | Google or Apple | Only if you choose **Sign in with Google** / **Sign in with Apple** |

Email reminders (Pro) are sent from our server, only for the countdowns where you turned them on.

## Lock the app

On iPhone and Android you can turn on **Settings → Privacy & security → Lock with Face ID /
fingerprint**. Finity then asks for your face or fingerprint when it opens and after 5 minutes in the
background (on iPhone your device passcode works too). Turning the lock on or off needs a successful
check too. The check is done by your phone itself — nothing about your face or fingerprint ever
reaches the app or our servers.

## Export or delete your data

Both are in the app — **Account → Account settings** — and in your account on burakov.net —
**Account → Data & deletion**:

- **Export my data** in the app gives you one file with your account and your Finity data. On
    burakov.net, **Export your data → Download my data** gives you one file with your data from every
    app.
- **Delete account** (in the app) or **Delete my account** (on burakov.net) signs you out at once and
    removes your data from **every** app on the
    account. You can change your mind for **30 days**: sign in again and choose **Restore my
    account**. After that, the data is erased for good.

<Warning>
    Deleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription bought through the App Store or Google Play.
    Cancel it there first.
</Warning>
