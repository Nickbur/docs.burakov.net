Life in weeks shows your **entire life as a grid** — one square per week, 40 to a row. It's a
memento-mori picture, built to give perspective rather than dread.

## Set it up

The first time you open **Life**, answer three questions:

- **Gender** — Male, Female or Other.
- **Date of birth or age?** — your **birth date**, or just your **age** (1–130). A birth date makes
    everything more precise, and the calendar's life context and day-dated milestones and photos need
    it.
- **Country** — pick it from the list, or tap **Detect** to use your device's location (Finity asks
    for location permission the first time). Only the country is kept.

Saving needs a connection. You can change your answers later — if you change your date of birth, your
milestones, photos and journal days move to the matching weeks everywhere in the app.

## Reading the grid

The grid is sized by the **average life expectancy in your country** (73 years if there's no figure
for it), so its length is grounded rather than arbitrary. Above it:

- **Lived** — weeks behind you,
- **Left** — weeks ahead, and
- **Total** — the whole grid,

with a note of the average it's based on. It's a canvas, not a verdict — the point is perspective.
The current week is always ringed.

### Life stages

Squares are tinted by stage of life:

| Stage | Ages |
| --- | --- |
| Childhood | 0–11 |
| Adolescence | 12–17 |
| Young adult | 18–44 |
| Middle age | 45–59 |
| Elderly | 60–74 |
| Old age | 75–89 |
| Long-livers | 90+ |

### Map layers

**Map layers** switch parts of the picture on and off: **Lived / not lived**, **WHO periods** (the
stages above), **Custom periods** (your chapters), **Events** and **Photos**. All are on to start
with; your choice is remembered on each device.

Tap any square to see its stage, your age and any chapters it belongs to, and to open that week with
its milestones and photos.

## Mark the chapters of your life

Chapters — school, a city, a relationship, a job — are **custom periods** on the grid:

<Steps>
    <Step title="Select the weeks">
        Tap **Selection** and drag across the grid. It scrolls as you drag and shows the size of your
        selection in weeks, months or years.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Name it">
        Give it a name (up to 120 characters) and pick one of 10 colours, then **Save zone**.
    </Step>
</Steps>

Rename, recolour or delete chapters under **Your zones**. Where chapters overlap, the newest one is
shown on top.

## Add milestones

Milestones are the moments that made a week matter. Tap **Add event** (or **Add event here** on a
week):

- a **label** (up to 120 characters),
- one of 12 **icons** and 10 **colours**, and
- a **date** — a specific **day** if you gave your birth date, otherwise a **week** of your life.

A week can hold several milestones. Each shows how long ago it was (or how long until it), and you
can edit or delete it any time.

## Photos

Photos you add to your [journal](/finity/journal) appear on their weeks in the grid too, so
scrolling back becomes a memory walk instead of an abstract count.

<Note>
    On the free plan you can have **1 chapter** and **3 milestones**; Pro removes the limits. Nothing
    you already have is removed. See [Pricing](/finity/pricing).
</Note>
