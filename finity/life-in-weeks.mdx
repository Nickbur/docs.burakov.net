Life in weeks shows your **entire life as a grid** — one square per week. It's a memento-mori
picture, built to give perspective rather than dread.

## A realistic horizon

The grid is seeded with **regional life-expectancy data**, so its length is grounded rather than
arbitrary. It's a canvas, not a verdict — the point is perspective, not a countdown to fear.

## Layers

Add **layers** for the chapters of your life — school, a city, a relationship, a career — and the
weeks stop being anonymous. You can see, at a glance, how long each chapter really was.

## Events & photos

Pin **events and photos** to specific weeks. Scrolling back becomes a memory walk instead of an
abstract count — and the same week can be both a dot on your lifetime and a page in your journal.
