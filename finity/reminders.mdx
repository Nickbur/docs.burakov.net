Each countdown has its own reminders, set in the **Notifications** part of its form. There are two
channels:

| Channel | Sent by | Default | Plan |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Push** | Your device itself (a local notification) | On, **at the event time** | Every plan |
| **Email** | The Finity server, to your account email | Off | **Pro** |

## When you're reminded

For each channel, choose any combination of:

- **At the event time**, and
- **Notify before** — an amount (from 1 up to 1,000) of **minutes, hours, day(s) or week(s)** ahead.

"1 day before" lands at the same clock time on the day before, in the countdown's own time zone;
minutes and hours are exact.

## What a reminder looks like

A push reminder shows the countdown's title and its description (or just *Reminder*). An early one
reads *Coming up: …*. Tapping it opens the countdown. On Android, reminders use their own
**Reminders** notification channel, which you can fine-tune in your phone's settings.

Email reminders show the time in the countdown's time zone, in the language you use Finity in.

## Allow notifications

Finity asks for permission to send notifications after you first sign in — or, if it hasn't yet, the
first time you save a countdown. If you decline, you'll see *Turn on notifications in system settings to get reminders.* — turn them on for
Finity in your phone's or computer's settings.

## Limits worth knowing

- **Push reminders are scheduled on your device.** Finity keeps the next **6 occurrences** of each
    countdown and up to **60 reminders in total** queued (the soonest ones win), and tops them up
    every time you open the app. For a countdown that repeats very often, open Finity now and then so
    the queue doesn't run dry.
- **On the web and desktop**, push reminders only fire **while Finity is open**. Use the phone app,
    or email reminders, for anything you can't miss.
- **Email reminders need an active Pro plan** when they're due. If Pro ends, email reminders stop;
    push reminders carry on.

<Tip>
    For the one date you really can't miss, turn on both channels: a push reminder for the moment and
    an email the day before.
</Tip>
