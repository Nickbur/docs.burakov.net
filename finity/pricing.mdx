Finity is **free to use**. The free plan has every core feature with a few limits on how much you can
create; **Pro** removes the limits and adds a few extras.

## Plans

| Plan | Price | What you get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Free** | Free | Countdowns, life in weeks, the journal with **free AI weekly summaries**, your character, and push reminders — within the limits below |
| **Pro** | $19.99 / year | No limits, plus **email reminders**, the **year in review** and your **AI character read** |
| **Lifetime** | $49.99 once | Everything in Pro, for life |

The app shows the exact price before you pay; it can differ by country and store. Where it's
offered, the yearly plan starts with a **7-day free trial**.

## Free plan limits

| You can create up to | Free | Pro |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Countdowns | 3 | No limit |
| Chapters of your life | 1 | No limit |
| Milestones | 3 | No limit |
| Journal photos | 3 per week | No limit |

The limits only stop you **creating** more — nothing you already have is ever removed. The
end-of-life counter doesn't count toward your countdowns.

## What Pro adds

- **No limits** on countdowns, chapters, milestones and photos.
- **[Email reminders](/finity/reminders)**, sent from the server, so you're reminded even when the
    app is closed.
- **[Year in review](/finity/journal#your-year-in-review)** — your year's journal as a single story.
- **[Character read](/finity/character#your-character-read)** — an AI-written take on your profile.

## Buy, restore and manage

- **Buy** from **Settings → Subscription** (or when you reach a limit). On iPhone and Android you pay
    through the App Store or Google Play; on the web, through the web checkout.
- **Pro follows your account**, so it works on every device you sign in on.
- **Restore purchases** on the subscription screen brings back a purchase made with the same store
    account.
- **Manage or cancel** from the subscription screen — **Manage in the App Store** /
    **Manage in Google Play** on a phone. A plan bought on the web is managed from your account on
    burakov.net — **Account → Subscriptions**, where you can cancel it, resume it before it ends, or
    change plan.
    If a payment fails, Finity shows a notice so you can update your payment details.

## When Pro ends

You keep everything you created. You can't add more beyond the free limits, email reminders stop
(push reminders carry on), and the year in review and new character reads need Pro again. A
character read you already have stays visible.
