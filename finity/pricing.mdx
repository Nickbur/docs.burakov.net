description: "Finity is free to use; Pro and a one-time Lifetime unlock add the AI layer and email reminders."
---

Finity is **free to use** — countdowns, life-in-weeks, the journal, the character, and local
reminders are all included.

## Plans

| Plan | Price | What you get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Free** | Free | Countdowns, life-in-weeks, journal & character, local reminders |
| **Pro** | $19.99 / year | Everything in Free, plus AI weekly recaps, the year-in-review, character readings, and email reminders |
| **Lifetime** | $49.99 once | Every Pro feature, once — for life |

## What Pro unlocks

- **AI weekly recaps** and the **year-in-review** from your journal
- **Character readings** — an AI interpretation of your profile
- **Email reminders** sent from the server, so you're reminded even with the app closed

<Note>
  Nothing you create is locked behind a plan — Free keeps full access to your countdowns, weeks and
  journal. Pro adds the AI layer and server-sent reminders on top.
</Note>
