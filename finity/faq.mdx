<AccordionGroup>
    <Accordion title="What platforms does Finity run on?">
        iPhone, Android, the web (as an installable app), and desktop for Windows, macOS and Linux. It's
        one app built to work the same everywhere, with your account syncing your data across all of them.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Does it work offline?">
        Mostly. Your countdowns, life map and journal are stored on your device and can be viewed and
        edited with no connection; they sync when you're back online. Signing in, saving your Life
        answers, photos, AI features, your Character and purchases need a connection.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Where is my data stored?">
        On your device, and synced to your own account so it appears on your other devices. Logging out
        removes it from that device — sync first so you don't lose recent changes. See
        [Accounts & privacy](/finity/accounts-and-privacy).
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Do I need to pay?">
        No. Finity is free to use, including the AI weekly summaries. The free plan limits how many
        countdowns (3), chapters (1), milestones (3) and photos per week (3) you can create. Pro (or a
        one-time Lifetime purchase) removes the limits and adds email reminders, the year in review and
        your character read. See [Pricing](/finity/pricing).
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Why didn't I get a reminder on my computer?">
        On the web and desktop, push reminders only fire while Finity is open. Use the phone app, or turn
        on email reminders (Pro), for anything you can't miss. See [Get reminded](/finity/reminders).
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Does the AI read my journal?">
        To write your weekly summaries, yes: when a week you wrote in ends, or when you tap
        **Generate summary**, that week's text is sent to an AI provider to write the summary. The year in
        review sends that year's entries, only when you generate it. Your character read uses only your
        numbers, never your journal text.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Is the life-in-weeks grid a prediction?">
        No. It's sized by the average life expectancy in your country to give a realistic horizon, but it's
        a tool for perspective — a canvas you annotate, not a verdict.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Why did my character's level go down?">
        Your character always reflects what you have now. If you delete countdowns, milestones, chapters or
        journal entries, the experience they earned goes too. See [Level up your character](/finity/character).
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="How do I delete my account?">
        In the app — **Account → Account settings → Delete account** — or from your account on
        burakov.net — **Account → Data & deletion → Delete my account**. You can restore it within 30
        days. Cancel any App Store or Google Play subscription separately.
    </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
