## Getting around

On a phone or a narrow window, Finity has a **bottom bar**:

| Tab | Opens |
| --- | --- |
| **Menu** | The full menu (a dot on it means an app update is available) |
| **List** | Your countdowns |
| **Calendar** | The month view |
| **Life** | Your life in weeks |
| **Notes** | Your journal |

Tapping the tab you're already on scrolls it back to the top. On a wide screen, the menu stays open
on the side instead: **Countdowns, Calendar, Life, Journal, Profile, Settings, About** and **Log
out**.

A few things have two names depending on where you look: **List** and **Countdowns** are the same
screen, as are **Notes** and **Journal**, and **Profile** opens your **Character**.

Finity reopens on the screen you were last using.

## Screens

| Screen | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Countdowns** | All your countdowns, sorted by date, each with a draining bar and a live number. **+** adds one; the gear icon opens Settings. See [Count down to a date](/finity/countdowns). |
| **New / edit countdown** | Title, description, date and time, time zone, repeat rule and reminders. |
| **Countdown detail** | The countdown ring, its next occurrence, repeat rule, anchor date and time zone, with **Edit** and **Delete**. |
| **Calendar** | A month with dots on the days that have countdowns, milestones or photos. See [See what's on a day](/finity/calendar). |
| **Day** | One day's countdowns, milestones and photos, and where that day sits in your life. |
| **Life** | Your life as a grid of weeks, with chapters and milestones. See [Map your life in weeks](/finity/life-in-weeks). |
| **Journal** | Today's line and mood, your past weeks, and the year in review. See [Keep a weekly journal](/finity/journal). |
| **Week** | One week's notes, mood, photos and AI summary. |
| **Character** | Your level, attributes, achievements and stats. See [Level up your character](/finity/character). |
| **Settings** | The options below. |
| **About** | The app version and update notice, a link to these docs, the Privacy Policy and Terms. |
| **Subscription** | Pro plans, **Restore purchases**, or your current subscription. See [Pricing](/finity/pricing). |

## Settings

| Setting | Options | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Appearance** | Auto, Light, Dark | Auto (follows your device) |
| **Language** | System language, English, Русский | System language — also used for email reminders |
| **Life counter** | Top or Bottom of the countdown list | Top |
| **Notifications** | **Send test notification** — sends a push to check that notifications reach your device | — |
| **Subscription** | **Manage subscription** | — |
| **Lock with Face ID / fingerprint** *(iPhone and Android)* | On / Off | Off — see [Accounts & privacy](/finity/accounts-and-privacy#lock-the-app) |
| **Send crash reports** | On / Off | On — turning it back on takes effect after a restart |

## Update prompts

When a new version is out, **About** shows *A new version is available* with an **Update** button
(on iPhone and Android). If your version is too old to keep working with the server, Finity shows an
**Update required** screen until you update.
