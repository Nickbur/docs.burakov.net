Your **Character** is an RPG-style profile built from your real life in Finity. Instead of a streak
you're afraid to break, it grows from what you actually do — the countdowns you keep, the weeks you
write about, the milestones and chapters you mark. Open it from **Profile** in the menu.

<Note>
    Your character is worked out on the server from the data you've synced, so it needs a connection to
    open, and changes made offline count once they've synced. It always reflects what you have **now**:
    delete a countdown or a milestone and its points go with it.
</Note>

## Experience and levels

You earn **experience (XP)** for:

| Activity | XP each |
| --- | --- |
| A countdown | 10 |
| A week with journal entries | 15 |
| A milestone | 25 |
| A chapter of your life | 10 |
| Each week of your current journal streak | 5 |

Levels get further apart as you climb: level 2 at **100 XP**, level 3 at **300**, level 4 at
**600**, level 5 at **1,000**, and so on.

## Attributes

Five attributes, each from 0 to 100:

| Attribute | Grows with |
| --- | --- |
| **Foresight** | Your countdowns |
| **Reflection** | Weeks you've journaled, and your streak |
| **Momentum** | Your journal streak |
| **Chronicle** | Your milestones |
| **Depth** | Your chapters |

## Your stats and streak

The character sheet shows **Countdowns**, **Weeks journaled**, **Streak**, **Milestones**,
**Chapters** and **Weeks lived**.

- **Streak** counts consecutive weeks with journal entries, back from your latest one.
- **Weeks lived** needs your date of birth in [Life](/finity/life-in-weeks); with only your age it
    shows 0.

## Achievements

| Achievement | Earned when you have |
| --- | --- |
| First countdown | 1 countdown |
| Planner | 10 countdowns |
| First note | 1 week with journal entries |
| Reflector | 10 weeks with journal entries |
| Consistent | A 4-week journal streak |
| Devoted | A 12-week journal streak |
| Milestone | 1 milestone |
| Chronicler | 10 milestones |
| First chapter | 1 chapter |
| Storyteller | 5 chapters |
| A decade lived | 520 weeks lived |

**Share** posts your level and title through your device's share sheet.

## Your character read

On **Pro**, tap **Reveal my character** for an AI-written title and a short paragraph (up to about
110 words) about what your profile says — what you've been leaning into and where you're heading.
Only your **numbers** are sent to the AI to write it — never your journal text. A read you already
have stays visible if Pro ends.
