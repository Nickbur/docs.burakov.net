Every countdown in Finity is two simple things: an **anchor date** (the moment you care about) and
an optional **repeat rule**. That small model covers your whole life without fuss.

## Create a countdown

Tap **+** on the Countdowns screen. The form has:

| Field | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Title** | Required, up to 300 characters. |
| **Description** | Optional notes, up to 8,000 characters. Also shown in its reminders. |
| **Event date & time** | The moment you're counting down to. Starts as tomorrow. |
| **Time zone** | The zone this countdown lives in — your device's by default. Pick **This device**, browse **All time zones**, or search. |
| **Countdown starts** | Where the bar starts draining from. Usually **now**; set an earlier date to see how much of a longer stretch has already passed. |
| **Repeat** | One-time, or a repeat rule (below). |
| **Notifications** | How you're reminded — see [Get reminded](/finity/reminders). |

## Repeat rules

Leave it as **One-time**, or choose **Repeats** every *N* **day(s), week(s), month(s) or year(s)**,
ending:

- **Never**,
- **On a date** — that whole day is included, or
- **After N times** — 10 by default.

A birthday is a yearly repeat; a visa renewal is a one-off; "water the plants" repeats every few
days and never ends. One-time countdowns that have passed show **Done**; repeating ones roll forward
to their next occurrence on their own.

## Time zones and daylight saving

Each countdown keeps its **own time zone**, and everything about it runs on that zone's clock:

- A repeat stays at the **same local time** through daylight-saving changes — "every day at 09:00"
    is always 09:00.
- A repeat that ends **on a date** includes that whole day.
- Reminders fire at the right moment for that zone, and email reminders show the time in the
    countdown's zone, in the language you use Finity in.

When you travel, a countdown stays on its own zone's clock until you change it — handy for a call or
a launch in another city.

## Reading a countdown

- In the **list**, each countdown has a **bar** that drains as the date approaches and a big live
    number — days, then hours, minutes and seconds as it gets close — plus its repeat rule and next
    date.
- Tap one to open its **detail**: a countdown **ring**, the **Next occurrence**, **Repeat**,
    **Anchor date**, **Time zone** and when you added it.

From the detail screen, **Edit** changes anything, and **Delete** removes the countdown after a
confirmation — this can't be undone.

## The end-of-life counter

Once you've set up [Life in weeks](/finity/life-in-weeks), Finity adds one extra card to your list:
a countdown to the end of your average life expectancy. Tap it to open Life. Choose whether it sits
at the **Top** or **Bottom** of the list in **Settings → Life counter**. It doesn't count toward
your countdown limit.

<Note>
    On the free plan you can have up to **3 countdowns**; Pro removes the limit. Countdowns you already
    have are never removed. See [Pricing](/finity/pricing).
</Note>
