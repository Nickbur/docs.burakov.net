## I'm not getting reminders

- **Notifications are off for Finity.** If you saw *Turn on notifications in system settings to get
    reminders.*, allow notifications for Finity in your phone's or computer's settings.
- **You're on the web or desktop.** Push reminders there only fire while Finity is open.
- **The countdown repeats very often.** Finity queues the next 6 occurrences of each countdown (and
    60 reminders in total) and tops them up when you open the app — open it now and then.
- **Email reminders** need an active Pro plan and the **Email** channel switched on for that
    countdown. Check your spam folder too.

See [Get reminded](/finity/reminders).

## Sign-in messages

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| *Incorrect validation code…* | Check the latest code in your inbox, or ask for a new one (after 90 seconds). |
| *Too many attempts. Please wait N min.* | Wait the time shown, then try again. |
| *Unable to connect…* | Check your internet connection. |
| *Google sign-in failed. Please try again.* | Try again, or sign in with your email and a code. |
| *Apple sign-in failed. Please try again.* | Same — or use your email and a code. |

## A change doesn't show up on my other device

- Both devices need to be **online** for a while — sync runs in the background when the app opens
    and when the connection comes back.
- If the same thing was edited on two devices, the most recent edit wins.

## My recent changes disappeared after logging out

Logging out removes Finity's data from that device immediately, including anything that hadn't synced
yet. Before logging out, open Finity online for a moment so it can sync.

## The journal says "Set up your Life first"

The journal follows your life in weeks. Open **Life** and answer its three questions — see
[Map your life in weeks](/finity/life-in-weeks).

## The calendar doesn't show where a day sits in my life

The calendar's life context, and milestones and photos dated to a specific day, need your **date of
birth**. If you only gave your age, open **Life** and edit your answers.

## Life, photos and AI messages

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| *Couldn't save — please try again.* (Life) | Saving your Life answers needs a connection — try again when you're online. |
| *Couldn't detect your location — please pick your country.* | Choose your country from the list instead. |
| *Couldn't add the photo…* | Check your connection and try again. |
| *AI summaries are unavailable right now* | The AI service is temporarily unavailable — try again later. |
| *The character read is unavailable right now.* | Same — try again later. |
| *Failed to load data. Check your connection…* (Character) | Your character is worked out on the server, so it needs a connection. |

## Purchases

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| *No plans are available right now.* | Try again later, or check that you're signed in to the App Store / Google Play. |

Bought Pro but it isn't showing? Tap **Restore purchases** on the subscription screen, signed in to
the same Finity account and store account you used to buy it.

## "Update required"

Your version of Finity is too old to keep working. Update it from the App Store or Google Play (or
reload the web app).
