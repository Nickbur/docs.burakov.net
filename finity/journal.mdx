The journal asks for very little — **a line a day**, a mood if you like, and a photo when you have
one. A ritual you can finish in thirty seconds is one you'll actually keep. It follows your
[life in weeks](/finity/life-in-weeks), so set that up first — until you do, the journal shows
*Set up your Life first*.

## Write today's line

Open **Notes** (the journal). At the top is **today**: write a line (up to 4,000 characters) and
pick one of five mood emoji. Only today's entry can be edited — earlier days are kept as they were
written.

Below it, every week you've written in is listed, newest first. Tap one to open it.

## A week's page

Each week has:

- a **weekly note** (up to 8,000 characters) and a **weekly mood**,
- your daily lines and **photos**, and
- an **AI summary** of the week.

### The AI summary

When a week ends, AI writes a short summary of it from what you wrote — the shape of the week, not
just its contents — and it's waiting on the week's page. It's **free on every plan**. Want it sooner?
Tap **Generate summary** (or **Refresh**). You can **edit** the summary, and Finity never replaces
your edited version without asking. The summary needs some text to work with — a mood on its own
isn't enough.

If last week has nothing in it yet, Finity may ask *Recap last week?* when you open the journal — at
most once per session. **Not now** dismisses it.

### Photos

Tap **Add photos** to add one or more pictures to a week. If you gave your birth date, you can also
pick which day of that week each photo belongs to. Finity makes a smaller copy (up to 1,024 pixels on
the longest side) and uploads only that — **your original photo never leaves your device**. Photos
also appear on their week in [Life](/finity/life-in-weeks) and on their day in the
[calendar](/finity/calendar).

## Your year in review

On **Pro**, the journal can stitch the current year's weeks into a single story: on the **2026 in
review** card (with the current year), tap **Generate 2026 recap**, any time during the year. It needs at least **10 weeks with entries** that
year — until then, Finity shows what you have so far and encourages you to keep journaling.

<Note>
    To write a week's summary, that week's text is sent to an AI provider — when the week ends, or when
    you tap **Generate summary**. The year in review sends that year's entries, only when you generate
    it. See [Accounts & privacy](/finity/accounts-and-privacy).
</Note>

<Note>
    On the free plan you can add up to **3 photos per week**; Pro removes the limit. See
    [Pricing](/finity/pricing).
</Note>
