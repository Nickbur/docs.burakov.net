title: "Quickstart"
description: "Sign in to Finity, create your first countdown, set up your life map, and write your first journal line."

---

Finity runs on **iPhone, Android, the web** (as an installable app) and **desktop** (Windows, macOS
and Linux). Get it from the [project page](https://burakov.net/projects/finity), or open
[finity.burakov.net](https://finity.burakov.net) in a browser.

<Steps>
    <Step title="Sign in">
        Enter your email and type the code you receive — that's it, no password. If you don't have an
        account yet, this creates one. You can also use **Sign in with Google**, or
        **Sign in with Apple** on iPhone. You land on your **Countdowns**.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Add your first countdown">
        Tap **+**, give it a **Title**, and set the **Event date & time** (it starts as tomorrow). If
        it recurs — a birthday, a monthly bill — switch **Repeat** on. Save it.

        Finity asks to send notifications after you first sign in (or, if it hasn't yet, the first time
        you save). Allow it to get reminders on this device. See [Count down to a date](/finity/countdowns) and [Get reminded](/finity/reminders).
    </Step>
    <Step title="Set up your life map">
        Open **Life** and answer three questions: your gender, your **date of birth** (or just your
        age), and your country. Finity draws your life as a grid of weeks.

        <Tip>
            Give your **date of birth** rather than your age if you can. The calendar's life context and
            day-dated milestones and photos need it.
        </Tip>
    </Step>
    <Step title="Write today's line">
        Open **Notes** (the journal), write a line about today and pick a mood if you like. When the
        week ends, Finity summarizes it for you automatically. See [Keep a weekly journal](/finity/journal).
    </Step>
    <Step title="Meet your character">
        Open **Profile** from the menu to see your **Character** — the level, attributes and
        achievements you've already earned. See [Level up your character](/finity/character).
    </Step>
</Steps>
