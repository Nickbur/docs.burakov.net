Swornlist is a **guest list and door for events where it matters who is in the room**. Guests prove their age or
identity once, on their own phone; after that they register for any Swornlist event with a tap and get a signed QR.
At the door, staff scan it and see the guest's verified name, age checks and photo — and decide.

Ticket money never goes through Swornlist: guests pay you the way you already take payments.

## The idea in one minute

<Steps>
    <Step title="The organizer sets the bar">
        Each ticket type asks for nothing, an **age check** (18+, 21+…) or an **identity check**. The event page says
        so up front.
    </Step>
    <Step title="The guest verifies once">
        A short document scan and selfie on the guest's phone. The result is reused at every Swornlist event until the
        document expires or two years pass — no second check, no extra cost.
    </Step>
    <Step title="The ticket is a signed QR">
        It works offline, in Apple or Google Wallet too, and cannot be forged or passed around.
    </Step>
    <Step title="The door matches face to photo">
        Staff scan, see the verified name, the age checks and the photo, and admit or deny — even with no signal.
    </Step>
</Steps>
