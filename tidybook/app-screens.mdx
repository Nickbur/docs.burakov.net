## Getting around

The bottom bar has four tabs:

| Tab | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Menu** | Sign in, your profile, **My card**, **Encryption**, **Settings**, **About** and **Log out**. A dot means an app update is available. |
| **Contacts** | Your address book — search, open, add contacts. |
| **Duplicates** | Groups of contacts that look like the same person — see [Find and merge duplicates](/tidybook/deduplication). |
| **Import** | Every way in and out — imports, connected accounts, push-back and exports. |

## Screens

| Screen | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Contacts** | The list, A–Z, with a search bar (*Search name, phone or email*). A banner points to duplicates waiting for review. The QR button opens **My card**; **+** adds a contact. |
| **Contact** | One contact's details. Tap a number to call or an address to email; **Share as vCard** sends the contact to someone. |
| **New contact / Edit contact** | The editor — see [Add, edit and find contacts](/tidybook/cleanup-and-editing). |
| **Review duplicates** | Each group of matching contacts with **Merge** and **Not duplicates**. |
| **Import & export** | Import from your phone, files or pasted text; connect Google and CardDAV; push changes back; export your book. |
| **My card** | Your own details as a QR code and NFC card — see [Export & share](/tidybook/export-and-share). |
| **Encryption** | How your synced book is protected, and the passphrase lock — see [Sync & encryption](/tidybook/sync-and-encryption). |
| **Sign in** | Your email and a one-time code, **Sign in with Apple** on iPhone, or Google. |
| **Subscription** | The **Sync** plan and **Restore purchases** — see [Pricing](/tidybook/pricing). |
| **About** | The version and update notice, a link to these docs, the Privacy Policy and Terms. |

## Settings

| Setting | Options | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Appearance** | System, Light, Dark | System |
| **Language** | System language, English, Русский | System language |
| **Subscription → Manage subscription** | — | — |
| **Lock with Face ID / fingerprint** *(iPhone and Android)* | On / Off | Off — see [Account & privacy](/tidybook/accounts-and-privacy#lock-the-app) |

## Update prompts

When a new version is out, **About** shows *A new version is available* with an **Update** button. If your version
is too old to keep working, a full-screen **Update required** message appears with **Update now**.
