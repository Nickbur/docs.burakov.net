title: "Find and merge duplicates"
description: "How Tidybook finds duplicate contacts, what a merge keeps, and how to undo one."

---

Tidybook looks for duplicates all the time and lists them in the **Duplicates** tab. It never merges anything on its
own — you review every group.

## How duplicates are found

Two contacts belong to the same group when they share:

- the **same phone number** — compared in international format, so `050 123 45 67` and `+380 50 123 4567` match; or
- the **same email address**, ignoring capital letters.

Matches chain together: if A shares a number with B, and B shares an email with C, all three form one group.

**Names alone never count.** Two different people called "Alex Smith" stay separate — that's the mistake that loses
contacts, and Tidybook won't make it.

## Review a group

The **Contacts** tab shows a banner when duplicates are waiting. In **Duplicates**, each group shows how many contacts
it holds, what they share, and each one's name, phone and email. The largest groups come first.

- **Merge** — combine the group into one contact.
- **Not duplicates** — dismiss the group; the contacts stay as they are.

## What a merge keeps

| Part of the contact | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Phones, emails, addresses, websites | **All** of them, from every contact, without repeats |
| Name | The fullest version |
| Nickname, company, birthday, photo | The first one found |
| Notes | Combined |
| Sources | All of them, so push-back still knows where the contact came from |

## Undo

Merged by mistake? Tap **Undo** on the confirmation that appears right after merging, and the original contacts come
back as they were.

<Note>
    Duplicate matching runs on your device. With Sync on, your other devices receive only the merged result.
</Note>
