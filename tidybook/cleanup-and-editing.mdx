## Find a contact

**Contacts** lists everyone A–Z. The search bar (*Search name, phone or email*) matches any part of a name, a phone
number or an email address, and shows how many contacts match.

A contact without a name is listed by its company, then email, then phone number.

## View a contact

Tap a contact to see its phones, emails, addresses, websites, birthday and notes, and which sources it came from.

- Tap a **phone number** to call it, or an **email address** to write to it.
- **Share as vCard** sends the contact to someone through your phone's share sheet, or copies it on the web.
- **Edit** opens the editor.

## Add or edit

Tap **+** in **Contacts** for a new contact, or **Edit** on an existing one.

| Section | Fields |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | Name and nickname |
| **Organization** | Company, title and department |
| **Phones, Emails, Websites** | As many as you need, each with a label — *mobile*, *home*, *work* or your own |
| **Addresses** | Street, city, region, postal code and country, with a label |
| **Birthday** | A date |
| **Note** | Free text |

Tap **Save**. To remove a contact, tap **Delete contact** in the editor — with Sync on, it's removed from your other
devices too.

## Tidying on save

Every time a contact is saved — by you, or by an import — Tidybook tidies it:

- **Phone numbers** are stored in international format (E.164), using your region for numbers written without a
    country code. The same number then always looks — and matches — the same way.
- **Emails** are trimmed and lower-cased.
- **Empty rows** are dropped, and repeated values within the contact are merged.

Fields that came from a source but don't appear in the editor are left untouched, so editing never loses data.
