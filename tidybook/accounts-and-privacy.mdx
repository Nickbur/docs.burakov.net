## Using Tidybook without an account

Importing, merging duplicates, editing, exporting and your QR card all work **without an account**, on one device.
Your book stays on that device. An account is needed for [Sync](/tidybook/sync-and-encryption) and the features that
come with it.

## Your account

Sign in with your **email and a one-time code** — no password. On iPhone you can also use **Sign in with Apple**, and
you can sign in with Google. It's one account across the apps at [burakov.net](https://burakov.net), but each app's
data is kept separate.

When you sign in for the first time, the book you built without an account is added to your account.

## What's stored where

| On your device | On the server (with Sync) |
| --- | --- |
| Your full address book | Each contact, **encrypted on your device** (AES-256-GCM) |
| My card | Your encryption key — held for you in default mode, or sealed by your passphrase with the lock on |
| Settings | Your account (email, name) and this app's language |

Contacts are encrypted before they leave your device. In default mode your account can recover the key, so this
isn't zero-knowledge; with the **passphrase lock**, only you can decrypt them. See
[Sync & encryption](/tidybook/sync-and-encryption#encryption).

Your data isn't sold or used for ads.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **Contacts — read** | To import your phone's address book. |
| **Contacts — change** | Only when you [push changes back](/tidybook/push-back) to your phone. |
| **Face ID / fingerprint** | Only if you turn on the app lock. |

## Lock the app

On iPhone and Android, turn on **Settings → Privacy & security → Lock with Face ID / fingerprint**. Tidybook then
asks for your face or fingerprint when it opens and after 5 minutes in the background (on iPhone your device passcode
works too). The check is done by your phone itself — nothing about your face or fingerprint reaches the app or our
servers.

## Export or delete your data

- **Your contacts:** export them as vCard or CSV at any time — see [Export & share](/tidybook/export-and-share).
- **Account data:** in your account on burakov.net, **Account → Data & deletion → Download my data** gives you one
    file with your data from every app (Tidybook contacts in it are still encrypted — use the vCard export for a
    readable copy).
- **Delete my account** (same place) removes your data from **every** app on the account. You can restore it within
    **30 days** by signing in again; after that it's gone for good. Your local book and any exports stay on your
    devices until you remove them.
