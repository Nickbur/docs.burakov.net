## "Could not read device contacts"

Tidybook isn't allowed to read your contacts. Turn on **Contacts** access for Tidybook in your phone's settings
(iPhone: **Settings → Tidybook → Contacts**), then import again. On the web, importing from the phone isn't available —
import a vCard file instead.

## A file imported nothing, or only names

- Prefer **vCard** (`.vcf`) — it carries every field.
- For CSV, make sure the file is **comma-separated** and saved as **UTF-8**. Spreadsheets in some regions save with
    semicolons; export again as "CSV UTF-8".
- Contacts with no name, phone or email are skipped.

## A CardDAV account won't connect

- Check the **Server URL** — use your service's CardDAV address.
- Services with two-step sign-in (iCloud, Fastmail, Google Workspace) need an **app-specific password**.
- In a browser, some servers block connections from other websites; connect from the iPhone or Android app instead.

## Contacts appear twice after importing again

Importing the same file again adds its contacts again. Open **Duplicates** and merge the copies.

## Two contacts I know are the same aren't listed as duplicates

Tidybook matches only identical phone numbers or email addresses. If they share neither, merge them by hand: copy the
details into one contact and delete the other.

## Changes don't reach my other devices

1. Are you **signed in** with the same account on both, with an active **Sync** plan?
2. Is the device **online**? Sync runs automatically once it is.
3. Is the **passphrase lock** on? Each device syncs only after you unlock it on the **Encryption** screen.
4. A very large first sync can take a while — keep the app open on **Contacts** until the spinner stops.

## "Wrong passphrase — try again"

Passphrases are case-sensitive. If you've forgotten it, your synced book can't be decrypted. The copy on any device
that's still unlocked, and your vCard exports, are unaffected — export from there and start over.

## My contacts are gone after logging out

Logging out removes the book from the device. Sign in again to get back everything that had synced.

## Sign-in messages

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| *Too many attempts. Please wait N min.* | Wait the time shown, then request a new code. |
| *Unable to connect…* | Check your connection. |

## "Update required"

Your version is too old to keep working. Tap **Update now**, or update Tidybook from your app store.
