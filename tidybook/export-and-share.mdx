## Export your book

In **Import → Backup / export**:

| Format | What it contains | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **vCard** (`.vcf`) | Everything — every field, including the ones Tidybook kept from your sources | Backups, moving to another app |
| **CSV** (`.csv`) | Name, given and family name, organization, first phone, first email, birthday and notes | Spreadsheets |

On the web the file downloads as `tidybook-contacts.vcf` or `tidybook-contacts.csv`. On iPhone and Android it opens
your share sheet — save it to Files, send it to yourself, or open it in another app.

<Tip>
    A vCard export is your independent backup: a plain file that opens anywhere and doesn't depend on Tidybook, the
    cloud or any account. Make one before big changes, and before turning on the
    [passphrase lock](/tidybook/sync-and-encryption#passphrase-lock).
</Tip>

## Share one contact

Open the contact and tap **Share as vCard** — it goes through your share sheet, or is copied on the web.

## My card

**My card** is your own business card. Open it from the QR button in **Contacts** or from the menu, and fill in
what you want to share: **name**, **title**, **organization**, **phones** and **emails**.

- **QR code** — appears as soon as the card has a name, phone or email. Anyone can scan it with their phone's camera
    to save you as a contact — no app needed on their side.
- **NFC** — on phones with NFC, write your card to an NFC tag so people can tap their phone to it.
- **Share card** — send it as a vCard through the share sheet.

Your card is separate from your address book and contains only what you put on it — never anyone else's contacts.
