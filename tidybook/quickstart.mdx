title: "Quickstart"
description: "Import your contacts into Tidybook, merge the duplicates, and turn on encrypted sync."
mode: "wide"
---

Tidybook runs on **iPhone, Android and the web**. Get the app from the
[project page](https://burakov.net/projects/tidybook), or open [tidybook.burakov.net](https://tidybook.burakov.net)
in a browser. You don't need an account to start.

<Steps>
    <Step title="Import your contacts">
        Open the **Import** tab. On your phone, tap **Import phone contacts** and allow access to your contacts. On
        any device you can also import a **vCard** or **CSV** file, or connect a Google or CardDAV account — see
        [Import contacts](/tidybook/importing).
    </Step>
    <Step title="Review the duplicates">
        Open the **Duplicates** tab. Each group shows contacts that share a phone number or email address. Tap
        **Merge** for the ones that are the same person, or **Not duplicates** for the ones that aren't. See
        [Find and merge duplicates](/tidybook/deduplication).
    </Step>
    <Step title="Tidy and edit">
        Open any contact in **Contacts** to fix names, add numbers or remove what you don't need. See
        [Add, edit and find contacts](/tidybook/cleanup-and-editing).
    </Step>
    <Step title="Keep a backup">
        In **Import → Backup / export**, export your book as a **vCard** file. It's a plain copy that doesn't depend on
        Tidybook or any account.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Turn on Sync (optional)">
        Sign in from the menu and subscribe to **Sync** to keep the book on all your devices, encrypted end to end.
        Whatever you built before signing in is kept. See [Sync & encryption](/tidybook/sync-and-encryption).
    </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
    Ready to put the clean book back on your phone or in Google? See [Push changes back](/tidybook/push-back) — you
    see a preview before anything changes.
</Tip>
