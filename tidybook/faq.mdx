<AccordionGroup>
    <Accordion title="Which platforms does it run on?">
        iPhone, Android and the web ([tidybook.burakov.net](https://tidybook.burakov.net)). With Sync, your book is the
        same on all of them.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Do I need an account?">
        No — everything on one device works without one. You need an account for Sync, connected accounts and
        push-back. See [Account & privacy](/tidybook/accounts-and-privacy).
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Does Tidybook change my phone's contacts?">
        Only when you [push changes back](/tidybook/push-back), after a preview you confirm. Importing only reads them.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Can I bring in contacts from Google, iCloud or Outlook?">
        Google: connect your account directly. iCloud and other CardDAV services: connect them with an app-specific
        password — see [Connect Google & CardDAV](/tidybook/connected-accounts). Outlook and anything else: export a
        vCard (`.vcf`) and import the file.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Will it merge two different people with the same name?">
        No. Contacts are matched only by the same phone number or email address, and you confirm every merge. See
        [Find and merge duplicates](/tidybook/deduplication).
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Why did my phone numbers change format?">
        Tidybook stores every number in international format (for example `+380501234567`), using your region for
        local numbers. That's what lets the same number match across sources.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Can Tidybook read my contacts?">
        Synced contacts are encrypted on your device. In default mode your account holds the key so you can recover the
        book on a new device; turn on the **passphrase lock** and only you can decrypt it. See
        [Sync & encryption](/tidybook/sync-and-encryption#encryption).
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="What if I forget my passphrase?">
        Your synced book can't be decrypted — that's what zero-knowledge means. The copies on your devices and any vCard
        exports are unaffected, so keep an export somewhere safe.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="How do I back up my contacts?">
        Export them as **vCard** — a plain file that belongs to you and opens anywhere. See
        [Export & share](/tidybook/export-and-share).
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="What does it cost?">
        **$9.99** once for every on-device feature, and **$7.99 / year** for Sync. See [Pricing](/tidybook/pricing).
    </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
