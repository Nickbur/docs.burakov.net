## Plans

| Plan | Price | What you get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Tidybook** | **$9.99** once | Everything on one device: import from your phone, files and pasted text; duplicate review with undo; editing and tidying; vCard and CSV export; your QR and NFC card; the app lock |
| **Sync** | **$7.99** / year | Everything above, plus: end-to-end-encrypted sync across all your devices and the web, encrypted cloud backup, [Google and CardDAV connections](/tidybook/connected-accounts), [push-back with preview](/tidybook/push-back), and the zero-knowledge [passphrase lock](/tidybook/sync-and-encryption#passphrase-lock) |

The one-time purchase is yours to keep. **Sync** is the only recurring part, because it's the only part with a running
cost — the encrypted relay between your devices.

## Buying

Buy from the **Subscription** screen (**Menu → Settings → Subscription**) — through the App Store or Google Play on
your phone, or by card on the web. **Restore purchases** brings a purchase back on a new phone. Manage or cancel the
Sync plan in the same place, or in your App Store / Google Play subscriptions.

## If you stop Sync

Your book stays on each device and keeps working, with every one-time feature. It just stops syncing between devices,
and connected accounts and push-back pause until you subscribe again.
