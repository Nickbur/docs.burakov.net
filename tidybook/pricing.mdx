Tidybook is **buy-once** — no subscription trap. You pay a single price and use every on-device
feature forever. Cloud sync across your devices is an **optional** low-cost yearly add-on, but you
never need a subscription just to *use* the app.

## Plans

| Plan | Price | What you get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Tidybook** | $9.99 once | Every on-device feature on one device — import, deduplication with review & undo, cleanup, the full contact editor, vCard / CSV export, and your QR card |
| **Sync** | $7.99 / year | Everything above, plus always-on cloud: multi-device sync, automatic cloud backup & restore, and the zero-knowledge passphrase lock |

## Why a plan for sync

The one-time purchase covers the app itself. The **Sync** plan covers the one part with an ongoing
cost — running the always-on, end-to-end-encrypted relay that keeps your book identical across your
devices. That's the honest split: buy the app once, and pay for the cloud only if you want it.

<Note>
  The one-time purchase never expires. If you ever stop the Sync plan, the app and everything on your
  device keep working — you simply stop syncing to new devices.
</Note>
