A **connected account** is a contacts source Tidybook stays linked to: it brings changes made there into your book,
and can [push your tidy book back](/tidybook/push-back) to it. Connected accounts are part of the
[Sync plan](/tidybook/pricing).

Tidybook's own book stays the master copy. A connected account is never overwritten unless you push to it yourself.

## Google

<Steps>
    <Step title="Connect">
        In **Import**, choose to connect **Google** and sign in to the Google account whose contacts you want. Google
        asks you to let Tidybook read and update your contacts.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Import">
        Tidybook brings in all of that account's contacts. Matches with contacts you already have show up in
        **Duplicates**.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Stay up to date">
        Contacts you add or change in Google later are brought into Tidybook.
    </Step>
</Steps>

Disconnect the account to stop Tidybook from reading or updating it — the contacts already in your book stay. You
can also remove Tidybook's access from your Google account's security settings.

## CardDAV

CardDAV is the open standard most contact servers speak — Nextcloud, Fastmail, iCloud, Synology and others.

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Server URL** | Your server's CardDAV address, for example `https://cloud.example.com/remote.php/dav` for Nextcloud. |
| **Username** | Your account name on that server. |
| **Password** | Your password — or an **app password**, which iCloud, Fastmail and others require. |

Tidybook finds your address book on the server, imports it, and keeps it connected like a Google account.

<Tip>
    Services with two-step sign-in (iCloud, Fastmail, Google Workspace) need an **app-specific password** for CardDAV
    — create one in that service's security settings.
</Tip>

## Your data

- A connection is used only to read and update that account's contacts.
- Contacts brought in from a connected account join your book like any other, so with Sync on they're stored
    **end-to-end encrypted**.
- See [Account & privacy](/tidybook/accounts-and-privacy) for everything Tidybook stores.
