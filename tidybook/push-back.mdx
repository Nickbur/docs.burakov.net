description: "Send your tidy Tidybook address book back to your phone, Google or a CardDAV server — with a preview of every change before anything is written."

---

Tidybook keeps its own book as the master copy and **never writes to your sources on its own**. When you're happy
with your tidy book, you push it back — to your phone's address book, a Google account or a CardDAV server — by
choice, one source at a time. Push-back is part of the [Sync plan](/tidybook/pricing).

## Push to a source

<Steps>
    <Step title="Pick the destination">
        In **Import**, choose to push changes back and pick a destination: your phone's contacts, or a
        [connected account](/tidybook/connected-accounts).
    </Step>
    <Step title="Check the preview">
        Before anything is written, Tidybook shows a **preview** of what will happen in that source: contacts that will
        be **added**, **updated** and **removed** — for example, the duplicates you merged.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Confirm">
        Confirm to apply the changes. Cancel, and nothing in the source changes.
    </Step>
</Steps>

## What's written

- Contacts are written back **in full**, including fields Tidybook kept but doesn't show — so a contact makes the
    round trip without losing anything.
- Tidybook knows which contacts came from which source, so merged duplicates replace their originals instead of
    adding more copies.
- Only the source you picked changes. Your other sources stay as they are until you push to them too.

<Tip>
    Before your first push to a source you rely on, export it — or your Tidybook book — as a **vCard** file. See
    [Export & share](/tidybook/export-and-share).
</Tip>

## Your phone's contacts

On iPhone and Android, pushing to the phone asks for permission to **change** your contacts — reading them for
import doesn't need it. You can turn this permission off again in your phone's settings afterwards.
