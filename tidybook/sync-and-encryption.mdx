With the [Sync plan](/tidybook/pricing), your book stays the same on your phone, tablet and the web. Each device keeps
its own full copy, so Tidybook works offline and syncs whenever it's online.

## Turn it on

Sign in from the menu with the same account on each device, and subscribe to **Sync**. If you built a book before
signing in, it's kept and added to your account — anything that matches contacts already there shows up in
**Duplicates**.

## When it syncs

Automatically — shortly after every change, when you open the app, and when your connection comes back. A spinner in
the **Contacts** header shows that a sync is running. There's nothing to press.

## How edits combine

Tidybook merges changes **field by field**, so edits from two devices don't overwrite each other:

| What changed | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Different fields of the same contact | Both changes are kept. |
| The same single field (name, company, birthday, note, photo) | The most recent edit wins. |
| Lists — phones, emails, addresses, websites | Additions from every device are kept. If one device adds a number while another removes it, the number **stays**. |
| A contact deleted on one device | It's deleted everywhere, even if another device edited it meanwhile. |

You never see a "which version wins?" dialog.

## Encryption

Every contact is encrypted on your device with **AES-256-GCM** before it's uploaded. Tidybook's server stores and
relays these sealed records to your other devices.

You choose between two levels of protection on the **Encryption** screen:

| | Default | Passphrase lock |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Who holds the key** | Your account keeps it, so a new device gets it when you sign in | Only you — it's derived from your passphrase |
| **New device** | Just sign in | Sign in, then enter your passphrase |
| **Forgot your passphrase?** | — | Your synced book **can't be decrypted** by anyone |
| **Zero-knowledge** | No — the key is recoverable through your account | Yes |

### Passphrase lock

<Steps>
    <Step title="Make a backup first">
        Export your book as a [vCard file](/tidybook/export-and-share). It's your safety net if you ever lose the
        passphrase.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Set the passphrase">
        On **Encryption**, tap **Set passphrase lock** and enter a passphrase of at least 8 characters, twice.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Unlock on each device">
        Each device asks for the passphrase before it syncs. Until you unlock, the device keeps working with its own
        copy and syncs once unlocked.
    </Step>
</Steps>

To remove the lock, unlock first, then tap **Remove passphrase lock**.

<Warning>
    With the passphrase lock on, nobody — including Tidybook — can recover your synced book without the passphrase.
    Keep a vCard export somewhere safe.
</Warning>

## Logging out

Logging out removes your book from that device. Everything that had synced comes back when you sign in again — so let
a sync finish before logging out.
