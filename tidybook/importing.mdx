Everything you import lands in **one address book** inside Tidybook. You can import without an account, and as many
times and from as many sources as you like. All imports are in the **Import** tab.

| Source | Where | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Your phone's contacts** | **Import phone contacts** *(iPhone and Android)* | Asks for access to your contacts the first time. |
| **A file** | **Import vCard / CSV** | `.vcf`, `.vcard` or `.csv`. |
| **Pasted text** | **Paste vCard / CSV text** | Paste a vCard or CSV straight from another app. |
| **Google** | Connected accounts | Stays connected — see [Connect Google & CardDAV](/tidybook/connected-accounts). |
| **CardDAV** | Connected accounts | Nextcloud, Fastmail, iCloud and other CardDAV servers — same page. |

## From your phone

Tidybook reads your phone's address book — names, company and job title, phones, emails, addresses, websites,
birthdays, notes and photos — and adds them to your book. Importing **doesn't change** your phone's contacts. To put
the cleaned-up book back on the phone, use [Push changes back](/tidybook/push-back).

## From files

- **vCard** (`.vcf`) is the best choice: every phone, email app and address book can export it, and it carries every
    field. vCard 3.0 and 4.0 are supported.
- **CSV** works for spreadsheets and simple exports — Google Contacts CSV columns are recognised. CSV carries less
    detail, so prefer vCard when you can.

To move contacts from a service that isn't connected, export a vCard from it and import the file.

## Nothing gets lost

Tidybook stores contacts in a detailed format close to vCard 4.0, and remembers **where each field came from**.
Anything a source provides that Tidybook doesn't show — an unusual field, a custom label — is **kept alongside** the
contact and written back out on export and push-back. Contacts make the round trip intact.

## After importing

- New contacts appear in **Contacts**, and any that share a phone number or email with an existing contact show up
    in **Duplicates** for review.
- Phone numbers are stored in one international format, using your region for local numbers.
- Importing a file again adds its contacts again — the copies show up in **Duplicates**, ready to merge.
- With **Sync** on, the new contacts are encrypted and synced to your other devices.
