Quietletter runs on **iPhone, Android and the web**. Get the app from the
[project page](https://burakov.net/projects/quietletter), or open
[quietletter.burakov.net](https://quietletter.burakov.net) in a browser. Running your own server
instead? Start with [Run your own instance](/quietletter/self-hosting).

<Steps>
    <Step title="Sign in">
        Enter your email and type the 6-digit code you receive (it's valid for 10 minutes). If you don't
        have an account yet, this creates one.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Create a switch">
        Tap **+** (or **Create a switch** on the empty list). Give it a **Title** — your recipients will
        see it — and keep the **Balanced** safety preset for now: check in every 30 days, with 14 days of
        grace.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Add a recipient and what they'll receive">
        Add at least one **recipient** (an email address; name and phone are optional) and at least one
        **action** — for example a **Message**. A message is delivered as a normal readable email unless
        you switch it to **Encrypted** and give it a passphrase. See
        [Create and edit a switch](/quietletter/create-a-switch).
    </Step>
    <Step title="Save">
        Tap **Save**. The timer starts now. Quietletter asks to send notifications — allow it so it can
        remind you before each deadline.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Check in for the first time">
        Tap **Check in**. The first time, you set a **check-in PIN** of 4 to 8 digits; after that, every
        check-in asks for it. See [Check in](/quietletter/check-in).
    </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
    If you added anything **encrypted**, give its passphrase to your recipient **outside the app** —
    in person, in a sealed letter, alongside your will. Without it, nobody can open that item, not even
    us.
</Warning>
