## The cycle

A switch moves through a simple cycle:

1. **Active** — counting down to your next check-in. Each [check-in](/quietletter/check-in) resets the
    deadline to the full interval.
2. **Grace period** — the deadline has passed. Quietletter warns you; a check-in now returns the
    switch to Active.
3. **Fired** — the grace period ended without a check-in, and your recipients received the switch.
    This is final.

**Fire time = deadline + grace period**, and the grace period is never shorter than **48 hours**.
Firing is decided on the server, checked every minute — so it doesn't matter whether your phone is
on, offline or lost. If you check in at the same moment the server is about to act, your check-in
wins.

A switch fires on **inactivity only**. Nobody — no recipient, no support agent — can trigger it
early.

## Safety presets

| Preset | Check in every | Grace period | Warnings (hours after the deadline) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Paranoid** | 1 week | 3 days | 0, 24, 48 |
| **Balanced** *(default)* | 30 days | 14 days | 0, 72, 168 |
| **Legacy** | 90 days | 30 days | 0, 168, 336 |

Preset warnings go out by **push and email**.

## Custom schedule

Choose **Custom** to set everything yourself:

- **Check in every** 1 to 3,650 **days, weeks or months**.
- **Grace period** in **hours or days** — at least **48 hours**.
- **Warnings** — 1 to 20 steps. Each has an **Offset (hours into grace)** — how many hours after the
    deadline it goes out — and its channels: **Push**, **Email**, **SMS**. Across all steps, both
    **push and email** must be used at least once.

<Note>
    Every warning must go out **before the fire time**, so each offset has to be shorter than the grace
    period. A later step shows *Each warning must come before the switch fires* and the switch can't be
    saved until you fix it.
</Note>

**SMS warnings** are available on **Pro** with a custom schedule: ticking **SMS** on a step asks for
a **Warning phone (for SMS)**.

## Time zones

The check-in interval follows the **calendar in your device's time zone**: "every month" ends on the
same date and local time, even across a daylight-saving change (and on the last day of a shorter
month when needed). The grace period and warning offsets are exact numbers of hours.

## How you're warned

**Before the deadline**, the app reminds you on your device — **48 hours, 24 hours and right at the
deadline** — if you allowed notifications. Tapping the reminder opens the switch.

**During the grace period**, the server sends the warning steps of your schedule:

| Channel | What it says |
| --- | --- |
| **Email** (to your account email) | Subject: *Check in to keep "…" from firing*. It states when the switch will fire, in your time zone, and has an **I'm here — check in** link that opens the switch in the web app, where you sign in and confirm with your PIN. |
| **Push** | *Quietletter check-in* — *"…" will fire soon — check in to stay safe.* Tapping it opens the switch. |
| **SMS** *(Pro)* | *Quietletter: check in to keep "…" from firing.* |

Warning emails are written in the language you use the app in (English or Russian).

<Tip>
    The fastest way to stop a warning is to open the app and tap **Check in** on that switch.
</Tip>
