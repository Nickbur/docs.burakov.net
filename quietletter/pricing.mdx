## Plans

| Plan | Price | What you get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Free** | Free | **1 switch** with **Message** actions (readable or encrypted), every safety preset, and push and email warnings |
| **Pro** | $19.99 / year | More switches, **encrypted files**, **secrets** and **private pages**, **SMS warnings**, and a text message to recipients with a phone number when a switch fires |

The app shows the exact price before you pay; it can differ by country and store. Pro follows your
account, so it works on every device you sign in on.

<Note>
    On the free plan, every switch that hasn't fired counts toward the limit — **disarmed switches
    included**. A switch that has fired doesn't count, so you never have to delete it (and its
    recipients' release links) to start a new one.
</Note>

## Buy, restore and manage

Open **Settings → Subscription**. On iPhone and Android you pay through the App Store or Google Play;
on the web, by card through the web checkout. **Restore purchases** brings back a purchase made with
the same store account; **Manage subscription** takes you to the App Store or Google Play to change or
cancel it. For a web purchase, manage or cancel it from your account on burakov.net —
**Account → Subscriptions**.

## If Pro ends

Your switches are **not** turned off:

- Every switch keeps running and, if it fires, delivers **all** its content — files, secrets and
    private pages included.
- **SMS** stops: SMS warnings and the recipients' text message need an active Pro plan when they're
    sent.
- You can still **check in**, **disarm**, **re-arm**, **delete** and **export** at any time.
- Saving **changes** to a switch that uses Pro features needs Pro again.

## Self-hosted instances

On [your own instance](/quietletter/self-hosting), every feature is unlocked with no subscription.
