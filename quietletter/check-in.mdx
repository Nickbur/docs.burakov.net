A check-in is your proof of life: it resets the switch's deadline to the full interval and, if the
switch was in its grace period, makes it **Active** again.

## Check in

Tap **Check in** — on the switch's card in the list, or on its detail screen — and confirm with your
**PIN**. You'll see *Checked in — your switch is safe.* The button is there for switches that are
**Active** or in their **Grace period**. A **Disarmed** or **Paused** switch shows **Re-arm** instead:
confirm it the same way, and the switch is **Active** again with a fresh cycle that starts now (*Switch
re-armed — the countdown starts now.*).

Each switch is checked in separately.

## Your check-in PIN

- **The first time** you check in on a device, you set a **check-in PIN** of **4 to 8 digits**,
    entered twice.
- **After that**, every check-in asks for it, so a check-in is never an accidental tap.
- **Forgot it?** Tap **Forgot PIN? Reset it** and set a new one. The PIN is a confirmation step on
    this device, not a password for your account.
- The PIN is **per device**: it's stored only on the device (as a one-way hash) and removed when you
    log out. A new phone or browser asks you to set one.

## Face ID or fingerprint

On iPhone and Android you can confirm check-ins with your face or fingerprint instead: turn on
**Settings → Privacy & security → Confirm check-ins with Face ID / fingerprint** (turning it on asks
for a successful scan). If a scan fails or you cancel it, your PIN still works.

## Reminders before the deadline

If you allowed notifications, the app reminds you **48 hours, 24 hours and right at** each deadline.
Tapping the reminder opens the switch. These reminders come from your device itself; on the web they
work only as far as your browser allows.

## Checking in during the grace period

Missed the deadline? Check in as usual — at any point before the switch fires. The warning email's
**I'm here — check in** link takes you to the switch in the web app; sign in, then confirm with your
PIN.

<Note>
    During the grace period, the switch shows a **Grace period** badge. The warning email states the
    exact time it will fire if you don't check in.
</Note>
