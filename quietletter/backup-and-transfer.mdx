All your switches can be saved to **one file** and imported again — as a backup, or to move between
the hosted service and [your own instance](/quietletter/self-hosting), in either direction.

## Export

Open **Settings → Backup & transfer → Export switches** and confirm with your PIN (or Face ID /
fingerprint). On the web the file downloads as `quietletter-export-` followed by the date; on iPhone
and Android the share sheet opens so you can save or send it.

## Import

Open **Settings → Backup & transfer → Import switches**, pick the file and confirm. Quietletter then
shows what was imported and what was skipped, and why.

## What the file holds

- **Encrypted stays encrypted.** Encrypted messages, files, secrets and private pages travel as
    ciphertext with their passphrase-protected key. After a move your recipients open them with the
    same passphrase, and no server ever sees it.
- **The rest is plain text.** Titles, recipient emails and phone numbers, schedules and any readable
    message are readable in the file. **Keep it somewhere safe.**
- **Live switches only.** Switches that have already fired aren't exported.

## How an import behaves

- **Nothing fires early.** Every imported switch starts its check-in timer at the moment of import,
    and a disarmed switch stays disarmed.
- **Safe to repeat.** A switch that's already there is skipped — never duplicated or overwritten — so
    importing the same file twice changes nothing.
- **The same rules apply.** Each switch is checked like one you saved by hand (for example the
    48-hour minimum grace period, or warnings that must fall before the fire time); invalid ones are
    skipped without stopping the rest.
- **Plan limits apply on the hosted service.** On the free plan you can have one live switch in total —
    including the ones you already have, but not switches that have fired — and files, secrets, private
    pages and SMS warnings need Pro.
    A switch with a public page (a self-hosted-only feature) is skipped there.

A file from a newer version of Quietletter than the one you're importing into is refused; update first.

## Move to your own instance (or back)

<Steps>
    <Step title="Export">
        While the app is connected to where your switches live now, export them as above.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Switch sides">
        Connect your instance in **Settings → Self-hosted instance** — or, to come back, choose **Use the
        hosted service** there and sign in. See [Run your own instance](/quietletter/self-hosting).
    </Step>
    <Step title="Import">
        Import the file on the new side.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Check in">
        Check in on each switch and review its schedule. Then disarm or delete the old copies on the side
        you left, so they don't fire later.
    </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
    Moving doesn't remove the switches from where you exported them — they keep counting down there. Turn
    them off on the old side once the move is done.
</Warning>
