## Getting around

The bottom bar has **Menu** and **Switches**. The menu holds **Switches**, **About**, **Settings** and
**Log out**. When the app is connected to your own instance, the top of the menu shows that
instance's address.

## Screens

| Screen | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Sign in** | Your email and a one-time code — or **Connect your instance** to use your own server. |
| **Switches** | Every switch as a card with its state and *Check in within …* countdown, and a **Check in** button. **+** creates a switch. |
| **Switch detail** | The state and countdown, the **Schedule**, **Recipients** and **Actions**, with **Check in**, **Disarm** and **Delete** — or **Re-arm** on a disarmed switch. The pencil icon opens **Edit switch** until the switch fires. |
| **New switch / Edit switch** | Title, safety preset, recipients and actions — see [Create and edit a switch](/quietletter/create-a-switch). |
| **PIN** | Set or confirm your check-in PIN. Also confirms re-arming a switch, and exporting and importing switches. |
| **Settings** | The options below. |
| **Subscription** | Pro plans and **Restore purchases**, or your current subscription. |
| **About** | The app version and update notice, a link to these docs, the Privacy Policy and Terms. |
| **Release page** | What a recipient opens in the browser when a switch fires — see [What your recipients receive](/quietletter/recipients). |

## Switch states

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Active** | Counting down to the next check-in. |
| **Grace period** | The deadline has passed; you're being warned. Check in to return to Active. |
| **Fired** | Delivered to the recipients. Final — shown as *Fired on …*; it can't be edited, checked in or disarmed. |
| **Disarmed** | Turned off; it won't count down or fire until you **Re-arm** it. |

## Settings

| Setting | Options | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Appearance** | Auto, Light, Dark | — |
| **Language** | System language, English, Русский | Also the language of your warning emails and your recipients' emails. |
| **Notifications → Send test notification** | — | Checks that push notifications reach this device (hosted service). |
| **Subscription → Manage subscription** | — | Hosted service only. |
| **Lock with Face ID / fingerprint** | On / Off (off by default) | iPhone and Android. Asks when the app opens and after 5 minutes in the background. |
| **Confirm check-ins with Face ID / fingerprint** | On / Off (off by default) | iPhone and Android. Your PIN still works if the scan fails. |
| **Backup & transfer** | **Export switches**, **Import switches** | See [Back up and move your switches](/quietletter/backup-and-transfer). |
| **Self-hosted instance** | Connect, or **Use the hosted service** | See [Run your own instance](/quietletter/self-hosting#connect-the-app). |

## Update prompts

When a new version is out, **About** says *A new version is available*. If your version is too old to
keep working with the server, an **Update required** screen asks you to update first.
