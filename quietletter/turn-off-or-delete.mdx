## Disarm a switch

Open the switch and tap **Disarm**, then confirm *Disarm this switch? It will stop counting down and
will not fire until you re-arm it.* A disarmed switch keeps its recipients and content, but it no
longer counts down, warns or fires.

## Re-arm a switch

Open the disarmed switch and tap **Re-arm**, then confirm with your PIN (or Face ID / fingerprint). The
switch is **Active** again with the same recipients and content, and a fresh check-in cycle starts now.

## Delete a switch

Open the switch and tap **Delete**, then confirm *Delete this switch permanently? This cannot be
undone.* This removes the switch, its recipients and all its content from the server.

If the switch has **already fired**, deleting it also removes your recipients' release links — they
won't be able to open what you left them anymore.

## A switch that has fired

Firing is final. A fired switch stays in your list as **Fired on …**; it can't be checked in, disarmed
or edited. Its release links work for 90 days after firing — see
[What your recipients receive](/quietletter/recipients).

## The free plan's one switch

On the free plan you can have **one live switch**. A disarmed switch still counts — re-arm it, or delete
it to start a new one. A switch that has **fired** no longer counts, so you can start a new switch without
deleting it (and your recipients keep their release links). Or upgrade to Pro. See
[Pricing](/quietletter/pricing).
