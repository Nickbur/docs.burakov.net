description: "What happens on your recipients' side when a Quietletter switch fires — the email, the release page, unlocking encrypted items, and how long the link works."

---

Your recipients don't need an account or the app. When a switch fires, each of them gets an **email**,
and — if the switch has encrypted items — a **private link** to open them in any browser.

## The email

- **Subject:** *Something was left for you:* followed by the switch's title.
- It greets the recipient by name if you gave one.
- **Readable messages** are written right in the email, in the order you set, each under its
    **Subject** if you gave one.
- If the switch has any **encrypted items**, the email has an **Open what was left for you** button —
    a personal link for that recipient.
- On **Pro**, a recipient with a phone number also gets a text message telling them to check their
    email.

If an email can't be delivered at first, Quietletter keeps retrying for up to **24 hours** after the
switch fired.

## The release page

The link opens a page showing the switch's **title**, a greeting by name if you gave one,
**Delivered on** with the date, and each item — readable messages appear right away:

- **Encrypted items** each ask for their **own passphrase** — the one you set for that item. The
    recipient types it and taps **Unlock**; decryption happens in their browser, and the passphrase is
    never sent anywhere. A wrong one shows *Wrong passphrase, try again.*
- **Files** then show **Download** with the file's name.
- **Private pages** and **secrets** appear as text once unlocked.

## How long it works

Release links and the encrypted content behind them are kept for **90 days** after the switch fires,
then deleted for good. After that the page shows *Link not available*.

## Help your recipients be ready

<Steps>
    <Step title="Tell them it may come">
        Let each recipient know they might one day get an email from Quietletter, and to check their spam
        folder if they expect one.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Give them the passphrases">
        Share each encrypted item's passphrase **outside the app** — in person, in a sealed letter, with
        your will. Without it, nobody can open that item.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Tell them about the 90 days">
        Encrypted items can be opened for 90 days after the switch fires. Recipients should download files
        and copy text they want to keep.
    </Step>
</Steps>
