## What the server can and can't see

| The server can see | The server can never see |
| --- | --- |
| Your switches' **titles**, schedules, state, check-in and fire times, time zone | The **contents** of encrypted messages, files, secrets and private pages |
| **Recipients** — emails, names and phone numbers — and your warning phone | Any **passphrase** |
| Which **actions** a switch has, message **subjects**, file **names, types and sizes** | The decryption key in usable form |
| **Readable messages** — the ones you chose to deliver as normal email | |

In one line: **we can see *that* a switch exists, who it's for and *when* it fires — never what's
inside an encrypted item.**

## How the encryption works

<Steps>
    <Step title="Encrypted on your device">
        When you save an encrypted item, your device encrypts it with a fresh random key
        (XChaCha20-Poly1305). That key is itself locked with a key derived from the item's **passphrase**
        (Argon2id). What's uploaded is the ciphertext, the locked key and the values needed to unlock it
        — never the passphrase or the readable content.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Share the passphrase out-of-band">
        You give the passphrase to your recipient **outside the app** — in a will, a sealed envelope, or
        in person. We never see it, so we can't lose it or hand it over.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Your recipient decrypts in their browser">
        When the switch fires, your recipient opens their link and enters the passphrase. Decryption
        happens **entirely in their browser**.
    </Step>
</Steps>

Once saved, encrypted content can't be read back — not even by you. That's the point.

<Warning>
    **Use long passphrases.** The app requires at least 12 characters and rates each passphrase as you
    type. The locked key is stored on the server (and in your export files), so a weak passphrase could
    be guessed by anyone who obtained that data. And if a passphrase is lost, the content can't be
    recovered by anyone.
</Warning>

## Readable messages

Not everything has to be encrypted. A **Message** is delivered as a normal readable email by default,
clearly labelled *Delivered as a normal email — not encrypted*. Switch it to **Encrypted** if it
matters. Files, secrets and private pages are always encrypted.

## Your account

- **Sign in** with your email and a 6-digit code (valid for 10 minutes). After five wrong codes, the
    app makes you wait five minutes.
- Your **account is always recoverable by email**, independently of any passphrase — forgetting a
    passphrase never locks you out of *managing* your switches, only out of reading that encrypted
    item.
- **Lock the app** on iPhone and Android with **Settings → Privacy & security → Lock with Face ID /
    fingerprint**: it asks when the app opens and after 5 minutes in the background. The check is done
    by your phone — nothing about your face or fingerprint reaches us.
- **Log out** removes the app's data and your check-in PIN from that device. Your switches stay on
    the server and keep running.

## Export or delete your data

Both are in your account on burakov.net — **Account → Data & deletion**:

- **Export your data → Download my data** gives you one file with your data from every app,
    including your switches, recipients and encrypted payloads exactly as stored. To move switches to
    another Quietletter, use [Back up and move your switches](/quietletter/backup-and-transfer) instead.
- **Delete my account** signs you out at once. You can restore the account within **30 days** by
    signing in again; after that, everything is erased.

<Warning>
    **Deleting your account stops your switches.** From that moment they no longer warn or fire — your
    recipients will receive nothing. Deleting the account also doesn't cancel an App Store or Google
    Play subscription; cancel it there.
</Warning>
