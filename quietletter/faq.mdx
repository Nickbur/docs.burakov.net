<AccordionGroup>
    <Accordion title="What if I just forget to check in for a while?">
        Nothing fires quietly. Your device reminds you before each deadline, and after a missed deadline
        the switch enters its grace period and warns you by push and email (and SMS on Pro). Only after the
        full grace period — never less than 48 hours, and typically days or weeks — does it fire. Check in
        any time before that. See [Schedule, grace & warnings](/quietletter/how-it-works).
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Can someone trigger my switch early?">
        No. A switch fires on **inactivity only** — your own missed check-ins. No recipient, trustee or
        support agent can fire it early.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="What happens if I lose a passphrase?">
        Encrypted content is **zero-knowledge**: if the passphrase is lost, that item can't be recovered —
        not even by us. Keep it where you'd keep a will, and make sure your recipient will have it. Your
        **account** itself is always recoverable by email.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="I forgot my check-in PIN.">
        Tap **Forgot PIN? Reset it** and set a new one. The PIN is a confirmation step on your device, not
        a password. See [Check in](/quietletter/check-in).
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Can I read what I saved?">
        Readable messages, yes. Encrypted content is never shown again after saving — not even to you. To
        change it, enter the new content with a passphrase in **Edit switch**.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Does my recipient need an account or the app?">
        No. They get an email; readable messages are right in it, and encrypted items open in any browser
        from a private link, with the passphrase you shared. See
        [What your recipients receive](/quietletter/recipients).
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="What does it cost?">
        The free plan covers one switch with message actions and the full check-in loop. **Pro
        ($19.99/year)** adds more switches, encrypted files, secrets, private pages and SMS. If Pro ends,
        your switches keep running and still deliver everything. See [Pricing](/quietletter/pricing).
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Which platforms are supported?">
        iPhone, Android and the web. The recipient release page is always web, so anyone can open what you
        left them without installing anything.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Can I run it myself?">
        Yes — the core is open-source (AGPL-3.0) and self-hostable. See
        [Run your own instance](/quietletter/self-hosting).
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Can I move my switches between the hosted service and my own instance?">
        Yes, in either direction, with one export file. Encrypted contents stay encrypted and open with the
        same passphrase, and an imported switch starts its timer fresh, so nothing fires early. See
        [Back up and move your switches](/quietletter/backup-and-transfer).
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="What happens to my switches if I delete my account?">
        They stop — they will no longer warn or fire. You can restore your account within 30 days by
        signing in again; your switches then stay **Paused** until you re-arm each one in the app, so
        nothing fires without your check-in. See [Encryption & privacy](/quietletter/zero-knowledge).
    </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
