## Nothing is painting

Work through these:

1. **Is Fogline on?** If the button on the map says **Start**, tap it — Fogline then stays on until you tap
    **Pause**.
2. **Are you in a covered region?** Fogline has road data for Kyiv, the Kyiv region and the other regions of
    Ukraine. The first time you're in a region, it needs a connection to download that region's road data. See
    [Regions & offline maps](/fogline/offline-maps).
3. **Is location allowed — and precise?** If not, Fogline shows a message with a shortcut to your phone's settings
    (iPhone: **Always** and **Precise Location**; Android: allowed, **Precise** on).
4. **Is location turned on** for the phone at all?

## Painting stops when the screen is off

- iPhone: allow location **Always**.
- Android: allow notifications for Fogline (Android 13+) and set its battery usage to **Unrestricted**.
- If you swiped the app away, tracking paused — open Fogline again and it picks up where it left off.

See [Keep painting in the background](/fogline/background-tracking).

## A road I drove isn't painted, or one I didn't is

GPS can be off by tens of metres, especially among tall buildings and in tunnels. A stretch is painted when a
position lands within 45 metres of it — so drift can miss a stretch or paint a nearby one.

## My coverage seems low

Coverage counts every mapped path — footpaths, service roads and steps too — by number of stretches. See
[Track your coverage](/fogline/coverage-and-stats).

## The map is blank

You haven't downloaded the base map — your painted roads still show. Tap **Download** on the banner or in
**Settings → Map data**. If the button comes back without finishing, the download failed — try again on Wi-Fi.

## My roads disappeared after logging out

Logging out removes Fogline's data from the phone immediately, including roads not yet synced. Sign in again to
get back everything that had synced.

## Sign-in messages

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| *Incorrect validation code. Please try again.* | Check the latest code, or ask for a new one. |
| *Too many attempts. Please wait N min.* | Wait the time shown. |
| *Invalid email format* | Check the email address. |
| *Unable to connect. Please try again later.* | Check your connection. |
| *Apple sign-in failed. Please try again.* | Try again, or sign in with your email. |
