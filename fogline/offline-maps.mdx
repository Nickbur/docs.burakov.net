---
title: "Offline maps"
description: "Download a region once and paint it with no signal; add other regions as downloadable packs."
---

Fogline is **offline-first**. Once you've downloaded a region, both the basemap and the road network
live on your device — so your map paints and displays with **no mobile signal**, wherever you happen
to travel.

## Download a region

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get the Kyiv & oblast pack">
    The main pack covers **Kyiv and the surrounding oblast**. Download it once, on Wi-Fi, and you're
    set to start painting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add other regions">
    Other regions are available as **separate downloadable packs**. Grab the ones you travel in; skip
    the rest.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage storage">
    You can delete a pack any time to free space and re-download it later — your painted roads are
    kept safe on your account regardless.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Map packs are **not bundled into the app** — you download them on first use — so the install stays
  small and you only store the regions you actually travel.
</Note>

## Keeping a region fresh

The road network changes slowly, so region packs are **regenerated occasionally** and you update them
**on demand**. Each pack shows a **"data as of"** date, so you always know how fresh it is, and
updating a pack keeps every road you've already painted.
