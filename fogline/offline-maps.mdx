## Where Fogline paints

Fogline needs **road data** for an area to paint it. It's available for **Kyiv**, the **Kyiv region** and the other
regions (oblasts) of Ukraine. Fogline picks your region automatically from your location and switches as you cross
into another one. Outside Ukraine, nothing is painted.

## Two kinds of data

| Data | What it's for | How you get it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Road data** | The road stretches Fogline paints | Downloaded automatically the first time you're in a region, then kept on your phone |
| **Base map** | Streets, labels and landscape you see under your roads | You download it per region — painting works without it |

## Download a base map

- On the Map screen, tap **Download** on the banner, or use **Settings → Map data**. Progress shows as a
    percentage; when it's done, Settings says *Downloaded — available offline*.
- **Delete** in Settings removes a region's base map to free space — your painted roads stay.
- Downloaded maps don't update themselves; **Settings → Map data** shows the date each region's data is from
    (*Data as of …*). Delete and download again for a newer one.

If a download fails, the **Download** button comes back — try again on a stable Wi-Fi connection.
