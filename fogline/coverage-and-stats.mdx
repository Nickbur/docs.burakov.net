## Coverage %

Your coverage is the share of an area's road stretches you've painted:

> Coverage = painted stretches ÷ all stretches in the area

It counts **stretches**, not kilometres, and every kind of road and path counts — see
[What counts as a road](/fogline/painting-roads#what-counts-as-a-road). It's shown for your **city** and for the
**district** you're in, on the Map and on Stats.

## The Stats screen

A big **coverage ring** shows your % for your city, with your district's % beside it, and these numbers:

| Number | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Roads painted** | Road stretches you've painted — everywhere |
| **km painted** | The length of what you've painted |
| **New this week** | Stretches painted for the first time in the last 7 days |
| **This city** / **This district** | Stretches painted out of all stretches in your city / district |

*Coverage syncs to your account. Raw location traces never leave your device.*

## Reset your map

**Reset map** at the bottom of Stats clears every painted road — on this device, your other devices and in your
account — after confirming *Reset your map?* If you're offline, the reset reaches your account as soon as you're back
online. It can't be undone.
