## Getting around

The bottom bar has **Menu**, **Map** and **Stats**. The menu holds **About**, **Settings** and **Log out**.

## Screens

| Screen | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Map** | Your painted roads, your coverage, **Start** / **Pause**, and the map controls. See [Explore your map](/fogline/the-map-screen). |
| **Stats** | Coverage for your city and district, your numbers, and **Reset map**. See [Track your coverage](/fogline/coverage-and-stats). |
| **Settings** | The options below. |
| **About** | The version and update notice, a link to these docs, the Privacy Policy and Terms. |

## Settings

| Setting | Options | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Map data** | Your current region's base map: **Download**, or **Delete** once downloaded; other downloaded regions with **Delete**; each shows the date its data is from (*Data as of …*) | — see [Regions & offline maps](/fogline/offline-maps) |
| **Appearance** | Auto, Light, Dark — the map follows it | Auto (follows your phone) |
| **Language** | System language, English, Русский, Українська | System language |
| **Lock with Face ID / fingerprint** | On / Off | Off — see [Account & privacy](/fogline/accounts-and-privacy#lock-the-app) |
| **Send crash reports** | On / Off | On |

## Update prompts

When a new version is out, **About** says *A new version is available* (a dot on **Menu** points to it). If
your version is too old to keep working, an **Update required** screen asks you to update first.
