## One free account

Fogline uses a single **free burakov account** (email, Google, or Apple) that works across all your
apps and devices. **Signing in is required** — it's what lets your painted map back up and follow you
from one phone to the next. There are **no subscriptions** and nothing to buy.

## Private by design

Fogline is built so the **sensitive part never leaves your phone**:

- **Your raw location trail** — the record of where and when you actually travelled — stays **on your
    device** and is used only to paint roads. It is **never uploaded**.
- **Only your painted roads** — the plain list of which road stretches are lit up, plus your coverage
    totals — sync to your account, so your map can back up and reach your other devices.
- **The basemap and road network** live **offline on your device** as downloaded packs.

The server knows which roads you've coloured in, but not the routes, times, or trips that painted
them.

## Permissions it asks for

<Steps>
    <Step title="Location — including in the background">
        Fogline needs **background (always) location** so it can paint roads while the app is closed and
        the screen is off. That passive tracking is the whole point — without it, there's nothing to
        paint.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Notifications — on Android">
        On Android, Fogline shows a quiet, ongoing notification while it's tracking in the background —
        that's how the system lets an app keep painting with the screen off, so you can always tell it's
        running.
    </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
    Fogline is a **personal project, not a business** — your data isn't sold or used for ads. Because a
    movement map is personal, it's built to keep your raw trail on your device and share only the roads
    you've painted.
</Note>
