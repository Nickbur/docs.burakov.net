## Your account

Sign in with your **email and a one-time code** — no password. On iPhone you can also use **Sign in with
Apple**. Signing in is required: it's what backs up your map and brings it to your other devices. It's one free
account across the apps at [burakov.net](https://burakov.net); there's nothing to buy.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **Location** — *Always* on iPhone, *while using* on Android, with **Precise** on | To match where you are to roads, including with the screen off. Approximate location is too coarse to paint anything. |
| **Notifications** *(Android 13+)* | To show the notification Android requires while Fogline tracks in the background. |

See [Keep painting in the background](/fogline/background-tracking).

## What's stored where

| On your phone | On the server, with your account |
| --- | --- |
| Your painted road stretches, with the time each was painted | Your painted road stretches and their region — nothing else |
| Recent positions, only until they're matched to roads | — |
| Downloaded maps and road data | — |
| Your settings | — |

**Your positions never leave your phone.** Fogline keeps them only until they're matched to roads, then deletes
them. The server gets only the list of road stretches you've painted — not your positions, your route, or when you
travelled.

Your data isn't sold or used for ads.

## Other services

- **Crash reports**, if **Send crash reports** is on (it is by default) — turn it off in Settings.
- **Apple**, if you use Sign in with Apple.
- Maps and road data are downloaded from our own server.

## Lock the app

Turn on **Settings → Privacy & security → Lock with Face ID / fingerprint**. Fogline then asks for your face or
fingerprint when it opens and after 5 minutes in the background (on iPhone your device passcode works too). The
check is done by your phone itself — nothing about your face or fingerprint reaches the app or our servers.

## Export or delete your data

Both are in the app — **Account → Account settings** — and in your account on burakov.net —
**Account → Data & deletion**:

- **Export my data** in the app gives you one file with your account and your Fogline coverage. On burakov.net,
    **Export your data → Download my data** gives you one file with your data from every app.
- **Delete account** (in the app) or **Delete my account** (on burakov.net) signs you out at once and removes
    your data from **every** app on the account. You can
    restore it within **30 days** by signing in again.
