title: "Quickstart"
description: "Install Fogline, sign in, download your region map, and start painting the roads you travel."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the app">
    Get Fogline from the [App Store](https://burakov.net/projects/fogline) or
    [Google Play](https://burakov.net/projects/fogline). It runs on **iOS and Android** — phones and
    tablets.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Create or sign in with a **free burakov account** (email, Google, or Apple). One account works
    across all your devices. Signing in is what lets your painted map back up and follow you from one
    phone to the next.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download your region">
    Download the **Kyiv & oblast** map pack — do it on Wi-Fi. It holds the road network and the
    basemap, so painting works fully offline afterwards.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow background location, then go">
    Grant **background location** so Fogline can paint while the app is closed, then simply go about
    your day. Every road you drive or walk starts filling in on its own.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Fogline is built to run **quietly in the background** — you don't keep it open while you travel.
  Set it up once, and it paints as you live your life.
</Tip>

## What you need

- A phone or tablet on **iOS or Android**.
- A **free burakov account**.
- A one-time **map download** for your region (Wi-Fi recommended).
- **Background-location permission**, so roads paint even with the app closed and the screen off.
