<Steps>
    <Step title="Install the app">
        Get Fogline from the [project page](https://burakov.net/projects/fogline) — for **iPhone and iPad (iOS 15
        or later)** or **Android 7.0 or later**.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Sign in">
        Enter your email and type the code you receive — no password. On iPhone you can also use **Sign in with
        Apple**. Signing in is required; it's free, and it's what backs up your map.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Tap Start">
        On the **Map** screen, tap **Start**. Fogline explains why it needs your location in the background — and
        that your positions never leave the phone — then asks for access:
        - **iPhone:** choose **Always** and keep **Precise** on, so it can keep painting with the screen off.
        - **Android:** allow location with **Precise** on.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Travel">
        Drive or walk anywhere in [the regions Fogline covers](/fogline/offline-maps) — Kyiv, the Kyiv region and
        the rest of Ukraine. The roads you travel are painted in as you go.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Download the map (optional)">
        On the map, tap **Download** on the banner to save your region's base map for offline use. Painting works
        without it — the map just shows a plain background.
    </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
    You only tap **Start** once: Fogline stays on — with the screen off and when you reopen the app — until you tap
    **Pause**. If you swipe the app away or restart your phone, tracking picks up again the next time you open
    Fogline.
</Note>
