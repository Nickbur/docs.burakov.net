Once you've tapped **Start**, Fogline keeps painting with the screen off or another app open, and carries on
whenever you reopen the app — until you tap **Pause**.

## Before Fogline asks for location

The first time you tap **Start**, Fogline explains what it needs and why: your location **in the background**, so
roads keep painting while the phone is in your pocket; **precise** location, because approximate location is too
coarse to tell one street from the next. Your positions stay on the phone — only the painted road stretches sync.
You can tap **Pause** at any time.

If location is off or not allowed, Fogline tells you and offers to open your phone's settings.

## iPhone and iPad

- Allow location **Always**, with **Precise Location** on (Settings → Fogline → Location).
- While Fogline is tracking in the background, iOS shows the blue location indicator.

## Android

- Allow location with **Precise** on. "While using the app" is enough — Fogline keeps tracking through a
    visible notification.
- While it's tracking, an ongoing notification reads *Fogline is painting your roads — Recording the roads you
    travel.* On Android 13 and later, allow notifications for Fogline so the notification can show.
- Some phones stop background apps to save battery. If painting stops when the screen is off, set Fogline's
    battery usage to **Unrestricted** (or exclude it from battery optimisation) in your phone's settings.

## What stops tracking

- Tapping **Pause**.
- Logging out.
- **Closing the app completely** (swiping it away) or restarting the phone pauses tracking until you next open
    Fogline — it then picks up on its own; you don't need to tap **Start**.

## Battery

Fogline records a position only after you've moved about 25 metres and stays quiet while you're standing still, so
a parked phone uses very little battery. On long drives GPS works constantly — keep the phone on a charger.

<Note>
    Fogline doesn't keep your route. Positions stay on your phone only until they're matched to roads, then they're
    deleted — they never leave the phone. See [Account & privacy](/fogline/accounts-and-privacy).
</Note>
