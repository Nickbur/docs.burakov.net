## What syncs

Your **painted roads** — which road stretches you've painted, in which region — back up to your account. Only the
stretches are sent — never your positions, route, speed, or the times you travelled.

## When it syncs

- Shortly after new roads are painted,
- when you come back to the app, and
- when you sign in.

On another device, sign in with the same account: your painted roads appear there too.

## Without a connection

Fogline works offline. Once a region's road data is on your phone, painting, the map and your stats all work without
a connection, and the roads you paint sync as soon as you're back online.

## How devices merge

Roads painted on any device are **added together**. Nothing is ever un-painted by syncing — the map only
grows, until you reset it.

## Logging out

**Log out** stops tracking and removes Fogline's data from this device straight away, without asking — including
roads painted since the last sync. Open the app online for a moment first so everything syncs.

## Resetting

**Reset map** on the Stats screen clears your painted roads on every device signed in to your account; if you're
offline, it reaches your account as soon as you're back online. See
[Track your coverage](/fogline/coverage-and-stats#reset-your-map).
