---
title: "FAQ"
description: "Coverage, platforms, background tracking, battery, privacy, and the honest edges."
---

<AccordionGroup>
    <Accordion title="What does it cost?">
        Nothing. Fogline is a **personal project — free, with no subscriptions and no in-app
        purchases**. One burakov account works across all your devices.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Which platforms are supported?">
        **iOS and Android**, phones and tablets, from one account. It's **mobile-only** — there's no
        desktop or web version, because the whole idea depends on the phone you carry with you.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Does it track me in the background?">
        Yes — that's the point. Fogline records where you travel with the app closed and the screen off,
        so roads paint themselves without you doing anything. Your raw trail stays on the phone; only the
        roads you've painted back up.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Where does it work?">
        **Kyiv and the surrounding oblast** today, with **other regions as downloadable map packs**. Once
        a region is downloaded, painting and the map work fully offline.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Why does it need me to sign in?">
        So your painted map **backs up and follows you across devices**. Sign in on a new phone and your
        coverage is right there. The account is free.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Will it drain my battery?">
        Fogline samples your location **only as you move** and stays quiet when you're still, so it's
        tuned to sip battery in the background rather than run flat out. Longer trips naturally use more.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Does my location leave my phone?">
        No. Your **raw location trail never leaves the device** — it's used only to paint roads. The only
        thing that syncs is the plain list of roads you've painted, plus your coverage totals.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="What counts as painting a road?">
        **One pass, in any direction, by any means** — driving, walking, cycling, or riding as a
        passenger. Roads are split into stretches of about **40 metres**; travel one and it's painted for
        good.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="How accurate is it?">
        Your path is snapped to the **OpenStreetMap** road network, and GPS noise and standing still are
        filtered out, so your map follows real streets. Nothing is perfect — occasional GPS drift can
        miss or nudge a stretch.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Can I start over?">
        Yes. **Reset map** clears every painted road and sets your coverage back to zero, so your city
        starts fully fogged again. It's permanent.
    </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
