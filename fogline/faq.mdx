<AccordionGroup>
    <Accordion title="What does it cost?">
        Nothing — free, with no subscriptions and no in-app purchases.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Which platforms are supported?">
        **iPhone and iPad** (iOS 15 or later) and **Android** (7.0 or later). There's no web or desktop version —
        the idea depends on the phone you carry.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Where does it work?">
        In **Kyiv**, the **Kyiv region** and the other regions of Ukraine. Fogline downloads a region's road data
        the first time you're there. See [Regions & offline maps](/fogline/offline-maps).
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Does it paint automatically?">
        Yes. Tap **Start** once and Fogline keeps painting in the background — with the screen off and when you
        reopen the app — until you tap **Pause**. If you swipe the app away or restart your phone, it carries on as
        soon as you open Fogline again.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Why does it need me to sign in?">
        So your painted map backs up and follows you to other devices. The account is free.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Will it drain my battery?">
        Fogline records a position only after you've moved about 25 metres and stays quiet while you're standing
        still, so a parked phone uses very little battery. On long drives GPS works constantly — keep the phone on a
        charger, and tap **Pause** when you don't need it.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Does my location leave my phone?">
        Your positions don't — they stay on the phone only until they're matched to roads, then they're deleted.
        What syncs is the list of road stretches you've painted, nothing more. See
        [Account & privacy](/fogline/accounts-and-privacy).
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="What counts as painting a road?">
        One pass, in any direction, by any means — driving, walking or as a passenger. Roads are split into stretches
        of about **40 metres**; your position must be within 45 metres of one to paint it.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Why is my coverage so low?">
        Every mapped path counts — footpaths, service roads and steps included — and coverage counts stretches, not
        kilometres. See [Track your coverage](/fogline/coverage-and-stats).
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="How accurate is it?">
        Positions are snapped to the OpenStreetMap road network. GPS drift can occasionally paint a road you only
        passed near, or miss a stretch.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Can I start over?">
        Yes — **Reset map** on the Stats screen clears every painted road, on all your devices and in your account.
        It's permanent.
    </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
