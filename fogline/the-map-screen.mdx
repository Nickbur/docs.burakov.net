## What's on the map

- **Painted roads** in bright cyan, under the street names; everything else stays plain — the fog.
- Your **position**, once Fogline is on and the phone has a fix, as a marker pointing the way you're
    travelling.
- At the top left: your **coverage %** for your city and district, and how many road stretches you've painted in
    total.

The map opens on your painted roads and moves to your position once the phone has a fix. Street names are in
Ukrainian, and the map is light or dark with your **Appearance** setting.

## Controls

- **Recenter** — follow your position again (highlighted while it's following).
- **Zoom in** / **Zoom out**.
- **Start** / **Pause** — see [Paint roads as you travel](/fogline/painting-roads).

Moving or pinching the map yourself stops following; **Recenter** picks it up again. The map stays north-up.

## The download banner

If the base map for your region isn't on your phone yet, a banner shows the region and *Download this region
(… MB) to explore offline.* with **Download**. Without it you still see your painted roads, over a plain
background. See [Regions & offline maps](/fogline/offline-maps).
