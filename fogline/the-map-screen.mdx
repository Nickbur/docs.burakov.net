title: "The map screen"
description: "The live map: your position, the roads you've painted, and everywhere you haven't been yet."
---

The map is the whole point of Fogline. It opens on your **live position** over a clean basemap, with
every road you've already travelled **painted in** and everywhere you haven't been yet left fogged.
There's nothing to start or stop — you open it to see how far your map has spread.

## What you're looking at

<Steps>
  <Step title="Your position">
    A live puck shows where you are and which way you're facing, and the map follows you as you move.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Painted roads">
    Every road you've travelled is lit up in the accent colour. This is your coverage — it only ever
    grows.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The fog">
    Roads you haven't travelled stay muted, so the gap between where you've been and where you
    haven't is obvious at a glance.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Fogline keeps the map **clean and readable** — the painted roads are the highlight, not a heavy fog
  cloud laid over everything. The pleasure is watching the blank spaces quietly close up.
</Note>

## Coverage, right on the map

Alongside the map you can see your **% coverage** for the region and a **coverage ring** that fills
as you paint more of it. The full breakdown — roads painted, kilometres painted, and what's new this
week — lives one tap away on the [Coverage & stats](/fogline/coverage-and-stats) screen.

## Day and night

The map **auto-switches between day and night** for a high-contrast, easy-to-read display in any
light, and you can pan and zoom freely to explore where you've been and where you still haven't.
