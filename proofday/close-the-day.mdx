The heart of Proofday is one small ritual: **closing the day**. It's deliberately minimal, so it's easy
to keep up every single day.

## Close today

On **Today**, answer the prompt — *What did you get done today, and how did it move you forward?* — in
a line or two. Optionally tap a **mood** from 1 to 5 (tap the selected one again to clear it). Then tap
**Close the day**.

- You'll see *Day closed — nicely done.*, the title changes to **Day won**, and the badge *You showed
    up today — logged and won.* appears.
- An empty entry can't close the day: *Write at least one line to close the day.*
- Changed your mind? Edit the text and tap **Update entry**.
- An entry can be up to 20,000 characters.

Quality is never judged. A quiet day counts exactly as much as a big one.

## Take a rest day

Need a day off? While today is still open, tap **Take a rest day instead**. You'll see *Rest day set —
your streak is safe.* A rest day doesn't add to your streak, and it doesn't break it either. You can
take one whenever you need — there's no limit. If you end up writing an entry after all, closing the
day turns it into a win.

You can also plan a rest day ahead: open an upcoming day from the **Calendar** and mark it as a rest day.
Past days can't become rest days — [fill them in](/proofday/past-days) instead.

## Won, rest or missed

| Day | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Won** | Closed with an entry — today or later from the calendar. |
| **Rest** | You took, or planned, a rest day. |
| **Missed** | A past day with no entry and no rest day. |

Today stays **open** until midnight on your device's clock — it's never "missed" while it's still
today. A missed day isn't final: you can [fill it in later](/proofday/past-days) from the calendar.

<Note>
    The day ends at **midnight on your device's clock**, wherever you are. Writing about yesterday after
    midnight? Open yesterday from the **Calendar** — Today is already the new day.
</Note>
