Proofday runs on **iPhone, Android and the web**, all from one account. Get the app from the
[project page](https://burakov.net/projects/proofday), or open
[proofday.burakov.net](https://proofday.burakov.net) in a browser.

<Steps>
    <Step title="Sign in with your email">
        Enter your email and type the 6-digit code you receive — no password. If you don't have an account
        yet, this creates one. You can also sign in with **Google**, or with **Sign in with Apple** on iPhone.
        Right after you first sign in, Proofday asks to send notifications — allow it so your evening reminder
        can reach you.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Close your first day">
        On **Today**, write a line about what you got done, pick a mood if you like, and tap **Close the
        day**. The day is **won** and your streak starts at one.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Set up your evening reminder">
        Your evening reminder is already on at 21:00. To change it, open **Settings** (the gear icon on Today)
        and, under **Evening reminder**, choose the **Time** and **Intensity**. See
        [Set up your evening reminder](/proofday/reminders).
    </Step>
    <Step title="Lock it, if you like">
        On iPhone and Android you can lock Proofday with Face ID or your fingerprint in **Settings → Privacy
        & security**.
    </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
    If you skipped the notification prompt, allow notifications for Proofday in your phone's or browser's
    settings — the reminder can't reach you without them.
</Tip>
