Proofday reminds you in the evening to close the day. Set it up under **Settings → Evening reminder**.

## Time and intensity

| Setting | Options | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Daily reminder** | On / Off | On |
| **Time** | Any time | 21:00 |
| **Intensity** | Gentle, Firm, Relentless | Gentle |

The intensity decides how many reminders you get if the day is still open:

| Intensity | Reminders (minutes after your time) |
| --- | --- |
| **Gentle** | 0 — one reminder |
| **Firm** | 0, 45, 90 |
| **Relentless** | 0, 30, 60, 90, 120, 150 |

Reminders that would fall after midnight are skipped. The first one says *Take 60 seconds — what did
you get done today?*; follow-ups say *Still open — log today's win before midnight.* Tapping one opens
**Today**.

## Allow notifications

Proofday asks for permission to send notifications right after you first sign in. If you declined, you'll
see *Notifications are off — enable them to get your evening nudge.* when you change a reminder setting —
allow notifications for Proofday in your phone's or browser's settings.

## How reminders are scheduled

- On iPhone and Android, reminders are scheduled **on your phone** for the next 7 days and refreshed every
    time you open the app. If you don't open Proofday on that phone for more than a week, they run out until
    you do.
- **Closing the day** stops the rest of that evening's reminders. Web reminders stop wherever you closed the
    day; a phone keeps its own reminders until you open the app on it.
- On Android, reminders use their own **Daily reminder** notification channel.

## On the web

In the browser, reminders arrive as notifications even when Proofday isn't open in a tab, as long as your
browser allows notifications for proofday.burakov.net. They use the same time and intensity, and stop once
the day is closed.

<Note>
    Proofday never sends reminders by email. If you never allow notifications, everything still works — you
    just close the day when you open the app.
</Note>
