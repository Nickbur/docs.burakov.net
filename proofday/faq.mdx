<AccordionGroup>
    <Accordion title="What does it cost?">
        The daily habit is free — closing days, rest days, the calendar, streak, momentum, the reminder and
        your last 30 days. **Pro** adds the AI reports and your full history: **$29.99/year** (with a 7-day
        free trial), or **$59.99** once for Lifetime. See [Pricing](/proofday/pricing).
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="What actually counts as a 'won' day?">
        Closing the day with a non-empty entry. Proofday never judges the length or quality of what you
        wrote — showing up and logging honestly is the whole win.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="I missed a day. Did I lose my streak?">
        Fill it in: open the day from the **Calendar** and save an entry — it counts as won and your streak
        comes back. And on a day you plan to take off, tap **Take a rest day instead**: rest days pause the
        streak instead of breaking it. See [Keep your streak](/proofday/streaks-and-momentum).
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Can I plan rest days in advance?">
        Yes. Open an upcoming day from the **Calendar** and mark it as a rest day. Past days can't become rest
        days — fill them in instead.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Do I have to set goals?">
        No. Proofday is goal-free — there are no objectives or tags to maintain. The AI finds your trends
        from what you actually wrote.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Does it work offline?">
        Yes. You can close and edit days with no connection; they sync the next time you're online. AI
        reports need a connection. See [Use it offline and on several devices](/proofday/devices-and-offline).
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Are my entries private?">
        They're tied to your account and never shared, sold or used for ads. They aren't end-to-end
        encrypted, because syncing and the AI reports need to read them — see
        [Account & privacy](/proofday/privacy).
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Which platforms are supported?">
        iPhone, Android and the web — all from one account. There's no desktop app.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="What if I never enable notifications?">
        The app still works fully — you just close the day whenever you open it. The evening reminder is a
        convenience, not a requirement.
    </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
