## Offline first

Your entries are saved **on your device first**, so you can close a day, take a rest day or fix a past
day with **no connection**. Everything you need for the daily habit works on a plane.

AI reports need a connection.

## Syncing

Proofday syncs your journal to your account automatically:

- when you open the app or come back to it,
- a moment after you change something, and
- when your connection comes back.

There's no sync button and nothing to set up — sign in with the same account on each device.

## The same day on two devices

If you edit the same day on two devices while one of them is offline, the **most recent save wins** for
that whole day. To avoid surprises, let a device sync (open it while online) before editing the same day
elsewhere.

## Logging out

**Log out** from the menu removes Proofday's data from that device straight away, without asking —
including changes that haven't synced yet. Open the app online for a moment first so everything syncs.
Your journal stays safe in your account and comes back when you sign in again.

If a different account signs in on the same device, the previous account's data is removed from it
first.

<Note>
    Your **settings** — appearance, language and the evening reminder — are saved per device, so set them
    up on each device you use.
</Note>
