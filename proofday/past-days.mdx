## The calendar

**Calendar** shows one month at a time, weeks starting on Monday, with each day marked **Won**,
**Missed** or **Rest**. Move back as far as you like, or ahead to plan rest days. Days before you started
using Proofday stay blank — they never count as missed.

## Open a day

Tap any day to open it — past days to read, fill in or fix, upcoming days to plan a rest day. It shows the
day's status — **Won**,
**Rest day**, **Missed**, **Not closed yet** or **Upcoming** — and its entry.

## Fill in a day you missed

Open the missed day, answer *What did you get done that day?*, and tap **Save the day**. The day becomes
**won**, like any other — your streak and momentum count it. There's no deadline for this: any past day
you can open can be filled in.

## Change or remove an entry

- **Change** — edit the text or mood and tap **Update entry**.
- **Remove** — tap **Remove this entry**. It's removed right away, **without asking**, and a past day
    becomes **Missed**. There's no undo.

## The free plan's 30 days

On the free plan, you can open the **last 30 days**. Older days show a lock; opening one shows *Older
history is a Pro feature* with **Unlock full history**. Nothing is deleted — your older entries are kept,
still count toward your **Wins**, and come back as soon as you have Pro. See [Pricing](/proofday/pricing).
