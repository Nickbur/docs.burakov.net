## How it works

The AI reads **your own entries** and reflects them back in a clear, encouraging voice. It never
invents anything — the achievements are yours; it just makes them legible. Generation happens on
the server, and results are cached so revisiting a recap doesn't regenerate it.

Because Proofday is **goal-free**, you never fill in objectives or tags. The trends and themes are
inferred from what you actually wrote, which is exactly what makes them honest.
