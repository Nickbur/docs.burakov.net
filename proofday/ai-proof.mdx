Your daily entries build a record of everything you got done. On **Pro**, the **Proof** tab turns that
record into four reports, written by AI from **your own words** — it doesn't invent achievements, it makes
yours legible.

## The four reports

| Report | Covers | Needs at least | You get |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Week** | The current week, Monday to Sunday | 1 day with an entry | One short paragraph |
| **Month** | The current month | 1 day with an entry | Two or three paragraphs |
| **Trends** | The current year | 5 days with entries | 3–6 themes, each marked as building or fading |
| **Brag-doc** | The current year | 3 days with entries | Sections of resume-style bullet points |

Only days with written entries count — rest days and empty days don't. The AI reads each day's date, mood
and text.

## Generate and regenerate

Open **Proof**, pick a report and tap **Generate**. It shows when it was generated. **Regenerate**
writes a fresh version and replaces the old one — there's one saved report per type and period.

Reports cover the **current** week, month or year by default; earlier weeks, months and years stay
available in **Proof**.

## Edit a report

You can edit the text of any report — your edits are saved to your account and sync to your other devices.
Once you've edited a report, **Regenerate** leaves your version alone, so your wording is never overwritten.

## Export your brag-doc

To use your brag-doc outside Proofday — for a performance review or your CV — open **Proof → Brag-doc** and tap
**Export**. It's shared through your phone's share sheet, or copied on the web, as themed sections of
resume-style bullet points.

| Message | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| *Not enough entries yet for this report — keep logging.* | The period doesn't have enough days with entries yet (see the table). |
| *The AI service is unavailable right now. Try again later.* | The AI service is temporarily down. |
| *Couldn't generate the report. Try again.* | Something else went wrong — including reaching the daily AI allowance (30 requests a day per account, shared across burakov apps). |

Reports need a connection, and they aren't stored on your device.
