The **Today** screen shows three numbers: **Streak**, **Momentum** and **Wins**. Here's exactly how
each is counted.

## Streak

Your **streak** counts back from today:

- every **won** day adds one;
- **rest** days — and today, while it's still open — are skipped: they neither add to it nor break it;
- the first **missed** day ends the count.

So a rest day is a pause, not a reset, and an unfinished today never costs you yesterday's streak.

## Momentum

**Momentum** is the share of your last 30 days that you won:

> Momentum = won days ÷ (won days + missed days), over the last 30 days

Rest days and an open today don't count either way, and days before you started using Proofday are
ignored. It shows 0% until at least one day has been decided. Unlike a streak, one missed day only dips
it a little — it's the honest picture of your consistency without the all-or-nothing anxiety.

## Wins

**Wins** is every day you've ever won — all-time, including days older than the free plan's 30-day
window.
