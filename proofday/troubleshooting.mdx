## I'm not getting the evening reminder

- **Notifications are off** for Proofday in your phone's or browser's settings (for example, you declined the
    prompt after signing in), or **Daily reminder** is switched off.
- **On iPhone or Android, you haven't opened the app for over a week.** Reminders are scheduled a week ahead;
    open Proofday to top them up.
- **On the web**, notifications are blocked for proofday.burakov.net in your browser, or the browser isn't
    running.
- **You closed the day on another device.** A phone keeps its own reminders until you open the app on it.

## I'm writing after midnight and Today shows the wrong day

The day ends at midnight on your device's clock. To write about yesterday, open yesterday from the
**Calendar**. If the app was left open overnight, close and reopen it so Today moves to the new date.

## An edit didn't show up on my other device

Both devices need to be online for a moment to sync. If the same day was edited on two devices while one
was offline, the most recent save wins.

## My older days are locked

On the free plan you can open the last 30 days. If you have Pro and still see locks, go online and tap
**Restore purchases** on the subscription screen. See [Pricing](/proofday/pricing).

## AI reports

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| *Not enough entries yet for this report — keep logging.* | Week and Month need 1 day with an entry, Brag-doc 3 and Trends 5 in the current year. |
| *The AI service is unavailable right now. Try again later.* | Try again later. |
| *Couldn't generate the report. Try again.* | Check your connection. If you've generated a lot today, you may have reached the daily AI allowance — try again tomorrow. |

Earlier weeks and months stay available in **Proof**; to use your brag-doc outside Proofday, export it. See
[AI proof](/proofday/ai-proof).

## I logged out and lost recent changes

Logging out from the menu removes Proofday's data from that device immediately, including anything not
yet synced. Before logging out, open the app online so it can sync.

## Sign-in messages

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| *Incorrect validation code. Please try again.* | Check the latest code in your inbox, or ask for a new one (after 90 seconds). |
| *Too many attempts. Please wait N min.* | Wait the time shown. |
| *Invalid email format* | Check the email address. |
| *Unable to connect. Please try again later.* | Check your internet connection. |
