## Getting around

On a phone or a narrow window, the bottom bar has **Menu**, **Today**, **Calendar** and **Proof**. On a
wide screen the menu stays open on the side: **Today**, **Calendar**, **Proof**, **Settings**,
**About** and **Log out**.

## Screens

| Screen | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Today** | Close or update today's entry, take a rest day, and see your **Streak**, **Momentum** and **Wins**. The gear icon opens Settings. See [Close your day](/proofday/close-the-day). |
| **Calendar** | A month view of your won, missed and rest days. Tap a day to open it. |
| **Day** | View, fill in, change or remove one day's entry, or plan an upcoming rest day. See [Fill in or fix a past day](/proofday/past-days). |
| **Proof** | Your AI reports — week, month, trends and brag-doc. Generate, edit and export them. See [AI proof](/proofday/ai-proof). |
| **Settings** | The options below. |
| **Subscription** | Pro plans and **Restore purchases**, or your current plan. See [Pricing](/proofday/pricing). |
| **About** | The version and update notice, a link to these docs, the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. |

## Settings

Settings are saved on each device and reset when you log out.

| Setting | Options | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Appearance** | Auto, Light, Dark | Auto (follows your device) |
| **Language** | System language, English, Русский | System language |
| **Daily reminder** | On / Off | On |
| **Time** | Any time of day | 21:00 |
| **Intensity** | Gentle, Firm, Relentless | Gentle — see [Set up your evening reminder](/proofday/reminders) |
| **Notifications on this device** | On / Off | On — notifications Proofday sends to this device from its servers; the evening reminder keeps its own switch above |
| **Permission** | Shows whether notifications are allowed, with **Allow** while they aren't | — |
| **Manage Pro** | Opens the subscription screen | — |
| **Lock with Face ID / fingerprint** *(iPhone and Android)* | On / Off | Off — see [Account & privacy](/proofday/privacy#lock-the-app) |
| **Send crash reports** | On / Off | On — turning it back on takes effect after a restart |

## Update prompts

When a new version is out, a dot appears on **Menu** and **About** says *A new version is available*. If
your version is too old to keep working, an **Update required** screen asks you to update first.
