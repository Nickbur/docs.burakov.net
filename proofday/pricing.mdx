## Plans

| Plan | Price | What you get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Free** | Free | Closing your days, rest days, the calendar, streak, momentum and wins, the evening reminder, app lock — and your **last 30 days** of history |
| **Pro** | $29.99 / year | Everything in Free, plus the **AI proof** reports and your **full history** |
| **Lifetime** | $59.99 once | Every Pro feature, for life |

The app shows the exact price before you pay; it can differ by country and store. The yearly plan starts
with a **7-day free trial**.

## What Pro unlocks

- **[AI proof](/proofday/ai-proof)** — weekly and monthly recaps, trends, and a career brag-doc you can
    export.
- **Full history** — open and edit any past day, not just the last 30. On the free plan older days are
    kept, just locked.

## Buy, restore and manage

- **Buy** from **Settings → Manage Pro**, or when you reach a Pro feature. On iPhone and Android you pay
    through the App Store or Google Play; on the web, through the web checkout.
- **Pro follows your account** to every device you sign in on.
- **Restore purchases** brings back a purchase made with the same store account.
- **Manage or cancel** from the subscription screen (**Manage in the App Store** / **Manage in Google
    Play**). A subscription bought on the web is managed from your account on burakov.net —
    **Account → Subscriptions**, where you can cancel it, resume it before it ends, or change plan.
- If a payment fails, the app shows a payment-problem notice so you can fix it.

## When Pro ends

Nothing is deleted. Days older than 30 days lock again, and new AI reports need Pro — reports you already
generated stay viewable.
