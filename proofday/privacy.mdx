Proofday keeps a journal of your days, so it's worth being clear about what's stored and why.

## What Proofday stores

Your **entries** — the daily notes and optional moods — are saved on your device first, then
synced to the server and tied to your account. That's what lets your journal follow you across
devices, and what the AI proof layer reads to build your recaps and brag-doc.

Because those features need to read your entries, Proofday is **not** end-to-end encrypted: your
notes are readable on the server to sync them and generate your AI proof. They're your private
journal, tied to your account — not shared, not sold, and not used to build an advertising
profile.

## One account, no separate sign-up

Proofday uses your single account across the Burakov apps — sign in once with an email code, no
password to manage. The same account works on iOS, Android, and the web, and your journal follows
you between them.

## What it needs, and what it doesn't

  </Card>
  <Card title="No tracking" icon="shield-check">
    No advertising profile, no engagement scoring. Proofday is a journal, not a feed.
  </Card>

<Note>
  You stay in control: edit or delete any day at any time, and you can delete your account, which
  removes your entries and reports.
</Note>
