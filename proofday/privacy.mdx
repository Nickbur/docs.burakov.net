## Sign in

You sign in with your **email and a 6-digit code** (valid for 10 minutes) — no password. You can also sign
in with Google, or with **Sign in with Apple** on iPhone. After five wrong codes, the app makes you wait five minutes. It's one
account across the apps at [burakov.net](https://burakov.net).

## What Proofday stores

Your **entries** — each day's text, mood and status — are saved on your device first, then synced to the
server and tied to your account. That's what lets your journal follow you across devices, and what the
AI reads when you generate a report. Generated **reports** are kept on the server too.

<Note>
    Proofday is **not** end-to-end encrypted. Your entries are stored readable on the server so they can
    sync and be used for your AI reports, and the service's operator can access them when needed to run
    the service or answer a support request. They're never shared, sold or used for advertising.
</Note>

## Which services see what

| Service | What it receives | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **An AI provider** | The entries of the week, month or year you're generating a report for | Only when you tap **Generate** (Pro) |
| **A crash-reporting service** | Error details with an account identifier (not your name or email), your time zone, and occasionally a recording of the app screen with all text masked | Only if **Send crash reports** is on — turn it off in Settings |
| **A push-notification service** | A device push token linked to your account, so reminders reach this phone or browser | Once you allow notifications |
| **Google or Apple** | Your sign-in request | Only if you sign in with them |
| **The App Store, Google Play or the web payment provider** | Your purchase | When you subscribe |

There's no advertising profile and no engagement scoring.

## Lock the app

On iPhone and Android you can turn on **Settings → Privacy & security → Lock with Face ID /
fingerprint**. Proofday then asks for your face or fingerprint when it opens and after 5 minutes in the
background (on iPhone your device passcode works too; on Android, your fingerprint or face). Turning the
lock on or off needs a successful check too. The check is done by your phone itself — nothing about your
face or fingerprint ever reaches the app or our servers.

## Export or delete your data

Both are in your account on burakov.net — **Account → Data & deletion**:

- **Export your data → Download my data** gives you one file with your data from every app, including
    your Proofday days and reports.
- **Delete my account** signs you out at once and removes your data from **every** app on the account.
    You can restore it within **30 days** by signing in again; after that it's erased for good.

Sign other devices out under **Account → Security**.

<Warning>
    Deleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription bought through the App Store or Google Play.
    Cancel it there first.
</Warning>
