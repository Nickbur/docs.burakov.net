Hushgard is a **self-hostable, end-to-end encrypted** communication platform for organizations. Run it on your own server — or let it be managed for you — invite your team, and get **encrypted chat and calls** on every device. Your messages, files and calls stay on infrastructure you control.

## Two audiences, one product

- **Members** use the apps — chat, calls, files and topics — on web, desktop, iOS and Android. Start with **For users**.
- **Owners and admins** buy and run the organization: the server, the admin panel, members and policy. Start with **For admins**.

## What's included

Every plan includes **encrypted chat and calls** and **high-security chats** for an unlimited number of members. Three optional modules can be added at checkout or later:

- **[Topics](/hushgard/topics)** — posts, comments, polls and reactions in public topics your whole organization can read and search, or private, end-to-end encrypted topics only their members can read.
- **[AI assistant](/hushgard/ai-assistant)** — ask questions across the chats and topics you belong to and get answers with links to their sources. It runs on your organization's own server, with the AI model your admin configures.
- **Enterprise controls** — [single sign-on & SCIM provisioning](/hushgard/sso) with your identity provider, and [device management](/hushgard/device-management): managed app configuration, device policies and remote wipe.

Chat is always on; your admin can switch calls, high-security chats and either module off for the organization. The apps speak **English, Russian, German, French, Spanish, Italian and Dutch**.
