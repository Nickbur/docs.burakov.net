Semble is a **video meeting in your browser** with one extra participant whenever you want it: **Kai**,
an AI that belongs to no one and answers anyone in the room. Start a sentence with "Kai" and ask — Kai
answers out loud, citing the documents shared with the meeting. While Kai is on, Semble keeps a live
transcript, and afterwards writes a summary of the meeting.

## The idea in one minute

<Steps>
    <Step title="Start a room">
        Sign in with an email code, start an instant meeting or schedule one, and share the link. Guests join
        from the link with just a name — no account, no install.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Bring Kai in">
        When the conversation needs it, the host turns Kai on. Kai joins as a participant tile and starts
        listening.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Ask by name">
        Anyone in the room says "Kai, …" followed by the question — or types it. Kai answers out loud for
        everyone, and the answer appears in text with its sources.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Walk away with the record">
        After the call the host has the transcript, a summary in the style they chose — and, if they
        recorded it, the audio.
    </Step>
</Steps>
