Semble is a **video meeting in your browser** with one extra participant whenever you want it:
**Kai**, an independent voice AI that belongs to no one and is equal to everyone in the room.
Say its name and ask — Kai answers out loud and in text, with citations from the documents
shared with the meeting, keeps a live transcript, and writes a neutral summary afterwards.

## The idea in one minute

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a room">
    Sign in with an email code, start an instant meeting or schedule one, and share the link.
    Guests join from the link with just a name — no account, no install.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bring Kai in">
    When the conversation needs it, the host turns Kai on. Kai joins as a participant tile and
    starts listening.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask by name">
    Anyone in the room — guests included — says "Kai, …" and asks — in English, Russian, and many other languages — or types the
    question. Kai answers out loud for everyone, with the same answer and its sources shown in text.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Walk away with the record">
    After the call the host has the attributed transcript, a neutral summary of key points,
    decisions, open questions and action items — and, if they recorded it, the audio.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Kai is nobody's assistant. It treats every participant the same, answers only when addressed,
  and never interrupts — if someone starts talking, it stops.
</Note>
