WhySoSlow is a **personal driving companion for Kyiv** on iPhone and Android. It's a **follow-map, not a
navigator**: no routes and no turn-by-turn, just a big display with the **speed limit in force next to your
speed**, and a warning **before each automatic speed camera**. It's free, and you can correct it — fix a
limit or add a camera from behind the wheel.

## What works offline

| Works with no signal | Needs a connection |
| --- | --- |
| The display, speed limits and camera warnings for **Kyiv and Kyiv Oblast** (their data comes with the app) | Signing in |
| Your own limit fixes and markers | Downloading the base map (about 100 MB per oblast) |
| The base map, once downloaded | Data for another region, the first time you drive there |
| | Camera updates (checked once a day) and map or road-data updates |
| | Syncing your edits, and uploading AI sign advisor photos (they wait on your phone until then) |
