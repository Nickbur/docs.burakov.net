WhySoSlow is a **personal, offline driving companion for Kyiv**. It's a **follow-map, not a
navigator**: no routes and no turn-by-turn, just a big heads-up display with the **speed limit in
force right next to your speed**, and a timely warning **before each automatic speed camera**. Everything works with no signal, and it's yours to correct — fix any limit or drop your
own markers from behind the wheel.

<Note>
    WhySoSlow is a personal project — **free, no subscriptions**. It covers Kyiv and the surrounding
    oblast today, with other regions available as downloadable map packs.
</Note>

## The idea in one minute

<Steps>
    <Step title="Mount your phone and start a drive">
        Put the phone in a car mount with the screen on, open WhySoSlow, and tap **Start**. The map
        follows you — there's nothing to type and nothing to tap while moving.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Watch one honest number">
        Your speed sits next to the limit in force. It turns **amber** the moment you're over the posted
        limit and **red** once you cross Ukraine's **+20 km/h** fineable line — a single calm beep, not
        a siren.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Let the cameras warn you first">
        About **30 seconds** before each automatic camera you get a distinct alert — only in the
        direction it enforces, and at the camera's own limit.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Fix what's wrong">
        Spotted a bad limit or a missing camera? Correct it from the driver's seat. Your edits sync
        across your phones and survive map updates.
    </Step>
</Steps>
