## How discounts combine

In one order, discounts apply in this order: **bundle discount → one coupon *or* gift certificate → reward points**,
then tax is added. So a shopper can combine a bundle, a coupon and points, but **not** a coupon with a gift
certificate — both go into the same code field, one code per order.

## Reward points

Points are for **signed-in customers** only. Set the rules in **Sales & Marketing → Loyalty points**.

| Global rule | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Earn rate | 1 | Points per 1 UAH paid for goods. |
| Redemption rate | 0.01 | Value of one point in UAH (100 points = 1 UAH). |
| Minimum to redeem | 100 | Points needed before they can be spent. |
| Expiry | 365 days | Points expire this long after they're earned. |

You can override the **earn rate** for a category or a product. A product-specific rate wins; otherwise the highest
rate among the product's categories; otherwise the global rate.

- Points are earned on what the customer actually pays for the goods, after discounts, and are credited when the
    order is placed.
- Points can pay for up to the full value of the goods.
- The customer sees their balance and history under **Account → Reward Points**; product pages show how many points a
    product earns.
- Expired points are removed by the background worker once a day.

<Warning>
    Points aren't taken back when an order is cancelled or refunded, and there's no manual adjustment screen yet.
</Warning>

## Coupons

Create codes in **Sales & Marketing → Coupons**:

- **Percentage** (up to 100%) or **fixed amount**.
- Optional **minimum order**, **start** and **expiry** dates, **total uses** and **uses per customer** (counted by
    account, or by email for guests).
- Codes are case-insensitive — they're stored upper-case.

## Gift certificates

Create them in **Sales & Marketing → Gifts** with a code, amount, optional expiry and description. A certificate
works like store balance: each order draws down what it uses, and the code stays valid until the balance reaches
zero. Certificates can be created and deleted, not edited.

## Bundles

Product sets (**Products → Product Sets**) sell several products together at a discount.

| Setting | Options |
| --- | --- |
| Members | Products and the quantity of each. |
| Discount | **Percent** off, a **fixed** amount off, or a **fixed price** for the whole set. |
| Schedule | Optional start and end dates. |
| Display | Heading, sort order, active flag. |

Bundles appear on `/bundles` and on their members' product pages. **Add set** puts every member in the cart or none
of them. The discount applies automatically whenever all members are in the cart, however they got there, and
disappears if one is removed.

## Sale prices

Give a product (or a SKU) a **compare-at price** above its price: it's shown crossed out, the product counts as
**on sale** in filters, and it's listed on `/special`.

## Promo banner

A store-wide announcement strip. Turn it on and set its text in **Settings → Marketing** (**Promo Banner Enabled**,
**Promo Banner Text**). The demo text promises free shipping — replace it with your own.

<Note>
    **Sales & Marketing → Discounts** stores discount rules but doesn't apply them yet — use coupons, bundles or
    compare-at prices.
</Note>
