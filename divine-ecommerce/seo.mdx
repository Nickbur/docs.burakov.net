Store-wide SEO lives in **Tools → Search Engine Optimization**. Products, categories, brands, articles and pages also
have their own meta title, description and keywords in their forms. Managers need the `seo` permissions to open the
section — see [Staff & permissions](/divine-ecommerce/staff-and-permissions).

## Titles and descriptions

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Meta Title Template** | Builds the title of every storefront page. Variables: `{page_title}`, `{site_name}`, `{category}`. Default: `{page_title} — {site_name}`. |
| **Meta Description Template** | The meta description for pages that don't have their own. |
| **Default Meta Keywords** | Meta keywords for pages that don't have their own. |

`{page_title}` is the page's own meta title, or its name when the meta title is empty. `{site_name}` is the site name
from **Settings → General**.

## robots.txt and sitemap

- **robots.txt content** — served at `/robots.txt`. The default allows everything and points to the sitemap:

    ```text
    User-agent: *
    Allow: /

    Sitemap: /sitemap.xml
    ```

- **XML Sitemap** — when on, `/sitemap.xml` lists the store's public pages for search engines.

## Indexing

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Canonical URLs** | Adds a canonical link to every page. On by default. |
| **No-index empty categories** | Marks categories without products `noindex`, so search engines skip them. |

Filtered listings are always marked `noindex,follow`, with a canonical link to the unfiltered page — see
[Filters and sorting](/divine-ecommerce/storefront#filters-and-sorting).

## Analytics

| Setting | Format |
| --- | --- |
| **Google Analytics ID** | `G-XXXXXXXXXX` |
| **Google Tag Manager** | `GTM-XXXXXXX` |
| **Facebook Pixel** | Your pixel ID |

Each filled-in ID adds its tag to every storefront page; leave a field empty to leave that tag out.
