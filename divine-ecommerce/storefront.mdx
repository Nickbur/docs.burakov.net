The storefront is server-rendered HTML with TypeScript on top, so pages work without JavaScript and get faster
interactions when it's on.

## Pages

| Page | URL | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home | `/` | The slider, product sections and anything plugins add to the page. |
| Catalog | `/products` | Every active product, with filters. |
| Category / brand | `/category/{id}`, `/brand/{id}` | One category or brand, with filters. `/brands` lists all brands. |
| Collections | `/latest`, `/special`, `/featured` | Newest products; products whose *compare-at* price is above their price; featured products. |
| Bundles | `/bundles` | Product sets sold together at a discount. |
| Product | `/product/{slug}` | Image gallery, options, cross-variants, specifications, bundles, reviews, 1-click order. |
| Search | `/search` | Results across the whole store. |
| Blog & pages | `/blog`, `/blog/{slug}`, `/pages/{slug}` | Articles and info pages. |
| Contacts | `/contacts` | Contact details, map and a contact form. |
| Cart & checkout | `/cart`, `/checkout` | See [Checkout, orders & returns](/divine-ecommerce/checkout-and-orders). |
| Lists | `/wishlist`, `/compare` | Saved and compared products. |
| Account | `/account/…` | The customer's own area. |
| For search engines | `/robots.txt`, `/sitemap.xml` | See [SEO & analytics](/divine-ecommerce/seo). |

Only products with the **Active** status appear anywhere or can be bought.

## Product page

- **Gallery** — the product's images, with zoom and a full-screen view.
- **Options** — a product can have several option axes (colour, storage…) and a SKU for each combination, with its
    own price, compare-at price, stock and image. The shopper picks a combination before adding to cart; listings
    show the cheapest active SKU's price.
- **Cross-variants** — separate products grouped together (for example the same phone in different colours) with a
    switcher between them.
- **Specifications** — grouped attributes, also used by filters and the compare list.
- **Reviews** — anyone can leave a 1–5 rating, a title, the review text, and pros and cons. Every review waits for
    approval in the admin. Reviews from customers who bought the product are marked **Verified purchase**.
- **1-click order** — a short form that sends a quick order to the admin (**Quick orders**).
- **Points preview** — how many reward points the product earns.
- **Warranty and returns** — the product's own warranty and return window, set in the product form.

<Note>
    The delivery lines and the store list on the product page are fixed text in
    `src/App/Frontend/views/components/product-buy.twig` — edit them to match your store.
</Note>

## Filters and sorting

Every listing — catalog, category, brand, search, collections and wishlist — has the same filter panel:

- Brand, option values, filterable specifications (number ranges, yes/no, value lists), **in stock**, **on sale**,
    price range and rating.
- Sort by relevance, name, price, rating, popularity, views or newest; 16, 24, 48 or 96 per page.

Filters update in place, and the same URLs work without JavaScript. Filtered pages are marked `noindex,follow` for
search engines. Which attributes appear is set in **Catalog Filters** — see
[Set up your catalog](/divine-ecommerce/catalog#filters).

## Search

Suggestions appear from the second character typed: live products, plus categories, brands, articles and pages.
The search page requires every word to match, and matches word beginnings. New categories, brands, articles and
pages show up in search within about a minute.

## Customer accounts

- **Register** with name, email and a password of at least 6 characters. Email isn't verified.
- **Sign in** with email and password. **Forgot password** emails a link that works for 60 minutes (one request
    every 2 minutes).
- **Account pages:** profile, addresses, orders, wishlist, compare, waiting list, reviews, newsletter subscriptions
    and the points history.
- Guests can use the **wishlist** and **compare** (up to 4 products); a guest wishlist is merged into the account on
    sign-in. Buying never requires an account.

<Warning>
    The **waiting list** records interest in out-of-stock products, but no back-in-stock email is sent yet.
</Warning>

## Contacts and newsletter

- The phones, hours, socials, address and map in the **header and footer** come from **Settings → Contacts**.
- The `/contacts` page itself is a static template with sample content —
    edit `src/App/Frontend/views/page/contacts.twig`. Its contact form emails each message to the contact email set
    in **Settings → Contacts**.
- The newsletter sign-up uses double opt-in: subscribers confirm by email before they receive campaigns.
