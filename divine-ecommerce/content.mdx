## Blog

**Information → Articles** — each article has a title, slug, excerpt, content, image, publish date, featured
and active flags, and meta title, description and keywords.

- **Shop the story:** pick products in the article form and they're shown with the article, ready to add to cart.
- The blog lists 12 articles per page at `/blog`; each article lives at `/blog/{slug}`.
- Readers can't comment — the storefront has no comment form.

## Info pages

**Information → Pages** — delivery terms, privacy policy, public offer and the like, at `/pages/{slug}`. Each page
has a type (**page**, **privacy**, **terms** or **offer**), content, position, active flag, meta fields and a
**Show in header** flag.

## Menus

**Tools → Menu Builder** manages the menus for two places in the storefront:

| Location | Shown in | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Header** | The top navigation | One level of sub-items. If no header menu exists, pages with **Show in header** are listed instead. |
| **Footer** | The footer link column | |

The builder also offers a **sidebar** location, but no template displays it yet.

## Contact details

**Settings → Contacts** holds the main phone and email, additional phones, working hours (weekdays and weekend),
address, city, country, social links and a map embed. They appear in the header and footer, and messages sent
through the `/contacts` form are emailed to the main email.

The `/contacts` page is a static template — edit `src/App/Frontend/views/page/contacts.twig` to put your own
stores and details on it.

## Home slider

**Main → Slides** — each slide has a title, subtitle, image, link and button text, an overlay position and
style, a sort position and optional **start/end dates**, so you can schedule seasonal slides ahead of time.

## Newsletter

### Subscribers

Shoppers subscribe from the storefront form or their account. Subscription is **double opt-in**: they get a
confirmation email, and only after confirming do they get a welcome email and campaigns. Every campaign email
carries a personal unsubscribe link.

**Sales & Marketing → Newsletters** lists subscribers (you can delete them) and campaigns.

### Campaigns

<Steps>
    <Step title="Write the campaign">
        A subject and the message body.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Choose a segment">
        Filter confirmed subscribers by signup date range, signup source, customers vs guests, buyers or non-buyers,
        **win-back** (customers who haven't ordered for N days, 90 by default) and minimum amount spent.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Preview the audience">
        **Preview** shows how many subscribers match.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Send or schedule">
        Send now, or pick a time — the background worker checks every minute and sends due campaigns.
    </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
    Scheduled times are compared against the database clock, which runs in **UTC** — convert your local time when
    scheduling.
</Warning>

Emails go out one by one while the request runs. For large lists, prefer **scheduling** over **Send now**: a long
send started from the browser can hit the 30-second PHP time limit and stop part-way, and resending may then
duplicate emails for the subscribers who already received it.
