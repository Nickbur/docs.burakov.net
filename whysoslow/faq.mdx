<AccordionGroup>
    <Accordion title="What does it cost?">
        Nothing — free, with no subscriptions and no in-app purchases.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Which platforms are supported?">
        **iPhone and Android**, in portrait or landscape. There's no web or desktop version — it's built to be
        mounted in a car.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Is it a navigator? Does it do routes?">
        No. WhySoSlow is a **follow-map**, not a navigator — no destinations, no turn-by-turn. It shows the limit
        in force and warns about cameras.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Where does it work?">
        Speed limits and cameras for **Kyiv and Kyiv Oblast** come with the app. For any other region of Ukraine,
        the data downloads the first time you're there online. See [Offline maps](/whysoslow/offline-maps).
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Why does it need me to sign in?">
        So your own fixes and markers sync across your devices, and so the optional AI advisor can work. The
        account is free.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Does it run in the background?">
        No. It's built to run on screen, with the phone mounted, and it keeps the screen on while a drive is
        running. On Android, a quick switch to another app doesn't interrupt the drive (a notification shows
        while it's using your location). Once you stop the drive, it stops using your location.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Does it drain the battery or heat the phone?">
        It's built to run on a charger, with the screen on. To keep the phone cool it reads GPS about once a
        second, and if the AI sign advisor is on, you can take photos less often or at lower quality — see
        [Photo interval and quality](/whysoslow/ai-sign-advisor#photo-interval-and-quality).
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Why does the limit show '—'?">
        You're not on a road WhySoSlow can match — for example in a car park. Where the map data has no posted
        limit, WhySoSlow uses Ukraine's default for that kind of road instead. If a limit is wrong, add a
        [limit fix](/whysoslow/fix-a-limit).
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Is my camera always recording?">
        Only if you switch on the **AI sign advisor**. Then it takes a small photo of the road every few seconds
        (3–5, set in Settings) while the Drive screen is open; photos stay on your phone only until they're
        uploaded. See [AI sign advisor](/whysoslow/ai-sign-advisor).
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="How accurate are the limits and cameras?">
        Cameras come from Ukraine's official camera register, with OpenStreetMap filling the gaps. Limits come
        from OpenStreetMap, with Ukraine's default limits where a road has no number. Good, but not perfect —
        that's why you can fix any limit and add any camera yourself; your edits always win.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Does it use kilometres or miles?">
        Kilometres per hour, always.
    </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
