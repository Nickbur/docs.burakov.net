Map data is never perfect. When the limit shown is wrong, set the right one yourself. Your fix takes priority
over the map data, and syncs to your other devices.

## Add a fix

<Steps>
    <Step title="Pick the stretch">
        - **Where you are:** on the Drive screen, tap **+ Add**. It uses the stretch of road you're on
            (highlighted on the map) and your direction. (No position yet? *Start a drive first to add something
            at your location.*)
        - **Anywhere else:** press and hold a road on the map.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Set limit here">
        Choose **Set limit here**. Set the speed with the **−** / **+** buttons (in steps of 5, from 5 to 130
        km/h, starting at 50) or tap a preset: 20, 30, 50, 60, 80, 90, 110.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Direction">
        With **Both directions** off, the fix applies only to traffic going your way along that stretch. Turn it
        on to apply it in both directions. A fix placed from the map has no direction of travel, so it always
        applies both ways and the switch isn't shown.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Save">
        Tap **Save**. From now on, the roundel shows your limit along that whole stretch. Changed your mind? Tap
        **Undo** right after saving.
    </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
    Your fix is tied to the road itself, so it stays in place when the map data is updated.
</Tip>

To change or remove a fix, open **My data** — see [Manage your data](/whysoslow/manage-your-data).

The [AI sign advisor](/whysoslow/ai-sign-advisor) can also suggest fixes from road signs it reads, for you to
confirm.
