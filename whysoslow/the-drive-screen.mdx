The **Drive** screen is the whole point of WhySoSlow.

## Start and stop a drive

- Tap **Start** to begin a drive, and **Stop** when you're done.
- Or just set off: with the app open, WhySoSlow starts the drive by itself once it senses you're moving.
- While a drive is running, the screen stays on.
- On Android, a quick switch to another app doesn't interrupt the drive — a notification shows while
    WhySoSlow is using your location.
- There's no background tracking: once you stop the drive, WhySoSlow stops using your location.

## Your speed and the limit

The display shows, side by side:

- **Your speed** in km/h, from GPS.
- **The limit in force** as a road-sign roundel, with the **name of the road** in a pill.

The limit comes from the first of these that applies:

1. **A camera's enforced limit**, while you're approaching a camera that publishes one — the roundel then
    shows a small camera badge. If the camera's limit isn't published, the roundel keeps the road's limit and
    still shows the badge.
2. **Your own limit fix** for the stretch of road you're on — see [Fix a speed limit](/whysoslow/fix-a-limit).
3. **The road's posted limit** from OpenStreetMap, for the road you're on (matched within 45 metres).
4. **Ukraine's default limit** for that kind of road, where the map data has no number — for example
    50 km/h in a city or town.
5. Otherwise — when you're not on a mapped road — the roundel shows a dimmed **—**.

## The colours

| Colour | When | Sound |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Green** | At or below the limit | — |
| **Yellow** | Over the limit, but below your warning threshold | — |
| **Red** | At or above the limit **plus** your threshold (+20 km/h by default) — the tile pulses | One low buzz each time you go into red |

The threshold is **Warn above limit by** in Settings — see [Change or mute alerts](/whysoslow/adjust-alerts).

## The number on your speed tile

The small number shows how far you are from the red line:

- **−12** — 12 km/h below the point where it turns red.
- **+3 (23)** — in red: 3 km/h past your threshold, and 23 km/h over the posted limit.

## The map

The map follows you, **north up**, with an arrow for your position pointing the way you're heading. The road
you're on is highlighted, cameras show as red dots, and your own markers — potholes, roadworks and so on —
show with their own icons; temporary ones have an amber outline.

- **Recenter**, **Zoom in** and **Zoom out** are on the side. Moving the map yourself pauses following;
    **Recenter** picks it up again.
- The map is light or dark with your **Appearance** setting (Auto follows your phone). Street names are in
    Ukrainian.
- The **speaker** button turns alert sounds on or off.
