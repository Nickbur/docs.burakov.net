## Your account

Sign in with your **email and a one-time code** — no password. On iPhone you can also use **Sign in with
Apple**. Signing in is required. It's one free account across the apps at [burakov.net](https://burakov.net);
there are no subscriptions and nothing to buy.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **Location — while using the app** | To show your speed and the limit where you are, and to warn you before cameras. There's no background location — once you stop a drive, WhySoSlow stops using it. |
| **Camera** *(optional)* | Only for the [AI sign advisor](/whysoslow/ai-sign-advisor), and only if you turn it on. |

## What's stored where

| On your phone | On the server, with your account |
| --- | --- |
| Your settings | Your **limit fixes** and **markers**, with their locations |
| Your fixes and markers | **AI suggestions** — with the location and direction of each |
| Downloaded maps and road data, and AI advisor photos waiting to upload (deleted once uploaded) | **Photos** in which the advisor recognised a sign, kept until you delete your account |

WhySoSlow doesn't upload your location continuously. The server only receives a location with something you
add, and with each AI advisor photo. Your data isn't end-to-end encrypted, because syncing needs to read it.
It isn't sold or used for ads.

## Other services

- **An AI provider** receives every photo the AI sign advisor sends, to read the sign — only while the
    advisor is on.
- Maps and road data are downloaded from our own server.
- **Crash reports** — while **Settings → Privacy & security → Send crash reports** is on (the default),
    anonymous error reports go to an error-tracking service so bugs can be fixed. Turn it off any time;
    turning it back on takes effect after a restart.

## Lock the app

Turn on **Settings → Privacy & security → Lock with Face ID / fingerprint**. WhySoSlow then asks for your face
or fingerprint when it opens and after 5 minutes in the background (on iPhone your device passcode works
too). The check is done by your phone itself — nothing about your face or fingerprint reaches the app or our
servers.

## Export or delete your data

Both are in your account on burakov.net — **Account → Data & deletion**:

- **Export your data → Download my data** gives you one file with your data from every app, including your
    WhySoSlow fixes, markers and AI suggestions (not the photos).
- **Delete my account** signs you out at once and removes your data — including the advisor's photos — from
    **every** app on the account. You can restore it within **30 days** by signing in again.
