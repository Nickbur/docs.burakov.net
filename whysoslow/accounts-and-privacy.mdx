## One free account

WhySoSlow uses a single **free burakov account** (email, Google, or Apple) that works across all your
apps and devices. **Signing in is required** — it's what lets your personal edits sync between your
phones and powers the optional AI advisor. There are **no subscriptions** and nothing to buy.

## What's stored

- **Your personal layer** — the limits you fix and the markers you drop — is stored on your account
    and **synced** to the burakov server so it's there on every device. It is **not** end-to-end
    encrypted, because syncing needs to read it.
- **AI advisor photos** (only if you turn the advisor on) are **kept on the server** so suggestions
    can be produced; your device **deletes its copies after upload**.
- **The map, limits, and camera data** live **offline on your device** as downloaded packs.

## Permissions it asks for

<Steps>
    <Step title="Location — while in use">
        To show the limit where you are and warn you before cameras. WhySoSlow uses location **only while
        the app is open and on screen** — there is **no background or always-on location**.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Camera — optional">
        Only for the **AI sign advisor**, and only when you enable it. With the advisor off, WhySoSlow
        never uses the camera.
    </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
    WhySoSlow is a **personal project, not a business** — your data isn't sold or used for ads. It's
    built to help you drive, and shared as-is.
</Note>
