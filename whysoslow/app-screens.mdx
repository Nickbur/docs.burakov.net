## Getting around

The bottom bar has **Menu**, **Drive** and **My data** — and, on the Drive screen, **+ Add**. The menu holds
**About**, **Settings** and **Log out**. WhySoSlow works in portrait and landscape; landscape has its own
layout of the display.

## Screens

| Screen | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Drive** | The map and the display — your speed, the limit in force, camera warnings. Tap **Start** (or just drive off) to begin a drive, and **Stop** to end it. See [Read your speed and the limit](/whysoslow/the-drive-screen). |
| **Add** | **Set limit here** or **Add a marker** — see [Fix a speed limit](/whysoslow/fix-a-limit) and [Add a camera or hazard](/whysoslow/your-markers-and-limits). |
| **My data** | Your **AI suggestions**, **Limit edits** and **Markers** — see [Manage your data](/whysoslow/manage-your-data). |
| **Settings** | The options below. |
| **About** | The version and update notice, a link to these docs, the Privacy Policy and Terms. |

## Settings

| Setting | Options | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Warn above limit by** | Every km/h over, +10, +15, +20, +25 km/h | +20 km/h — see [Change or mute alerts](/whysoslow/adjust-alerts) |
| **Alert sound** | On / Off (also the speaker button on Drive) | On |
| **AI sign advisor** | On / Off | Off — see [AI sign advisor](/whysoslow/ai-sign-advisor) |
| **Upload on Wi-Fi only** *(when the advisor is on)* | On / Off | Off |
| **Photo every** *(when the advisor is on)* | 3, 4 or 5 seconds | 4 seconds |
| **Photo quality** *(when the advisor is on)* | Standard, Low — lower keeps the phone cooler | Standard |
| **Upload now** *(when the advisor is on)* | Sends the photos waiting on your phone straight away | — |
| **Offline map** | **Download**, **Update** when a newer map is ready, or **Delete offline map** once installed. Shows the date of the map, speed-limit and camera data. | — see [Offline maps](/whysoslow/offline-maps) |
| **Appearance** | Auto, Light, Dark | Auto (follows your phone) — the map follows it too |
| **Language** | System language, English, Русский, Українська | System language |
| **Lock with Face ID / fingerprint** | On / Off | Off — see [Account & privacy](/whysoslow/accounts-and-privacy#lock-the-app) |
| **Send crash reports** | On / Off — anonymous error reports that help fix bugs; turning them back on takes effect after a restart | On |

## Update prompts

When a new version is out, **About** says *A new version is available*. If your version is too old to keep
working, an **Update required** screen asks you to update first.
