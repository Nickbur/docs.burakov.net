## My speed stays at 0 and the limit shows "—"

WhySoSlow probably doesn't have your location — without it the drive doesn't start, and the button stays on
**Start**. Allow location for WhySoSlow **while using the app** in your phone's settings (on iPhone, also turn
on **Precise Location**), then tap **Start** on the Drive screen.

## The speed or position freezes

The phone has lost GPS — in a tunnel, under a bridge, or among tall buildings. It recovers when the signal
returns.

## No limit is shown ("—")

You're more than about 45 m from a mapped road, so WhySoSlow can't match one. On a mapped road with no posted
limit, it shows Ukraine's default limit for that kind of road instead. If a limit is wrong, add a
[limit fix](/whysoslow/fix-a-limit).

## I didn't get a camera warning

- The camera may only enforce the **other direction**.
- Right after you set off, the phone may not know your direction yet — warnings need it.
- The camera may not be in the data — add it as a [camera marker](/whysoslow/your-markers-and-limits).
- **Alert sound** may be off — the banner still shows.

## No sound

Check **Alert sound** (or the speaker button) and your phone's volume and silent switch.

## The screen turns off while driving

WhySoSlow keeps the screen on only while a drive is running. Check that the button shows **Stop** — tap
**Start** if it doesn't. On Android, a quick switch to another app doesn't stop the drive.

## The map is blank

You haven't downloaded the base map for this region yet — limits and cameras still work. Download it on
Wi-Fi from the prompt on the Drive screen or in Settings. If the download fails, you'll see *Download failed*
— tap **Retry** on a stable connection. See [Offline maps](/whysoslow/offline-maps).

## AI sign advisor

- *Too many requests. Please wait a moment.* — the AI allowance for today is used up. Turn the advisor off
    until tomorrow.
- No suggestions? Check that you allowed camera access and that the rear camera sees the road. With no
    connection — or on mobile data with **Upload on Wi-Fi only** on — photos wait on your phone; tap
    **Upload now** to send them.

## My fixes disappeared after logging out

Logging out removes WhySoSlow's data from the phone immediately, including anything not yet synced. Before
logging out, open the app online so everything syncs.

## Sign-in messages

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| *Incorrect validation code. Please try again.* | Check the latest code, or ask for a new one. |
| *Too many attempts. Please wait N min.* | Wait the time shown. |
| *Unable to connect. Please try again later.* | Check your connection. |
| *Apple sign-in failed. Please try again.* | Try again, or sign in with your email. |

## App lock

*This device has no Face ID or fingerprint set up.* — set one up in your phone's settings first. *Couldn't
confirm it's you — the lock stays off.* — the check failed; try again.
