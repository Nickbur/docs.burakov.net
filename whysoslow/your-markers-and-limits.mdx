Add what the map data misses: a camera that's missing, or a hazard you want to remember.

## Add a marker

Tap **+ Add** on the Drive screen (your current position and direction) or press and hold a spot on the map,
then choose **Add a marker** and its kind:

| Kind | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Camera** | Shows on the map as a red dot and **warns you** like any other camera. Set its **Enforced speed** (50 by default) and whether it covers **Both directions**. |
| **Accident**, **Checkpoint**, **Pothole**, **Roadworks**, **Other** | Show on the Drive map with their own icon, so you see them as you approach. |

Every marker can have a **Note**, and can be marked **Temporary** — temporary markers get an amber outline, on
the map and in the list. Nothing expires on its own: you remove markers yourself. Tap **Save**.

<Note>
    A camera marker without **Both directions** warns you when you drive in the direction you were heading
    when you added it. A camera placed from the map has no direction of travel, so it warns you both ways.
</Note>

Your markers sync to your account, so they're on every device you sign in on. To edit or remove one, see
[Manage your data](/whysoslow/manage-your-data).
