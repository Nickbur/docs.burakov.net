Map data is never perfect, and WhySoSlow is **yours to correct**. Your edits form a **personal
layer** that always takes priority over the underlying map, syncs across your phones, and survives
the next map refresh.

## Fix a wrong limit

If the limit shown is wrong for a stretch of road, override it: **select the stretch, set the
correct speed, confirm**. The controls are large and there's an **undo**, so it's safe to do even
while driving. Your override is stored by road geometry and name, so it stays put when the map data
updates.

## Drop your own markers

Add markers for what the data misses:

- **Cameras** — with a **bearing**, so they trigger a directional warning like the official ones.
  Add the enforced limit if you know it.
- **Hazards** — accidents, checkpoints, potholes, roadworks, or anything else.

**Permanent vs temporary** markers are shown with a different **outline colour**. There's no expiry
— you manage them yourself, so a temporary hazard stays until you clear it.

## Three ways to add something

<Steps>
  <Step title="On the map">
    While parked, tap a spot on the map to add a limit override or a marker there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="In motion">
    Add a marker or fix the current road's limit with a single large tap as you drive — confirm and
    undo keep it safe.
  </Step>
  <Step title="From an AI suggestion">
    Confirm a pre-filled suggestion from the [AI sign advisor](/whysoslow/ai-sign-advisor) — it lands
    in the same confirm flow as everything else.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Everything you add or edit lives on one **"My markers & limits"** screen, where you can review and
remove it. It all syncs to your account, so it's there on every device you sign in on.
