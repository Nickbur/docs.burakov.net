The **AI sign advisor** is an optional helper that reads roadside speed-limit signs through your phone's
rear camera and **suggests** limit fixes. It never changes anything on its own — you confirm each suggestion.

## Turn it on

Turn on **Settings → AI sign advisor** (off by default). The next time the Drive screen is open, your phone
asks for camera access. Mount the phone so the rear camera sees the road.

## How it works

<Steps>
    <Step title="It looks at the road">
        While the Drive screen is open, the app takes a small, low-resolution photo **every few seconds** (3–5,
        set in Settings) and tags it with your location and direction. Photos upload in batches to an AI that
        looks for a speed-limit sign facing your direction. It ignores minimum-speed, advisory and end-of-limit
        signs.
    </Step>
    <Step title="It suggests a limit">
        When it reads a sign, a suggestion appears in **My data → AI suggestions** as *Detected limit …* with the
        spot.
    </Step>
    <Step title="You decide">
        **Confirm** opens it as a pre-filled limit fix — check it and save, and it becomes a normal
        [limit fix](/whysoslow/fix-a-limit). **Dismiss** discards it.
    </Step>
</Steps>

## Photo interval and quality

Taking photos and uploading them warms the phone up. Two settings, shown while the advisor is on, let you
balance how much the advisor sees against how hot the phone gets:

- **Photo every** — 3, 4 or 5 seconds (4 by default). A longer interval keeps the phone cooler.
- **Photo quality** — **Standard** (default) or **Low**. Low keeps the phone cooler and uploads less data.

## Uploads and data

- Photos stay on your phone only until they're uploaded, then they're deleted from it. With no connection,
    they wait and upload when you're back online.
- **Upload on Wi-Fi only** keeps photos on your phone until you're on Wi-Fi, on iPhone and Android alike.
- **Upload now** sends the photos waiting on your phone straight away.
- Every photo goes to an AI provider to be read. Only photos in which a sign **was recognised** are kept on
    our server, with their location, until you delete your account.
- The AI has a daily allowance per account. When it's used up, you'll see *Too many requests. Please wait a
    moment.* — turn the advisor off for the rest of the day.

<Warning>
    The advisor takes a photo of the road ahead every few seconds while it's on and the Drive screen is open,
    and uploads them. Turn it off in Settings whenever you don't need it.
</Warning>
