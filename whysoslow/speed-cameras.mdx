WhySoSlow warns you before **automatic speed cameras**, so they never take you by surprise.

## When you're warned

A camera counts when it's **ahead of you** (within about 75° of your direction of travel) and within 1 km.
The warning comes when the camera is about **30 seconds** away at your current speed, or **300 metres**,
whichever is first — so roughly 30 seconds between about 36 and 120 km/h, and at about 300 m when you're
slow.

## Direction

If a camera has a known enforcement direction, you're warned only when you drive **into** it (within about
55° of that direction) — passing it the other way stays silent. A camera with no known direction warns you
**both ways**.

<Note>
    Warnings need your **direction of travel**. Right after you set off — before the phone knows which way
    you're heading — no camera warning can fire.
</Note>

## What you see and hear

- A banner: *Speed camera ahead · Limit … · distance*.
- The limit roundel switches to the **camera's enforced limit**, with a camera badge. If the camera's limit
    isn't published, the roundel keeps the road's limit and still shows the badge.
- A short rising **two-note chirp** — once per camera, and different from the overspeed buzz.
- If you're already past the camera's red line (its limit plus your threshold) as you approach, the warning
    is stronger and sounds different — slow down now.

## Where the camera data comes from

- Cameras come from Ukraine's **official register of automatic enforcement cameras** (published on
    data.gov.ua), which records each camera's direction and, where published, its enforced limit.
    **OpenStreetMap** fills in any cameras the register misses. Kyiv and Kyiv Oblast cameras come with the
    app; other regions' cameras download with their region data.
- **Your own camera markers** warn you just like these — see
    [Add a camera or hazard](/whysoslow/your-markers-and-limits).

The camera list updates itself: when you open WhySoSlow with a connection, it checks for changes, at most
once a day. Add any camera you know is missing.
