## Warn above limit by

**Settings → Warn above limit by** decides when your speed turns **red** and buzzes:

| Option | Turns red at |
| --- | --- |
| **Every km/h over** | Any speed above the limit |
| **+10 km/h** | Limit + 10 |
| **+15 km/h** | Limit + 15 |
| **+20 km/h** *(default)* | Limit + 20 — Ukraine's fineable line |
| **+25 km/h** | Limit + 25 |

Between the limit and that point your speed shows **yellow**, silently. See
[Read your speed and the limit](/whysoslow/the-drive-screen#the-colours).

## Alert sound

**Settings → Alert sound** — or the **speaker** button on the Drive screen, which is the same switch — turns
all sounds on or off: the overspeed buzz and the camera warnings. Visual warnings stay either way.

<Tip>
    If you hear nothing with sound on, check the phone's silent switch and volume.
</Tip>
