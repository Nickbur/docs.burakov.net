WhySoSlow keeps two kinds of data on your phone so it works with no signal.

## Road and camera data

The speed limits and cameras for **Kyiv and Kyiv Oblast** come **with the app** — they work offline from the
start. For every other region of Ukraine, WhySoSlow downloads the data automatically the first time you're
there with a connection, and keeps it.

Camera data refreshes on its own: WhySoSlow checks for changes when you open it online, at most once a day.
Speed-limit data is updated a few times a year, and WhySoSlow offers the update when a newer version is
ready. Your own fixes and markers always take priority and stay in place through updates — see
[Fix a speed limit](/whysoslow/fix-a-limit).

## The base map

The map itself — streets, labels, landscape — is a separate download, about **100 MB per oblast**. Without
it, the Drive screen still shows your speed, limits and cameras, over a plain background.

WhySoSlow picks your **region automatically** from your location, and switches as you cross into another
oblast.

- **Download** — on the Drive screen, a prompt shows your current region, its size and *works offline*; tap
    **Download** and watch the percentage. You can also download it in **Settings**. Use Wi-Fi. If the
    download fails, the prompt shows *Download failed* with **Retry**.
- **Delete** — in **Settings**, **Delete offline map** removes the current region's map; other regions
    you've downloaded are listed there with **Delete** too.
- **Update** — when a newer map is ready for a region you've downloaded (about every three months),
    WhySoSlow offers the update. Download it on Wi-Fi.

<Note>
    You can only download the map for the region you're **in** right now. To prepare for a trip, open
    WhySoSlow once you've arrived in the new region while you still have a connection.
</Note>

## Data dates

**Settings → Offline map** shows how current each kind of data is — *data as of* a date for:

| Data | How it stays current |
| --- | --- |
| **Base map** | A newer map is offered about every three months |
| **Speed limits** | Updated a few times a year; WhySoSlow offers the update |
| **Cameras** | Checked automatically, at most once a day, whenever you open the app online |
