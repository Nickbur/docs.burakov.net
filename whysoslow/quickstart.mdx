title: "Quickstart"
description: "Install WhySoSlow, sign in, download the Kyiv map, and take your first drive."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the app">
    Get WhySoSlow from the [App Store](https://burakov.net/projects/whysoslow) or
    [Google Play](https://burakov.net/projects/whysoslow). It runs on **iOS and Android** — phones
    and tablets.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Create or sign in with a **free burakov account** (email, Google, or Apple). One account works
    across all your devices. Signing in lets your personal edits sync and enables the optional AI
    advisor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download the map">
    Open the **Drive** screen and download the **Kyiv & oblast** offline map (about 112 MB — do it
    on Wi-Fi). After that, the map, the speed limits, and the cameras all work with **no signal**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mount and go">
    Put your phone in a car mount, ideally on a charger, with the screen on. Tap **Start**: the map
    follows you and the heads-up display shows your speed next to the limit in force.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  WhySoSlow assumes the phone is **mounted, charging, and screen-on** while you drive — so it's
  tuned to stay cool and legible, not to run in your pocket in the background.
</Tip>

## What you need

- A phone or tablet on **iOS or Android**.
- A **free burakov account**.
- A one-time **map download** for your region (Wi-Fi recommended).
- A **car mount** — WhySoSlow is a glance-and-drive display, not something to hold.
