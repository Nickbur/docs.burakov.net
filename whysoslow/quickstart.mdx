---

<Steps>
    <Step title="Install the app">
        Get WhySoSlow for **iPhone or Android** from the [project page](https://burakov.net/projects/whysoslow).
    </Step>
    <Step title="Sign in">
        Enter your email and type the code you receive — no password. On iPhone you can also use **Sign in with
        Apple**. Signing in is required; it's free, and it lets your own edits sync between devices.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Allow location">
        WhySoSlow asks for your location **while you use the app** — it needs it to show your speed and the
        limit. It doesn't track you in the background.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Download the base map">
        On the **Drive** screen, tap **Download** on the prompt for your region (about 100 MB — use Wi-Fi). The
        speed limits and cameras for Kyiv and Kyiv Oblast already work without it; the download adds the map
        itself.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Mount and drive">
        Put the phone in a car mount, ideally on a charger. On the **Drive** screen, tap **Start** — or just set
        off: with the app open, WhySoSlow starts the drive by itself once it senses you're moving. Your speed
        appears next to the limit in force.
    </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
    While a drive is running, WhySoSlow keeps your screen on. On Android, a quick switch to another app doesn't
    interrupt the drive — a notification shows while it's using your location. Tap **Stop** when you arrive.
</Note>
