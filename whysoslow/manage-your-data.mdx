## My data

**My data** lists everything you've added, in three sections:

- **AI suggestions** — limits read by the [AI sign advisor](/whysoslow/ai-sign-advisor), waiting for you to
    **Confirm** or **Dismiss**.
- **Limit edits** — your [limit fixes](/whysoslow/fix-a-limit).
- **Markers** — your [cameras and other markers](/whysoslow/your-markers-and-limits).

Tap an item to edit it — the limit, kind, note or **Temporary** — or use its trash button to delete it.
Deleted something by mistake? Tap **Undo** right away.

## Sync

Your fixes and markers are saved on your phone first, so adding them works offline. When you're signed in
and online, WhySoSlow syncs them to your account — shortly after each change, when you come back to the
app, and when the connection returns — so they appear on every device you use. If the same item changed on
two devices, the latest change wins.

## Logging out

**Log out** removes WhySoSlow's data from this phone straight away, without asking — including fixes and
markers that haven't synced yet. Open the app online for a moment before logging out so everything syncs.
