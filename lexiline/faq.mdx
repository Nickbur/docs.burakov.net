<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I followed a topic but nothing shows up. Is it broken?">
    No — that's expected. Lexiline only shows an item when something genuinely matches your topic,
    so a new or quiet topic can sit empty for a while. It's waiting, not stuck. The moment
    relevant content appears, it lands in your timeline.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How is this different from a search alert or a feed reader?">
    A search alert matches keywords and floods you with near-misses; a feed reader needs a source
    that's already publishing. Lexiline lets you follow a subject before it has any coverage,
    reads for meaning instead of just words, and learns from your feedback — so what arrives is
    what you actually meant.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does it cost?">
    Lexiline is free to start: follow **2 topics** with a daily digest, on any device. **Pro** —
    **$49.99/year or $5.99/month** — raises that to **20 topics**, checks the web more often, adds
    instant alerts, and writes you AI digests. There's no lifetime plan, because a watcher only
    earns its keep by running.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How quickly will I hear about a match?">
    On the free plan, matches arrive in your daily digest. On Pro, a topic can be set to **instant
    alerts** — a push the moment something appears — and checks run more often. Either way, the
    timeline always has everything new since your last visit.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which platforms are supported?">
    iOS, Android, and the web — all from one account, with your topics synced between them.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change or stop following a topic?">
    Anytime. Edit a topic to refine it, or delete it to stop the watching and clear its items from
    your timeline.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
