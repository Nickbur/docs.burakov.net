<AccordionGroup>
    <Accordion title="I followed a topic but nothing shows up. Is it broken?">
        No — Lexiline only adds what genuinely matches, so a new or quiet topic can sit empty for a while.
        If it stays empty for days, shorten the **title** (it's the phrase Lexiline searches for) and lower
        the strictness a little. See [Sharpen a topic](/lexiline/tuning-topics).
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="How quickly will I hear about a match?">
        On Free, each topic is checked once a day, and new matches reach you in your feed and your daily
        digest. On Pro, checks adapt to how much is happening — from about once an hour for a busy topic to
        once a day for a quiet one — and topics with **Notify me on every match** on are checked close to
        real time, with a push for every new match. See [How watching works](/lexiline/how-it-works).
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Why does an old article say it's new?">
        The time on a match is when Lexiline found it, not when it was published.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="What does it cost?">
        Free covers **2 topics**, checked once a day, with the feed and a daily digest. **Pro** —
        **$5.99/month or $49.99/year**, with a 7-day free trial — raises that to **20 topics** and adds
        faster checks, instant alerts, the Week and Month digests with an AI recap, and your full match
        history. See [Pricing](/lexiline/pricing).
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Which platforms are supported?">
        iPhone, Android and the web — all from one account, with your topics synced between them.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Which languages does it search in?">
        Topics are searched in English. The app itself is available in English and Russian.
    </Accordion>
    <Accordion title="Can I change or stop following a topic?">
        Anytime. Tap a topic to edit it, or swipe it left to delete it — that also removes its matches.
    </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
