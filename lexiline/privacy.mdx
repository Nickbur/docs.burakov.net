## Sign in

You sign in with your **email and a one-time code** — no password. You can also use **Sign in with
Google**, or **Sign in with Apple** on iPhone. After five wrong codes, the app makes you wait five minutes. It's one account across the
apps at [burakov.net](https://burakov.net), and your topics follow you to every device.

## What Lexiline stores

To keep watching while the app is closed, Lexiline keeps on its server, tied to your account:

- your **topics** — every field;
- the **matches** found for them (headline, snippet, link, website, score and reason) — including ones
    you hid, which help tune the topic. On Free you see the last 30 days of them; on Pro, all of them;
- your **feedback** (swipes) and your **Recaps**.

On your device it keeps a copy of your feed and topics so you can read them offline. Logging out removes
it from that device.

## Which services see what

| Service | What it receives |
| --- | --- |
| **A search provider** | Only your topic's **title**, to search for it — never who you are. |
| **An AI provider** | Your topic's fields, the candidate headlines and snippets it's judging, a few headlines you rated, and the text you give **Suggest with AI** — without your name or account. Requests are sent with data collection for training turned off. |
| **A push notification service** | An account identifier and the notification text, which can include topic titles. |
| **A crash-reporting service** | Error details with an account identifier (not your name or email), your time zone, and occasionally a recording of the app screen with all text masked — only if **Send crash reports** is on. Turn it off in Settings. |
| **Google or Apple** | Only if you sign in with them — the sign-in itself. |
| **The App Store, Google Play or the web payment provider** | Your purchase. Lexiline never sees your card details. |

There's no advertising profile and no engagement scoring.

## Lock the app

On iPhone and Android you can turn on **Settings → Privacy & security → Lock with Face ID /
fingerprint**. Lexiline then asks for your face or fingerprint when it opens and after 5 minutes in the
background (on iPhone your device passcode works too). The check is done by your phone itself — nothing
about your face or fingerprint ever reaches the app or our servers.

## Export or delete your data

Both are in the app — **Account → Account settings** — and in your account on burakov.net —
**Account → Data & deletion**:

- **Export my data** in the app gives you one file with your account and your Lexiline topics,
    matches, recaps and feedback. On burakov.net, **Export your data → Download my data** gives you one
    file with your data from every app.
- **Delete account** (in the app) or **Delete my account** (on burakov.net) signs you out at once and
    removes your data from **every** app on the account. You can restore it within **30 days** by
    signing in again and choosing **Restore my account**.

Your sessions on other devices are in the app under **Account → Active sessions**, and on burakov.net
under **Account → Security**.

<Warning>
    Deleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription bought through the App Store or Google Play.
    Cancel it there first.
</Warning>
