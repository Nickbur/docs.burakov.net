Lexiline is a calm tool, and that extends to how little it asks of you. Here's what it keeps and
why.

## What Lexiline stores

To watch a topic even when your app is closed, Lexiline keeps your **topics** and their matched
**timeline items** on the server, tied to your account. That's what lets a topic keep being
watched across your devices and while your phone is asleep. Your relevance feedback (the
thumbs-up / thumbs-down) is stored too, so your timeline stays tuned to what you meant.

## One account, no separate sign-up

Lexiline uses your single account across the Burakov apps — sign in once with an email code, no
password to manage. The same account works on iOS, Android and the web, and your topics follow
you between them.

## What it needs, and what it doesn't

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Notifications" icon="bell">
    Used only to deliver your digest and any instant alerts. Decline them and the app still works
    — you just read the timeline when you open it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Payments" icon="credit-card">
    Pro is billed through the App Store, Google Play, or the web billing provider. Lexiline never
    sees or stores your card details.
  </Card>
  <Card title="AI matching" icon="wand-magic-sparkles">
    Reading candidates for meaning and writing digests happens on the server. It works on the
    public content it's checking against your topics — not on anything private about you.
  </Card>
  <Card title="No tracking feed" icon="shield-check">
    There's no advertising profile and no engagement scoring. Lexiline shows your matches in time
    order — that's all it's built to do.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  You stay in control: edit or delete any topic at any time, and deleting a topic clears its
  items from your timeline.
</Note>
