No topic is perfect on the first day, because what you meant lives in your head. Four controls get it
there.

## Match strictness

Every result gets a match score from the AI. Only results at or above the topic's **Match strictness**
reach your feed.

- **Higher** — fewer, closer matches.
- **Lower** — more matches, including looser ones.

The default is **50%**. Below about **40%** it makes no further difference: weaker results aren't kept
at all.

## Include and exclude

Add the angles you care about to **Include**, and the ones you don't to **Exclude** — for example
exclude "trailer" and "merch" when you only want the announcement. They steer the AI's judgement rather
than acting as strict word filters.

## Teach it with a swipe

In the **Feed**, swipe a match:

| Swipe | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Right** (thumbs-up) | "More like this." The topic's strictness goes **down by 5%** (not below 20%), and the match stays. |
| **Left → Not relevant** | "Not what I meant." Strictness goes **up by 5%** (not above 90%), and the match is hidden. |
| **Left → Mute source** | Blocks that **website** for this topic from now on, and hides the match. |

You'll see *Thanks — Lexiline will learn from that.* The AI also looks at the last few matches you
liked and rejected when it judges new ones.

## Un-mute a site

Muting adds the site to the topic's **Exclude** list as `source:example.com`. To un-mute it, edit the
topic and remove that keyword.

<Tip>
    If a topic stays empty for days, make the **title** shorter and closer to how a headline would put it
    — it's the phrase Lexiline actually searches for — and lower the strictness a little.
</Tip>
