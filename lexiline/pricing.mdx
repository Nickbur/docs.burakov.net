## Plans

| Plan | Price | What you get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Free** | Free | Up to **2 topics**, checked **once a day**, with a daily digest |
| **Pro** | $5.99 / month or $49.99 / year | Up to **20 topics**, **faster checks**, **instant alerts**, the **Week** and **Month** digests with an AI **Recap**, and your **full match history** |

The app shows the exact price before you pay; it can differ by country and store. Pro starts with a
**7-day free trial**. There's no lifetime plan.

### What each plan includes

| | Free | Pro |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Topics** | 2 | 20 |
| **How often topics are checked** | Once a day | Adapts to each topic — more often when it's busy; topics with alerts on are checked close to real time |
| **A new topic's first search looks back** | 7 days | Up to 90 days |
| **Digest** | Day, plus a daily digest notification | Day, Week and Month, with an AI **Recap** |
| **Instant alerts** | — | ✓ |
| **Match history** | Last 30 days | Everything, for as long as you follow the topic |
| **Feed, AI suggestions, teaching by swipe** | ✓ | ✓ |

## Buy, restore and manage

- **Buy** from **Settings → Subscription** (*Go Pro*), or when you reach a Pro feature. On iPhone and
    Android you pay through the App Store or Google Play; on the web, through the web checkout.
- **Pro follows your account** to every device you sign in on.
- **Restore purchases** on the subscription screen brings back a purchase made with the same store
    account.
- **Manage or cancel** from the subscription screen (**Manage in the App Store** / **Google Play**).
    For a subscription bought on the web, use your account on burakov.net — **Account → Subscriptions**,
    where you can cancel it, resume it before it ends, or change plan.
- If a payment fails, the app shows a payment-problem notice so you can fix it.

## When Pro ends

Your topics keep running on the free schedule — checked once a day — and you can still edit them.
Instant alerts stop, and new matches reach you through the daily digest instead. You can't add new
topics while you have 2 or more, the **Week** and **Month** digests go back to Pro-only, and matches
older than 30 days are no longer shown.
