## A new topic found nothing

The first search didn't find anything above the topic's strictness. Make the **title** a short phrase a
headline would use, lower **Match strictness** a little (it has no effect below about 40%), and give it
time — on Free, topics are checked once a day, and on Pro a quiet topic settles at about once a day.

## "Couldn't save — try again."

Check your connection and that the fields aren't too long, then try again. Saving a **new** topic also
runs its first search, which can take up to half a minute.

## Old matches disappeared from the feed

On Free, the feed shows the last 30 days of matches; older matches are no longer shown. Pro keeps your
full match history.

## I muted a site by mistake

Edit the topic and remove its `source:` keyword under **Exclude**.

## I'm not getting alerts

- Instant alerts are part of Pro — check **Settings → Subscription**.
- Check that **Notify me on every match** is on for that topic (look for the lightning icon).
- Allow notifications for Lexiline in your phone's or browser's settings, then use **Settings →
    Notifications → Send test notification**. *Nothing was delivered* means notifications are off for
    this device.
- Matches from a brand-new topic's first search don't send alerts.
- Logged out on that device? Alerts stop until you sign in again.

## "Suggest with AI" stopped working

AI requests share a daily allowance per account across burakov apps. If you've reached it, fill the
fields in yourself or try again tomorrow. *Couldn't reach the AI — fill it in manually.* means the AI
service didn't answer.

## Edits don't save offline

Without a connection you can read your last feed and topics, but not change them. Swipes need a
connection too.

## Sign-in messages

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| *Incorrect validation code. Please try again.* | Check the latest code in your inbox, or ask for a new one. |
| *Too many attempts. Please wait N min.* | Wait the time shown. |
| *Unable to connect. Please try again later.* | Check your internet connection. |

## Purchases

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| *No previous purchases found.* | Make sure you're signed in to the same App Store / Google Play account you bought with. |
| *No plans are available right now.* | Try again later. |

## Still stuck?

Write to support from your account on burakov.net — **Account → Support**.
