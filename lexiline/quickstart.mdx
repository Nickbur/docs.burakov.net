title: "Quickstart"
description: "Sign in to Lexiline, follow your first topic with the help of AI, and read your first matches."
mode: "wide"
---

Lexiline runs on **iPhone, Android and the web**. Get the app from the
[project page](https://burakov.net/projects/lexiline), or open
[lexiline.burakov.net](https://lexiline.burakov.net) in a browser.

<Steps>
    <Step title="Sign in">
        Enter your email and type the code you receive — no password. If you don't have an account yet,
        this creates one. You can also use **Sign in with Google**, or **Sign in with Apple** on iPhone.
        The first time, you pick a username and Lexiline asks whether it may send notifications.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Add a topic">
        Open **Topics** and tap **+** (or **Add a topic**). In **What do you want to follow?** describe it
        in a sentence, then tap **Suggest with AI** — Lexiline fills in the title, what to watch for, and
        keywords to include and exclude. Adjust anything you like.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Start watching">
        Tap **Start watching**. Lexiline runs a first search straight away (*Finding matches…*) and tells
        you how many it found. This can take up to half a minute.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Read your feed">
        Open **Feed** — your matches across all topics, newest first. Tap one to read it at the source.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Teach it">
        Swipe a match right if it's what you wanted, or left for **Not relevant** or **Mute source**. See
        [Sharpen a topic](/lexiline/tuning-topics).
    </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
    Lexiline sends you a daily digest of new matches. On Pro, want a push the moment something new
    matches? Edit the topic and turn on **Notify me on every match**. See
    [Digests & alerts](/lexiline/digests-and-alerts).
</Tip>
