## Getting around

The bottom bar has four tabs:

| Tab | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Menu** | Your profile, **Feed**, **About**, **Settings** and **Log out**. A dot means an app update is available. |
| **Feed** | Every match across your topics, newest first — see [Read your feed](/lexiline/reading-your-feed). |
| **Topics** | The topics you follow — add, edit and delete them. |
| **Digest** | Matches grouped by topic for the last day, or the last week or month on Pro — see [Digests & alerts](/lexiline/digests-and-alerts). |

## Screens

| Screen | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Sign in** | Your email and a one-time code, **Sign in with Google**, or **Sign in with Apple** on iPhone. |
| **Welcome** *(first sign-in)* | A short introduction, permission for notifications, and choosing your username. |
| **Topics** | Each topic with *Watching · last match …*; a lightning icon marks topics with alerts on. Tap to edit, swipe left to delete. |
| **New topic / Edit topic** | The topic's fields, **Suggest with AI**, strictness and alerts — see [Follow a topic](/lexiline/following-topics). |
| **Subscription** | **Go Pro** plans (Pro starts with a 7-day free trial) and **Restore purchases**, or your current subscription. |
| **About** | The version and update notice, a link to these docs, the Privacy Policy and Terms. |

## Settings

| Setting | Options | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Appearance** | Auto, Light, Dark | Auto (follows your device) |
| **Language** | System language, English, Русский | System language — saved to your account |
| **Notifications → Send test notification** | — | Checks that push notifications reach this device. Turn notifications on or off in your device's settings. |
| **Subscription → Manage subscription** | — | — |
| **Lock with Face ID / fingerprint** *(iPhone and Android)* | On / Off | Off — see [Account & privacy](/lexiline/privacy#lock-the-app) |
| **Send crash reports** | On / Off | On — turning it back on takes effect after a restart |

## Update prompts

When a new version is out, **About** says *A new version is available* (with an **Update** button in
the phone apps). If your version is too old to keep working, an **Update required** screen asks you to
update first.
