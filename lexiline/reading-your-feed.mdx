The **Feed** tab collects matches from all your topics in one list, **newest first** — by when Lexiline
found them.

## What each match shows

- The **headline**, and a **match score** badge — green from 80%, amber from 60%, grey below.
- The **website** it's from, and the **topic** it matched.
- **When Lexiline found it** — not when it was published, so an older article found today shows as
    found today.
- A one-line **reason** the AI gives for why it matches.

Tap a match to open the full story at its source.

## Act on a match

Swipe right for "more like this", or left for **Not relevant** or **Mute source** — see
[Sharpen a topic](/lexiline/tuning-topics).

## Refresh

Pull down, or tap the refresh button, to load the latest matches. The feed doesn't refresh on its own
while it's open.

## How much it keeps

On Free, the feed shows the last **30 days** of matches; older matches are no longer shown. On Pro, it
keeps your **full history** for as long as you follow the topic. For a look back grouped by topic, use
the [Digest](/lexiline/digests-and-alerts) tab.

## Offline

The last feed and topics list you loaded stay readable without a connection. Swipes and edits need one.

<Note>
    With no topics yet, the feed says *Nothing watched yet*. With topics but no matches, it says *No
    matches yet* — Lexiline is still watching. A new or quiet topic can stay empty for a while; that's
    expected.
</Note>
