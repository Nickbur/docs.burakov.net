A **topic** is a subject you've asked Lexiline to watch. Add one from **Topics** with **+** (or **Add
a topic**).

## Let AI fill it in

New topics start with **What do you want to follow?** — describe it in a sentence or two (up to 500
characters), then tap **Suggest with AI**. Lexiline proposes a short title, what to watch for, and
keywords to include and exclude. **It replaces anything you've already typed** in those fields, so use
it first and adjust afterwards. It's free on every plan.

## The fields

| Field | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Title** *(required, up to 300 characters)* | **The phrase Lexiline searches for.** Keep it to the few words a news headline about it would contain — "dune part three", not a whole sentence. |
| **What to watch for** *(required, up to 2,000 characters)* | What kind of news you're waiting for, in plain language. The AI uses it to judge each result — it isn't searched for. |
| **Include** / **Exclude** | Up to 30 keywords each (up to 120 characters). Signals for the AI: results about the included ideas score higher, excluded ones lower. They aren't hard filters — except a muted site, written as `source:example.com`, which is blocked completely. |
| **Notify me on every match** *(Pro)* | Checks the topic close to real time and sends a push whenever new matches arrive — see [Digests & alerts](/lexiline/digests-and-alerts). Off by default. |
| **Match strictness** | How closely a result must match to reach your feed, from 0 to 100% in 5% steps; 50% by default. See [Sharpen a topic](/lexiline/tuning-topics). |

Tap **Start watching** (or **Save changes** when editing). For a new topic, Lexiline runs a first search
right away — *Finding matches…* — and then shows *Found N matches*. That first search looks at up to 20
results from the last 7 days on Free, or from up to the last 90 days on Pro, and can take up to half a
minute.

## Edit or delete a topic

Tap a topic in the **Topics** list to edit it. To delete one, swipe it left, tap **Delete** and confirm
*Delete this topic?* — this also removes its matches and your feedback on them.

## How many topics

| Plan | Topics |
| --- | --- |
| **Free** | 2 |
| **Pro** | 20 |

On the free plan, adding a third topic shows *This is a Pro feature* with **See plans**. See
[Pricing](/lexiline/pricing).

<Note>
    **Suggest with AI** shares a daily allowance of AI requests per account across burakov apps. If you
    reach it, fill the fields in yourself or try again tomorrow.
</Note>
