There are two ways to keep up besides scrolling the feed: the **Digest**, which Lexiline also sends you
once a day, or — on Pro — **alerts** for the topics that can't wait.

## The Digest

The **Digest** tab groups recent matches **by topic**, with a count for each. Choose the period:

| Period | Covers | Plan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Day** | The last 24 hours | Every plan |
| **Week** | The last 7 days | Pro |
| **Month** | The last 30 days | Pro |

Each topic shows up to 20 of its matches. Matches you marked **Not relevant** or muted are left out.

### Daily digest

Once a day, when your topics have new matches, Lexiline sends you one notification about them. Tapping
it opens the **Day** digest. There's no email digest.

### Recap

On **Week** and **Month** (Pro), Lexiline writes a short **Recap** — two to five sentences on what
happened across your topics. It's refreshed at most every 12 hours for a week and every 24 hours for a
month, so the newest matches may not be in it yet.

## Instant alerts (Pro)

On Pro, turn on **Notify me on every match** in a topic (a lightning icon then marks it in the Topics
list). Lexiline then checks that topic close to real time, and whenever a check finds new matches you
get one push notification — *New match for "…"* or *N new matches for "…"*. Tapping it opens the app.
You're never alerted twice about the same match, and the first search of a brand-new topic doesn't send
one. On the free plan, the switch shows *This is a Pro feature*.

Alerts work on iPhone, Android and the web (where your browser allows notifications).

## Notifications

Lexiline asks for notification permission the first time you sign in. To check they arrive, use
**Settings → Notifications → Send test notification**. To turn them off or on, use your phone's or
browser's settings. Logging out stops notifications to that device.
